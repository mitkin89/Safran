Jerome: _"For the one goes out_ (or _went out_) _of prison to reign; but the other, though born a king, was oppressed by poverty."_ Syro-hex. gives: ܣ. ܗܘ ܓܝܪ ܡܢ ܒܝܬ ܐܣܝܪܐ[?] ܢܦܩ ܠܡܡܠܟܘ. ܗܘ ܕܝܢ ܟܕ ܡܠܟܐ ܐܬܝܠܕ[?] ܗܘܐ ܨܪܝܟܐ[?]܀ See our remarks in Hex. on Job. xxxvi. 33. At the end Middeld. renders ܨܪܝܟܐ[?] ܗܘܐ as ἐστὶν ἐνδεής, noting: "The Syr. ܨܪܝܟܐ[?] [thus in Norberg's copy] I should think must be changed to ܨܪܝܟܐ[?], which stands for the Gr. ἐνδεής at Job. xxx. 4. Prov. xi. 16. xxi. 17. This fragment of Sym. is cited in Montef. and in the margin of the Holm. Cod. But there ἠπορήθη stands for ἐστὶν ἐνδεής. But to the Greek ἀπορέομαι corresponds ܦܣܩ[?] Jesai. viii. 22. Jerem. viii. 18." The Syr. ܦܣܩ[?] corresponds to τῷ ἀπορεῖσθαι in the sense of _being in doubt_, not, as here, of _being in want_. Further, ܨܪܝܟܐ[?] is the passive participle of the unused verb ܨܪܟ[?], in Ethpeel ܐܬܨܪܟ[?], which we hesitantly rendered ἠπορήθη in the Hex. on Psal. xxxiii. 11. Here, moreover, the Syriac translator himself has written ΗΠΟΡΗΘΗ beside ܨܪܝܟܐ[?].
