[47] "Here two versions are presented joined together: the first, which is that τῶν Ο΄, ὅτι πλειστάκις πονηρεύσεταί σε [σὲ om. Syro-hex.] καρδία σου; the other, ὅτι καὶ καθόδους πολλὰς κακώσει καρδίαν σου. This latter we think to be Aquila's; for the one brought forward from the Roman Ed. is altogether suspect and does not savour of Aquila. This one, on the contrary, is composed according to Aquila's manner, since he constantly renders פַּעַם by κάθοδος; and besides, much from Aquila has been brought into the text of Ecclesiastes τῶν Ο΄."—_Montef._ After πλειστάκις, Codd. 23, 253, 254 insert καιροῦ.
