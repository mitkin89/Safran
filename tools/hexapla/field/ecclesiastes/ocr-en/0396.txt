CAP. I.
[1] Codd. 161, 248. Knobel, in _Comment. ad Eccles._ p. 3, by I know not what error asserts that קֹהֶלֶת is rendered by Aquila συναθροιστὴν.
[2] Jerome: “In the Hebrew, for _vanity of vanities_, ABAL ABALIM is written, which, except for the LXX translators, all have rendered alike, ἀτμὸς ἀτμίδων, or ἀτμῶν.” Nobil. gives: Scholion. Οἱ λοιποί· ἀτμὸς ἀτμῶν. Montef. distinguished: Ἀ. ἀτμὶς ἀτμίδων. Σ. ἀτμὸς ἀτμῶν; which cannot be proved. Finally Syro-hex. notes on ματαιότης: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. ἀτμὸς, or ἀτμίς (ܠܗܓܐ); and on ματαιοτήτων: Σ. ἀτμοῦ, or ἀτμῖδος (ܕܠܗܓܐ).

[4] Nobil.
[5] The same.

[7] Syro-hex. reads in the text καὶ ἀνέτειλε (ܘܕܢܚ); in the margin, however: ܐ. ܘܕܢܚ܀ ܣ. ܘܕܢܚ[?]܀
[8] Jerome: “But for what we, following the Vulgate ed., have put, _leads to its place_, the Hebrew has SOEPH, which Aq. renders εἰσπνεῖ, that is, _breathes in;_ but Sym. and Theod., _runs back._” Nobil. gives: Ἀ. εἰσπνεῖ, not from the printed Jerome; for before Martianay, against the authority of all the MSS., προσέπνευσε was read. Symmachus' Greek reading ἐπαναστρέφει we fear may be conjectural; certainly we do not know its source.
[9] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܡ ܡܟܪܟܢ[?] ܒܚܘܕܪܐ܀ ܠ. ܒܚܘܕܪܐ ܗܢܘܢ ܡܟܪܟܝܢ[?]܀ Codd. 161, 248 have: Ἀ. κύκλου κύκλον, following which Middeld. rendered the Syriac κυκλοῖ κύκλον, as if ܡ were absent.
[10] Jerome. Nobil. gives: Σ. καταντᾷ.
