[31] Montef., following Nobilius, assigns to Aquila the reading τί δὲ ὁ ἄνθρωπος κ.τ.ἑ., which we, unless we are mistaken, have more correctly claimed for Symmachus. Moreover Nobil. notes: “S. Jerome: _who can go after the king;_” from which Montef. carelessly made: Σ. _who can go after the king._
[32] Syro-hex. ܠ. ܗܘ̇ ܕܐܬܐ ܒܬܪ ܡܠܟܐ. “It is doubtful whether the Syr. had in the text βασιλέως (ܡܠܟܐ) or βουλῆς (ܡܠܟܐ); for the diacritical point is missing.”—_Middeld._ The codex has ܡܠܟܐ, with the Greek word ΒΑϹΙΛЄΥϹ (sic) written beside it.
[33] Syro-hex. continues for Theodotion: ܥܡ ܗܠܝܢ ܡܕܡ[?] ܕܥܒܕܘܗ̇܀ The former part of the reading, σὺν τὰ ὅσα, is in Comp., Codd. 68, 161, 248, 252; the latter in Comp., Codd. III, 248. For the rest, here too Montef., from a misunderstood note of Nobilius, gives as if from Jerome: Σ. _before his maker._
