Cf. on Psal. lxvii. 5. “All these readings we borrow from Drusius and from one Regius, the latter of which assigns the last part of Aquila's version to the Fifth, thus: Ε΄. ὅτι ψευδεῖς συνεπιθέσεις αὐτῶν.”—_Montef._ For ἀπὸ ἀκριβασμοῦ, as was printed before us, we have assigned to Aquila his own ἀπὸ ἀκριβασμῶν σου. For ἀπήλεγξας Agellius thinks Symmachus wrote ἀπέλεξας, _thou hast picked out, as stones from the road, and cast away._ This conjecture is favoured by our Syrian, who excerpted this word from the version of Symmachus, rendering it ܓܒܝܬ, _thou hast chosen._ The Greek ἀπολέγειν, namely, sometimes has the sense of _choosing_, as the Syrian seems to have taken it here; more usually, however, that of _rejecting_ and _repudiating_, in which sense it was put by Symmachus.
