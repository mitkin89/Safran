PSALM. CXLII.

[2] Thus Montef., from one Regius. Chrysost. gives: Ἕτερος· ἐν τῇ π. σου. Ἄλλος· ἐν τῇ β. σου. The latter is Symmachus's rendering at Psal. xxxv. 6.
[3] Thus ἕτερος in Chrysost. Montef., from a Regius, ascribes it to Symmachus, against Syro-hex., which gives: ܐ. ܘܡܬܟܪܟܐ ܗܘܬ܀ Cf. on Psal. cxli. 4. But the reading ἡ ψυχή μου is suspect.
[4] One Reg., and without a name Chrysost. In our judgment, however, the reading savours more of Symmachus.
