PSALM. XIII.
[1] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܠܙܟܘܝܐ ܙܡܪܐ ܕܕܘܝܕ[?] ܀ Montef. gave the Hebrew thus: לַמְנַצֵּחַ מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד; and the Greek of the three translators the same as in the title of the preceding Psalm.
[2] One Reg., on 1 Reg. xxv. 25, has this note: Ἰστέον ὅτι ἐν τῷ ιγ΄ ψαλμῷ καὶ τῷ νβ΄ εἰς τὸ, εἶπεν ἄφρων, νάβαλ κεῖται ἐν τῷ Ἑβραϊκῷ, παρὰ δὲ Ἀκύλαν, ἀπορρέων.
[3] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܐܣܒܪ ܀ Perhaps ὑπέλαβεν is to be preferred on account of the similar passage Eccles. x. 3, where for אָמַר Sym. used ὑπολαμβάνειν. Cf. on Psal. lxxiii. 8.
[4] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܐܣܠܝܘ ܡܫܚܠܦܘܬܐ ܀ Cf. the Hex. on Psal. ix. 12. Jesai. lxvi. 4.
[5] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܚܪ ܀ Perhaps ἀπέβλεψε. See on Psal. xxxiii. 6.
[6] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܟܠ ܚܕ ܐܬܦܢܝ ܐܟܚܕܐ ܀
[7] "The Vaticanus [i.e. Vat. 754, not that πολυθρύλητος Vat. 1209, which is an error of Bruns in Kennicott's _Dissert. Gen._ p. 204: 'The commata of doubtful credit in Psal. xiv are present only in the margin of the Vatican Codex, as Montfaucon attests in the Hexapla'] notes the following on these words, which are written in the margin and not in the text: Οὐδαμοῦ κεῖνται τῶν ψαλμῶν· πόθεν δὲ ὁ ἀπόστολος εἴληφεν αὐτοὺς ζητητέον."—_Montef._ They are read under obeli in Psalt. Gall.; Syro-hex., however, surprisingly, gives them without a distinguishing mark. Hieron. in the Preface to Book XVI of the Commentaries on Isaiah (Opp. T. IV, p. 668): "Finally, all the Greek expositors who have left us commentaries of their learning on the Psalms mark these short verses with a spit and pass them over, plainly confessing that they are not found in the Hebrew, but in the common edition, which in Greek is called κοινὴ, and differs throughout the whole world." This question has been treated more fully by Jo. Theoph. Plüschke in the booklet entitled: _De Psalterii Syriaci Mediol. peculiari indole_ etc. pp. 29–39.
