[33] So Nobil. alone, as it seems. Learned men understand it as “_they defiled, they stained with blood_”, and appeal to the Syriac ܚܦܰܪ, “_he put to shame_”. If the reading is genuine, we shall have to take refuge in a double ἔκδοσιν of Aquila. I almost forgot to note that Syro-hex., in the text τῶν Ο΄, following the older Syriac, rendered ܒܙܥܘ, which is interchanged with the Greek ἔτρησαν, ἔτρωσαν, ἔρρηξαν, διέρρηξαν; never, unless I am mistaken, with ὤρυξαν.
