[42] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܗܕܐ ܙܟܬ ܠܝ܀, i.e., as Bugati renders it, _this was just to me_, or _this purified, justified me._ Rather Symmachus, unless I am mistaken, rendered: τοῦτο περιεγένετό (or περιγέγονέ) μοι, _this was left over to me_, an excellently Greek expression. Thus the Hebrew of Eccles. ii. 22, כִּי מֶה־הֹוֶה לָאָדָם, the same translator renders ὅτι γὰρ περιγέγονεν ἀνθρώπῳ. And our Syrian, in another fragment of Symmachus, Job. xxxix. 32, expressed the Greek περιγενήσεται by ܙܟܐ from ܙܟܐ, _he conquered_. Of a different kind is the Pael ܙܟܝ, _he justified_, from which ܙܟܬ cannot be derived.
