=== Blatt 0263 · S. 247 · –PSALM. LXXXIX. 17.] PSALMI.
--- Fußnoten
[19] Nobil., Vat. (The rarer word διεπέλασεν, as it were _came near across_, need not be exchanged for διεπέρασεν.) Otherwise Hieron. ibid. 14: "And we wonder greatly what the LXX, Theod., and the Sixth ed. meant by translating the Hebrew word AIS ‘_gentleness_’; whereas Aq., Sym., and the Fifth ed. translated ‘_haste_’, and ‘_suddenly_’ and ‘_quickly_’." Where _haste_ (σπουδή) should perhaps be referred to the later and more accurate recension of Aquila. For in the former he rendered אִישׁ, not חִישׁ.
[20] Euseb., Nobil., Vat. Hieron. ibid.: Σ. “_for we are cut down_ (perhaps _are cut off_) _suddenly, and fly away._” Syro-hex. ܣ. ܟܕ ܓܝܪ ܐܬܦܣܩܢ ܡܢ ܫܠܝ ܦܪܚܢ ܀ Where it should be noted that both the Syrian and the Latin translator took ἐκπεταννύμεθα in the same sense as ἐκπετάμεθα.

[30] Euseb. Syro-hex. ܣ. ܚܕܝ ܠܢ ܐܠܗܐ ܟܡܐ ܝܘ̈ܡܬܐ ܕܐܒܐܫܬ ܠܢ. ܫܢ̈ܝܐ ܕܚܙܝܢ ܒܗܝܢ ܒܝܫܬܐ ܀ As if it had read in the Greek: Σ. εὔφρανον ἡμᾶς, ὁ θεός, ὅσας (or πόσας) ἡμέρας ἐκάκωσας κ. τ. ἑ.
