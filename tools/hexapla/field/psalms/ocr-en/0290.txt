[53] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܥܕܡܐ ܕܐܬܥܢܐ ܐܢܐ ܠܐ ܝܕܥ ܗܘܝܬ. ܘܗܫܐ ܠܡܐܡܪܟ ܢܛܪܬ܀ ܣ. ܡܢ ܩܕܝܡ ܡܫܬܡܥ[?] ܗܘܝܬ. ܟܕ ܥܕܟܝܠ ܠܐ ܝܕܥ ܗܘܝܬ. ܗܫܐ ܕܝܢ ܠܡ̈ܠܝܟ ܢܛܪܬ܀. Hieron.: _Before I heard, I was ignorant;_ etc.
[54] Euseb., one Colb.
[55] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܡܩܦܝܢ[?] ܗܘܘ ܒܝ ܕܓܠܘܬܐ ܡܫܬܥܠܢ̈ܐ[?]܀. Bugati renders: _The proud were fabricating a lie against me_, following Castell, who without example attributes to τῷ ܩܦ[?] the sense _to fabricate_. With our Syrian, in the Peal, it is interchanged with the Greek ἅπτεσθαι, and construed with ܒ; of the Aphel conjugation, however, the lexicographers make no mention, nor have we an example at hand, except Prov. iii. 3. vi. 21 in the Syro-hex.: ἄφαψαι (ܐܩܦ[?]) δὲ αὐτὰς ἐπὶ σῷ τραχήλῳ. If we have rendered correctly, ἀφῆπτόν μου ψεῦσμα, Jerome seems to have imitated Symmachus, whose version is: _The proud fastened a lie upon me._ For the rest, with our passage Job. xiii. 4, and the Hex. there, should by all means be compared.
