Hex. on Psal. vi. 8. vii. 5. etc. Hieron. in the Epist. to Sun. and Fret. 46: “‘_Of thine enemies;_’ for which you say that in the Greek is written τῶν ἱκετῶν σου, that is, ‘_of those who entreat thee_’. [6] Euseb., and one Reg. MS. from Theodorus. Schleusner. in _Nov. Thes._ s. v.: “Montef. wrongly rendered, ‘_the earth shall be established;_’ rather, ‘_shall be stirred up, shall rise up together_’ … At one time I thought that συσταλήσεται should be read.” If συσταθήσεται is genuine, it can signify nothing but ‘_shall be joined together, shall be firmly established_’; a sense which, though almost contrary to the Hebrew, coheres aptly with what follows. Yet συσταλήσεται, ‘_shall shrink together with fear_’, would not be bad. Cf. on Psal. lxxii. 21.
