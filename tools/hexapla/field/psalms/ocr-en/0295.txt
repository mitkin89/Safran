[6] Thus ἕτερος in Chrysost. Nobil. assigns it to Aquila, with the agreement of the Syro-hex. ܐ. ܥܡ ܡܘܩܕܢܐ[?] ܕܪܬܡܐ܀ Cf. on Psal. ciii. 17. (The word ܡܘܩܕܢܐ[?], passed over by the lexicographers, stands for ἐσχάρα Job. xli. 10 in the Syro-hex. Cf. our Hex. on Ezech. xxiv. 9.)
[7] Chrysost., who assigns it to ἑτέρῳ. Nobil. cites from the same: Ἕτερος· βέλη τοῦ δυνάστου κ.τ.ἑ. He was followed by Montef., who, on the authority of Martianay, prefixes Σ. Θ. Finally our Syrian: ܣ. ܥܡ ܓܘܡܪܐ ܡܥܡܪܬܐ܀ Bugati renders, _with packed_, or _heaped-up coals_, I believe, not so much from the force of the Syriac word, which seems to have been unknown to him as well as to the lexicographers, as according to the reading attributed to the same translator in the Greek Hexapla. Most opportunely, therefore, our Ceriani advises by letter: “The form ܡܥܡܪܬܐ is sound, and Bar Hebraeus too records it on this passage, writing ܡܥܡܪܬܐ, without however explaining it; but in his Commentary on Ruth [iii. 2] he has this: ܐܕܪܐ ܕܣܥܪܐ[?] ܟܘܪܐ[?] ܗ̄ ܡܥܡܪܬܐ; which, as they confirm the word, so they declare its sense.” As regards the synonym ܥܡܪ, _he heaped up, piled up_, our Syrian at the passage Jos. ii. 6, καὶ ἔκρυψεν αὐτοὺς ἐν τῇ λινοκαλάμῃ τῇ ἐστοιβασμένῃ αὐτῇ ἐπὶ τοῦ δώματος, has ܕܥܡܝܪ ܗܘܐ ܠܗ, on the testimony of Masius in the _Syrorum Peculium_, who however renders it in Greek τὰ ἡτοιμασμένα αὐτῇ, deceived, as it seems, by the Complutensian, which reads τῇ ἑτοιμασμένῃ. (The passage of Joshua reads thus in Cod. Mus. Brit. MSS. Addit. 12,133: ܘܛܫܬ[?] ܐܢܘܢ ܒܩܢܝܐ ܕܟܬܢܐ ܗܘ ܕܥܡܝܪ ܗܘܐ ܠܗ ܥܠ ܐܓܪܐ܀)
