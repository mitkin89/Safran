[17] Euseb.
[18] Hieron. ibid. 13: "For what we have put, ‘_in them_’, and the Hebrew has BAEM, Symmachus translated more expressively, ὁλόκληροι … ‘_But if much_’, and, as Sym. interpreted, ‘_beyond expectation_’, they are eighty years." Whence Montef., following Martianay, produced: Σ. ὁλόκληροι ο΄ ἔτη· ἐὰν δὲ παρὰ προσδοκίαν. But here our Syrian opportunely comes to our aid, giving: ܣ. ܡܫܡܠܝܐ ܫܒܥܝܢ ܫܢ̈ܝܢ. ܐܢ ܕܝܢ ܒܠܐ ܣܒܪܐ[?] ܀ The last word may be rendered ἐν παραδόξῳ (cf. Luc. v. 26 in the Pesch.), and Agellius expressly testifies: "Sym. said παραδόξως, ‘_if they increase beyond expectation_’." The same on Psal. xix. 7 says: "This form of speech, Psal. lxxxix. 10, ‘_but if in strength_’, Sym. translated παραδόξως."
