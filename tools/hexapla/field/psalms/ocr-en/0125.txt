[25] So Comp., Ald., and codd. Psalt. Gall.: "_from a few._" Theodoret in the text τῶν Ο΄ reads ἀπὸ ὀλίγων, but in the commentary has παραπέμπων θανάτῳ, i.e. ἀπολύων. The old Psalter, as Nobilius attests, has "_destroying_", ἀπολλύων, which Augustine attests he found in one Greek codex, and which our Syriac found in its copy, rendering: ܡ̣ ܡܘܒܕ.

[28] "The full readings of Aquila and Symmachus we borrow from four MSS. Agellius has for Sym. χορτασθήσονται δὲ υἱοὶ, and similarly Euseb., Theodoret. and Drusius [but all these witnesses have χορτασθήσονται υἱοὶ, without the particle]. But one Reg. has υἱῶν, the earlier letters erased: for in the first hand it seems to have had υἱοί. At the end of the short verse Vat. notes: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. τὰ κατάλοιπα τοῖς νηπίοις. But the first two have a very different rendering, as you see."—_Montef._ In the text τῶν Ο΄ Syro-hex. reads υἱῶν, not ὑείων. In the reading of Aquila, which seems to rest wholly on the testimony of Montefalconius, Fischer in _Clavis Verss. GG._, p. 73, proposed τὴν περιουσίαν αὐτῶν for αὐτὰ περιουσίαν. From the version of Symmachus Euseb. cites καὶ τῶν ἀποθέτων, and χορτασθήσονται—νηπίοις αὐτῶν; Syro-hex., however: Σ. χορτασθήσονται υἱοὶ, καὶ ἀφήσουσιν (ܢܣܒܥܘܢ ܒܢ̈ܝܐ ܘܢܫܒܩܘܢ ܀).
