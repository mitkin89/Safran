[108] Thus Montef., noting: “Drusius [Nobil.] wrongly κράξαι.” The Syro-hex. cites: ܣ. ܙܒܢܐ ܗܘ ܠܡܥܒܕ ܠܡܪܝܐ, where Bugati: “Sym.: _It is time to seek the Lord. To seek_, I say, or _to cry to the Lord_, Gr. κράξαι, as Drusius printed, not πρᾶξαι, as Montfaucon preferred.” But that our translator put ܥܒܕ[?] in the sense of _crying_ is altogether incredible. If he found κράξαι in his archetype, ܠܡܥܒܕ must certainly be rewritten as ܠܡܩܥܐ[?]. The corrected reading πρᾶξαι is recognized by Origen. Opp. T. II, p. 811.
