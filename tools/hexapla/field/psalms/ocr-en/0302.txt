[2] Thus ἄλλος in Chrysost. Theodoret.: ἀντὶ τοῦ ὀνόματος, ὁ μὲν Ἀ. καὶ ὁ Θ. φόβον ἡρμήνευσαν, ὁ δὲ Σ. νόμον. Otherwise Hieron. in the Epist. ad Sun. et Fret. 78: “You say that you found in the Greek, _for thy name's sake;_ and we admit that many copies [the present-day ones all to a man, with the Syro-hex.] are found thus. But because we are devoted to the truth, we must simply state what is in the Hebrew. For _name_, or _law_, there is read among them THIRA, which Aquila rendered φόβον, _fear;_ Sym. and Theod. νόμον, that is, _law_, thinking it THORA, because of the similarity of the letters _Jod_ and _Vau_, which are distinguished only by size. The Fifth edition rendered _terror_; the Sixth, _word._”
