[2] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܒܫܘܦܪܐ ܕܬܕܐܐ ܐܣܡܟܢܝ ܀. Montef., from Nobilius, less probably cites: Ἀ. Σ. ἐν ὡραιότητι πόας κατεσκήνωσέ με.
[3] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܠܬܫܩܠܢܝ[?] ܀. Cf. on Psal. xxx. 4.
[4] Montef. printed: Σ. ἀνεκτήσατό με; which Nobil. rightly refers to v. 3. The Syriac cites: ܣ. ܐܗܡܝܢܝ ܀, which Bugatus renders “_he withdrew me_”, without sense. For ܐܗܡܝ the Lexicons offer: “_he turned away, withdrew_ (the eyes), _connived, neglected, did not care._” In Greek the verb means ὀλιγωρεῖν, παριδεῖν, ὑπεριδεῖν, and the like; the noun ܡܗܡܝܢܘܬܐ, however, ἀμέλεια (Sym. on Psal. lxxxix. 8. Ezech. xxxix. 26), and the adverb ܡܗܡܝܢܐܝܬ, ἀμελῶς (Jerem. xlviii. 10). Comparing indeed the passages Psal. xxx. 4 (where in the Hexapla is: תְּנַהֲלֵנִי. Ο΄. διαθρέψεις με. Ἀ. διαβαστάξεις με. Σ. τημελήσεις με) and Jesai. xl. 11 (where for יְנַהֵל, Ο΄. παρακαλέσει, Cod. 88 cites: Σ. τημελήσει. Θ. πραέως ἕξει (perhaps ἄξει)), we should be inclined to believe that in our passage too Symmachus rendered in the same way; but that the Syriac translator carelessly confused the somewhat more recondite word τημελεῖν with the more usual, but opposite in meaning, ἀμελεῖν. For we fear it cannot be proved that the sense of “_sparing_” or “_caring_” lies in the Syriac ܐܗܡܝ, as we tried to establish in _Otium Norvic._ p. 13.
