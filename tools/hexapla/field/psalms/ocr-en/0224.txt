=== Blatt 0224 · S. 208 · PSALMI. [PSALM. LXIX. 1–
--- Fußnoten
PSALM. LXIX.
[1] Nobil. from Theodoret. Psalt. Gall.: ※ (sic) “_because the Lord hath saved me_” :. Syro-hex. gives it without the obelus.
[2] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܠܥܒܕ[?] ܙܟܘܬܐ ܕܕܘܝܕ ܠܡܥܗܕܘ܀.
[3] Thus Comp., Ald., Theodoret., Syro-hex., and almost all codd. Psalt. Gall.: ÷ “_make haste_ : _O Lord, hasten to help me._”
[4] Thus Vat., which adds: Ο΄. οἱ ζητοῦντές μου τὴν ψυχήν.
[5] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܢܬܗܦܟܘܢ ܕܢܩܦ ܒܗܬܬܐ ܕܝܠܗܘܢ[?]܀ (Cod. C. ܢܗܦܟܘܢ[?]). Cf. Sym. on Job. xxiii. 8. Ezech. xxix. 6.
[6] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܐܗ ܐܗ[?]܀. Cf. on Psal. xxxix. 16.
[7] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܐܠܗܐ ܡܢ ܫܠܝ[?] ܐܬܐܫܕ[?] ܚܠܦܝ܀. The Latin is Bugati's. The form ܐܬܐܫܕ[?], so far as we know, is not recorded in the Lexicons.
[8] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܠܐ ܬܬܡܗܡܗ[?]܀.
PSALM. LXX.
[1] Thus Comp., Ald., Theodoret., with most books. Syro-hex.: εἰς τὸ τέλος ψαλμὸς τῷ Δ. Psalt. Gall.: ÷ “_A Psalm of David, of the sons of Jonadab, and of the first captives_” :.
[2] Thus Comp., Ald., Theodoret., and others, against Syro-hex.
[3] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܠܫܘܥܐ[?] ܚܣܝܢܐ ܐܝܟ ܕܢܥܪܩܘܢ[?] ܐܡܝܢܐܝܬ. ܦܩܕܬ ܕܐܬܦܪܩ܀. Cf. on Psal. xxx. 3, where the Hebrew has לְצוּר מָעוֹז, _for a rock of strength_, which is found here also in some copies.
[4] Euseb.
[5] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܘܕܡܚܒܠܢܐ[?]܀. The Greek word is uncertain. Cf. our remarks in _Otio Norvic._ p. 46.
[6] “From two Regian MSS.”—_Montef._ The same gives from the same MSS.: Ἄλλος· ὁ ὑπερασπιστής; which seems foreign to this place.
