=== Blatt 0217 · S. 201 · —PSALM. LXVII. 15.] PSALMI.
--- Fußnoten
[22] Suidas s. v. ἑκούσια, as pointed out by J. C. G. Ernesti in _Gloss. Sacr._ p. 40.
[23] Vat.
[24] Euseb., who reads τὴν (for καὶ) μεμοχθ. Theodoret. gives: Ἀ. καὶ μεμοχθ. σὺ ἥδρασας.
[25] Euseb. Montef., following Theodoret., printed ἣν ἐξεποίησας καὶ ἥδρασας. Syro-hex. gives: Σ. ἣν ἐξεπόνησας, ἥδρασας (ܠܗܝ ܕܦܠܚܬ ܫܬܐܣܬ). (With ܦܠܚ compare ܦܘܠܚܢܐ[?], διαπόνημα, in the Hex. on Psal. xv. 4.)
[26] Montef., who falsely cites Drusius as his witness.
[27] Euseb. Theodoret. and Nobil. have τοῖς εὐαγγελιζομένοις for εὐαγγελιζομένῃ.
[28] Montef. printed: Σ. β. τῶν στρατιωτῶν κ.τ.ἑ., noting: “Thus Euseb., Theodoret., [Nobil.] and two MSS. Euseb. has, ἢ ἀγαπητοὶ ἐγένοντο.” In fact Euseb. reads στρατειῶν; Theodoret., however, has στρατιῶν in the text, with the var. lect. στρατιωτῶν. The Syrian, according to Cod. A., has ܕܚܝ̈ܠܘܬܐ, τῶν δυνάμεων, which is the LXX version; but Codd. B. and C. exhibit ܕܦܠ̈ܚܘܬܐ, τῶν στρατιῶν. Because of Eusebius' reading, ἢ ἀγ. ἐγένοντο, Schleusner. suspects that these words did not come from Symmachus, but contain a mere interpretation of the preceding ἠγαπήθησαν. But the Syrian acknowledges them, whose note is to be transcribed presently. (In the text τῶν Ο΄, τοῦ ἀγαπητοῦ occurs only once in Comp., Ald., and part of the codices. Psalt. Gall. gives: “_King of hosts_ ※ _of the beloved_ ↙.”)
[29] “One Reg. MS. from Theodorus.”—_Montef._ Theodorus in _Catena PP. GG._, T. II, p. 349 gives: Ἀ. καὶ ὡραιότητος οἴκου μερίσεται λάφυρα. Hieron.: “_and the beauty of the house shall divide the spoils._”
[30] One Reg. MS., Theodoret., Nobil., the two latter of whom read διανεμεῖ. Syro-hex. gives the version of Symmachus of v. 13 entire: ܣ. ܡܠܟ̈ܐ (Cod. C. ܡ̈ܠܟܐ) ܕܦܠܚܘܬܐ (ܘܕܦܠ̈ܚܘܬܐ) ܐܬܚܒܒܘ. ܡܚܒ̈ܒܐ ܗܘܘ. ܘܡܕܒܪܢܘܬܐ[?] ܕܒܝܬܐ ܡܦܠܓܐ ܒܙ̈ܬܐ܀.
[31] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܒܝܢܝ ܬܚܘ̈ܡܐ܀. Hieron.: “_between the middle boundaries._” Cf. Hex. on Gen. xlix. 14.
[32] “Euseb., and one Reg. MS. from Theodorus, who reads ἀναμέσον for μεταξύ.”—_Montef._
