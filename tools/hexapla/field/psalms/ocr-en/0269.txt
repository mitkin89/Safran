PSALM. XCIV.
[1] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܥܡ ܬܫܒ̈ܚܬܐ[?] ܒܫܝܦܘܪܐ ܢܝܒܒ ܠܗ܀ The word ܒܫܝܦܘܪܐ, _with the trumpet,_ serves only for definition. Cf. on Psal. lxxx. 2. xcvii. 4.
[2] One Coislin. Cf. on Psal. xcvii. 4. xcix. 1.
[3] Thus Comp., Ald., Cod. Alex., and others. The Syro-hex. has only: καὶ β. μ. ἐπὶ πάντας τοὺς θεούς.
[4] Nobil.
[5] The same. Euseb. cites: Σ. ἐπάνω πάντων τῶν θεῶν.
[6] Nobil. Hieron.: _in whose hand are the foundations of the earth._
[7] Vat.
[8] Nobil. Theodoret. cites: Ἀ. Σ. δυσηρεστήθην. "Some have rendered ἐβδελυξάμην."—_Agell._ It is an explanation of Theodoret.
[9] Theodoret., Nobil.
PSALM. XCV.
[1] Scholion in Nobil. The obelus is in the Psalt. Gall. (The reading ᾠκοδομεῖτο is supported by the Syro-hex., Cod. Alex., and others; and so, on the testimony of Agellius, the Vat. manuscript in the old writing, although a later hand has made it ᾠκοδόμηται. To the contrary, Mai printed οἰκοδομεῖται, noting: "The second hand τ̅ο̅ for τ̅α̅ι̅.")
[2] Thus Comp., Ald., and the manuscripts.
[3] Cod. Bodl. N E B. 2. 120.
[4] Nobil. Syro-hex. ܐ. ܦܚ̈ܠܐ[?]܀ ܣ. ܠܐ ܐܝܬܝܗܘܢ܀ On Aq. cf. the Hex. on Jesai. xxxi. 7.
[5] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܬܫܒܘܚܬܐ ܘܫܘܒܗܪܐ ܩܕܡܘܗܝ. ܚܝܠܐ ܘܡܫܝܢܘܬܐ[?] ܦܐܝܘܬܐ[?]܀ On ܫܘܒܗܪܐ, ἀξίωμα, cf. on Psal. xliv. 4, 5.
[6] Euseb. in the Comment. p. 107. Montef. printed from Colb.: αἱ σ. τῶν ἐθνῶν.
[7] _Catena PP. GG._, T. II, pp. 958, 966, as Schleusner points out in the _Opusc. Crit._ p. 246.
[8] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܒܫܘܒܗܪܗ ܕܩܘܕܫܐ܀
[9] Theodoret. and two Regii. Nobil. cites: Ἀ. ὠδινήσατε. Syro-hex. ΩΔΙΝΗCΑΙ (sic) ܐ. ܣ. ܚܒܠܘ܀ The same notes: "ܚܒܠܘ (is said) of the pains of childbirth."
