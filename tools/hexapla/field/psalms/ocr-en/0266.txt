[1] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܙܡܝܪܬܐ ܕܡܙܡܘܪܐ ܠܝܘܡܐ ܕܫܒܬܐ܀

[5] Cod. Bodl. N E B. 2. 120, copied by Bruns, in the margin, which has been wretchedly trimmed by the bookbinder, cites: Συμ …. εντω …. στησ …. σεβο …. μοια …. τωκαι …. σει …. τεσ …. τερ …. μεν …. φει …. Bruns notes: "Assuming that some letters have been badly drawn in my copy, I fill the gaps thus: Συμμάχου· ἐν τῷ βλαστῆσαι ἀσεβεῖς ὁμοίως τῷ χόρτῳ, καὶ ἤνθησαν πάντες οἱ ἐργαζόμενοι ἀνωφέλειαν." We had the manuscript inspected anew; whereupon it appears that Bruns's copy was most accurately made, except that the second syllable in σεβο, and the sign και after τω, cannot be explained with certainty. In the former clause we have largely followed Bruns; in the latter, having rejected his reading, we have not ventured to substitute another; especially since our Syrian supplies a fairly probable version of Symmachus.
