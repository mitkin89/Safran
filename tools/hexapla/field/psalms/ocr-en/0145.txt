[22] “From Drusius [Nobil.], and one codex.”—_Montef._ For the Vth Montef. printed _μαιομένου_, against his predecessors. For Symmachus Vat. has _παιδία_ instead of _πεδία,_ in agreement with our Syriac, which cites: ܣ. ܕܡܓܒܠ ܛܠܝܐ, i.e. _τυποῦντος_ (or _ἐκτυποῦντος_) _παιδία._ “For my part I do not think it should be doubted that the Syriac read _παιδία_ instead of _πεδία_ . . But whether it read _πλάσσοντος_, ‘_fashioning_’ or ‘_forming_’, instead of _πληθύνοντος_, I leave for others to discuss.”—_Bugat._ For _πεδία_ stands the passage Psal. xli. 2, where for the Hebr. אַיָּל, “_hart,_” the Vth or VIth ed. renders _πεδίον._ As for the other word, rejecting _πλάσσοντος,_ which our Syriac constantly renders by ܓܒܠ, I have no probable conjecture by which the Syriac could be reconciled with the Greek.
