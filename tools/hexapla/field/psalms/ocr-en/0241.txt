--- Fußnoten
[4] (Forts.) as well as to Symmachus ἀνομβρήσω is attributed in _Catena PP. GG._, T. II, p. 632 … In the same Catena the preposition ἐξ before ἀρχῆθεν is entirely omitted, and, as I for my part think, rightly.”—_Schleusner._ He is mistaken. For with that translator ἀρχῆθεν constantly answers to τῷ קֶדֶם; but here, where the Hebrew has מִנִּי קֶדֶם, in keeping with his ἀκριβολογίᾳ and his affectation of Homeric forms, he put ἐξ ἀρχῆθεν.
