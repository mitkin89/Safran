[1] (Forts.) γνωσιν ἔλθῃ ἑαυτοῦ, _when he has despaired of himself;_ which he took from Symmachus, who renders ἀθυμεῖν." For the rest, just as here ܥܩܬ ܠܗ represents ἀθυμεῖ, so ܥܩܬܐ is interchanged with ἀθυμία in a reading of the same translator on Ezech. vii. 25.

[3] Theodoret., Nobil., Vat. and Cod. 264. For ἐψαθυρώθη Schleusner cites ἐπυρώθη from the _Catena PP. GG._, T. III, p. 7, but rightly regards it as a gloss. Suidas: Ψαθυρός, ξηρός. Καὶ τὰ ὀστᾶ μου ὡσεὶ καῦσις ἐψαθυρώθη. The Syrian renders not badly: Ἀ. ὡς καῦσις (ܡܩܝܕܐ[?]) _were crushed, were ground_ (ܐܬܦܪܦܪܘ[?]=διεθρύβησαν, Sym. on Psal. lxxxix. 6). For ψαθυρὸς in its primary sense is _friable._ Cf. the Hex. on Jos. ix. 5.
