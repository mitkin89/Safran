=== Blatt 0265 · S. 249 · –Psalm. XC. 14.] PSALMI.
--- Fußnoten
[12] (Forts.) Psal. x. 2) περιπατοῦντος. The Syriac ܡܚܒܠܢܐ stands for ὀλοθρευτὴς 1 Cor. x. 10 in both versions; for διαφθείρων, as it seems, in the Hex. on Jerem. vi. 28; also for λοιμός, _corrupter,_ Hos. vii. 5. Here indeed I would believe that Aquila rendered ἀπὸ λοιμοῦ, _from the pestilence,_ which the Syrian seems to have taken of a _pestilent man._
[13] Nobil. Hieron.: _from the bite of one raging at noon._ Cf. our Hex. on Hos. xiii. 14.
[14] Thus Vat., on the testimony of Adler, who thinks the particle οὐ should be deleted; and Nobil., who cites it without the negative; both wrongly. More to blame is Montef., who printed from Corderius: Σ. ἀπὸ συγκυρήματος δαιμονιώδους μεσημβρίας.
[15] Euseb. Theodoret., Nobil.: Ἀ. Σ. ἐκ πλαγίου σου. Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܡܢ ܓܢܒܟ܀. Cf. Gen. vi. 17 in the Syro-hex.
