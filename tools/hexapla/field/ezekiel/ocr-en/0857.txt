At ἡ Ἑλλὰς (ܗܠܠܣ) (not, as Norberg, at φύλακες, v. 11) Syro-hex. adds in the margin without an index: ܐ. ܣܘܢ. ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܘܠܐ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ܀ For ܐ. ܣܘܢ, which yields no sense, I correct: ܐ. ܣ. ܝܘܢ, to which is immediately subjoined, for the sake of explaining the Hebrew name, ܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܘܠܐ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ. Hieron.: “JAVAN means Greece, which is interpreted, _It is and it is not._” That is, the Hebrews, a nation born for devising etymologies, hammered out this sense somehow from יֵשׁ, _it is_, וְ, _and_, and אַיִן, _it is not_.—“Here, moreover, is a scholion not cited by Norberg: ܣܘܟܠܗ܀[?] ܝܘܢ. ܡܬܦܫܩܐ ܕܝܢ. ܕܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܘܠܐ ܐܝܬܘܗܝ. ܣܟܠ ܗܢܐ ܕܐܠܗܐ[?] ܐܡܥܡܗ[?] ܗܠܝܢ[?] ܡܥܡܕܝܢ܀[?] By this scholion the correction ܐ. ܣ. ܝܘܢ is confirmed, I think.”—_Ceriani._
