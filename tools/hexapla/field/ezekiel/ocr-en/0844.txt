--- Fußnoten
[26] (Forts.) ܐܘܣܝܐ[?] ܡܬܡܕܪܬܐ[?]. The verb of the Pael form ܡܰܕܰܪ[?] is used of fruits which _spoil_ or _pervert_ their flavours in S. Ephraem's Carm. Nisib. XXIX, 120, where Bickell in the _Addenda_ compares the Arabic مَذِرَ, _it was spoiled, it rotted._ Related is the equally unknown word ܡܕܘܪܘܪܐ[?], which our Syrian interchanged with the Greek σκωρία in Hex. on Psal. cxviii. 119. Our friend Ceriani takes another path in a letter written to us four years ago: “What if ܡܕܘܪܐ[?] were an adjective from ܡܕܪܐ[?], which denotes _clay without sand_ in Thomas of Novaria p. 261; so that our translator rendered the word rather freely from the context, _silver mixed with clay?_”)
