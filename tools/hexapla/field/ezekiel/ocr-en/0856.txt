Jes.: ※ οἱ ὅμ. οἱ οἰκοδ. σε κ.τ.ἑ., differently from Parsons., who copied from the margin of Cod. XII: οἱ αμοροι σοι οἱ οἰκοδ. (without σε) κ.τ.ἑ. Hieron.: “_Thy neighbours who built thee have filled up thy beauty_… For which, intending I know not what, the LXX translated thus: _Beelim thy sons have surrounded thee with beauty._ For the word _Beelim_ is not found at all in this place among the Hebrews, but instead of _Beelim_ is written GEBULAIC, which signifies _thy borders_. In what they said, _thy sons_, too, they were deceived by the ambiguity of the word and the similarity of the writing, since with different pronunciation the same letters are read _of the masons_ and _of the sons_, BONAICH and BENAICH.”
