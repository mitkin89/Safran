[24] Syro-hex. ܬ. ܘܗܿܝ ܕܐܝܬܝܗ̇ ܢܘܚܐ ܐܬܪܐ ܕܦܬܟܪ̈ܐ ܕܒܝܬܐ ◆. The Syriac word ܢܘܚܐ is used for ἀνάνευσις Psal. lxxii. 4, where I suspect that our Syriac had in his copy ἄνεσις, which is read today in one codex, not, as occurred to Bugati, ἀνάπαυσις. In Greek writers ἀνιέναι means _to leave free to all,_ as _the sea, untilled land_ (Jesai. v. 6 in the LXX). We shall not deny, however, that our Greek is very uncertain.
[25] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܠܬܪܥܐ ܕܝܢ ܠܓܒܐ ܠܨܠܘܬܐ ◆. In the LXX text after βορρᾶν Codd. 22, 23, 36, and others insert: εἰς προσευχὴν πηχῶν πέντε περικύκλῳ. Hieron. renders the Hebrew: "_And the door of the side toward the place of prayer_ (which is not found in the LXX), _one door toward the way of the north, and one door toward the way of the south; and the breadth of the place for prayer_ (which the LXX likewise did not translate) _five cubits round about._"
