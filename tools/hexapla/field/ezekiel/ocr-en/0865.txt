=== Blatt 0865 · S. 849 · –Cap. XXIX. 7.] EZECHIEL.
--- Fußnoten
[58] (Forts.) the LXX, _and_ [_thou_] _the God of their fathers,_ is not found in the Hebrew."
CAP. XXIX.
[1] Thus Comp., Ald., Codd. 42, 49, 86, 87, and others, and Syro-hex., which in the margin, char. med., has: (ἐν τῷ ἔτει) τῷ δωδεκάτῳ, ἐν τῷ δωδεκάτῳ μηνί.
[2] Cod. 86, on the testimony of De Reg.
[3] Cod. Jes. Thus in the text Comp., Ald., Codd. III (with καὶ λ.), XII, 22, 23, 36, and others, and under the asterisk Codd. 87, 88, and Syro-hex. (which marks: ※ λάλησον, καὶ ↙ εἰπόν). For εἰπὸν De Reg. cites from Cod. 86: Οἱ Γ΄. ἐρεῖς.
[4] Cod. XII. Thus in the text Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex., and without the asterisk Comp., Ald. (with βασιλέα), Codd. III, 23, 26, 36, and others.
[5] A scholion in Nobil. Cod. Jes. has the scholion: τὸ Ἑβραϊκὸν τὸ κῆτος ἔχει, ἵνα ἐμφανῇ (ἐμφήνῃ ?) τὸν κροκόδειλον. Cf. Hex. on Jesai. xxvii. 1.
[6] Cod. 86.
[7] Theodoret. Instead of αὐτοῦ, Αἰγύπτου is in Ald., Codd. 49, 68, and others, and Syro-hex. in the margin. Next Cod. 87 marks: ※ ἐμοί εἰσιν οἱ π., without the metobelus, perhaps for: ἐμοί ※ εἰσιν ↙ οἱ π. (For יְאֹרִי Codd. 62, 147 have οἱ ποταμοί μου.)
[8] Cod. 86, on the testimony of De Reg. Hieron. renders the Hebrew: _I have made myself._
[9] Cod. 86. Syro-hex. cites: Θ. χαλινὸν (ܦܓܘܕܐ[?]), which reading Comp., Codd. XII (in the margin), 23, 36, and others have in the text.
