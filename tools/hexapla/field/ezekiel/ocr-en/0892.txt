Hieron.: "Sym. rendered this passage thus: _And he made_ περιστάσεις [not παραστάσεις, as Drus. and Montef.], which we may call _surroundings_, for which in the Hebrew there is ELIM, which Aq. rendered κρίωμα, and we have turned into _fronts_. Again, where the LXX put _the theim of the court_, in the Hebrew there is EL, in the singular number, which Sym. translated _near the court_, Aq. κρίωμα, that is, _the front of the court_."
