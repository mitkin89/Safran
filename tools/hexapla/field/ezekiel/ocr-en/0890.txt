Whence Montef. published: Ἀ. ἐξέναντι τοῦ βορρᾶ. Σ. Θ. ἀπὸ νότου ἀπὸ μεσημβρίας, noting: "We borrow the Greek reading of the translators from the Codex Regius;" which can hardly be true of Aquila, since it is sufficiently established that this translator as well as the LXX read מִנֶּגֶד for מִנֶּגֶב, and that the word _north_ belongs to Jerome's explanation.
