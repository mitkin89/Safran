CAP. XLVI.
[1] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܒܝܕ ܐܘܪܚܐ ܕܬܪܥܐ[?] ◆. Immediately after, τῆς πύλης τῆς ἔξωθεν (not ἔσωθεν) is found in Comp. (without the latter article), Ald., Codd. III, XII, 22, 23, 36, and others, and Syro-hex.

"Of this verse the LXX give two versions; of which the second, which however in C [Cod. 87] is marked with an obelus, alone agrees word for word with the Hebrew text."—_Drach._ He is mistaken; for the first version, καὶ προσκυνήσει—ἕως ἑσπέρας, belongs to v. 2, and agrees accurately with the Hebrew.
