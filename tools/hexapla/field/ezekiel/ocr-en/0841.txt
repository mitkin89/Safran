[48] (Forts.) chronius had in view the other meaning of the Hebrew word, _of wiping_ or _polishing_, in which it is used in Eccles. x. 10. And the grammarians unanimously report that Ῥύψαι was used by the Attic writers for the more common σμῆξαι.
[49] Montef. cites from Cod. Jes.: Ἀ. ἐν τοῖς θαραφείν. Σ. τὰ εἴδωλα. But De Reg. from Cod. 86: Ἀ. Θ. ἐν τοῖς θεραφείν. Cf. Hex. on Gen. xxxi. 19.
[50] Cod. 86, according to De Reg. Less probably Parsons. from Codd. 86, 88: Ἀ. τύρσεις. Θ. προμαχῶνας. Cf. on Cap. iv. 2. Syro-hex. cites: ܣ. ܦܘܪܓܘܣ̈ܐ[?] ◆ ܬ. ܡܓܕ̈ܠܐ ◆. (Both Syriac words are interchanged with the Greek πύργος; the former more rarely, which is read in 4 Reg. ix. 17. Dan. iv. 26. 1 Esdr. i. 52 (from Lagarde's correction). In the passages Jerem. xxxiii (xl). 4. Ezech. iv. 2 our Syrian renders προμαχῶνας by ܦܐܪܐ[?] and ܦܐܪ̈ܐܣ[?].)
