=== Blatt 0821 · S. 805 · –Cap. XVI. 29.] EZECHIEL.
--- Fußnoten
[32] Hieron.: “For _sewn together_ and _of divers colours_, and _high places_, or _idols_, Aq. and Theod. rendered ἐμβολίσματα, which signifies various rags sewn together here and there, and an idol made like a plaster, as it were a πολύρραφον garment.”
[33] Hieron. Syro-hex. ܣ. ܢܡܪ̈ܐ ܡܨܒܥܢ̈ܝܬܐ[?] ◆, i.e., as Norberg renders, _many-coloured high places._ Bernstein, in the _Zeitschrift_ etc. T. III, p. 409, asserting that such a sense is foreign to the word ܢܡܪ̈ܐ, unhappily conjectures ܡܩܒ̈ܐ[?], _burnt offerings_, for places where burnt offerings were offered. “Perhaps ܢܡܪ̈ܐ renders the single word טְלֻאוֹת, since Castell. has from Ferrarius: ܢܡܪܐ, _an undergarment worked with the needle;_ and Cod. Ambros. Syr. M. 18 sup.: ܢܡܪܐ. ܗ̄. ܦܪ̈ܘܡܐ[?] ܕܐܡܝܪ ܠܚܠܡܐ[?].”—_Ceriani._ At εἴδωλα ῥαπτὰ Parsons. copied from the margin of Cod. XII: ὑψηλὰ διαβεβοησμένα (perhaps διαβεβοημένα, or διαβεβλημένα).
[34] Hieron. continues Symmachus: _and thou didst commit fornication in them, things not done, nor to be._ Syro-hex. cites: ܣ. ܕܠܐ ܗܘܘ ܘܠܐ ܗܘܝܢ ◆.
[35] Thus Cod. 87, Syro-hex., and without the asterisk Codd. XII (in the margin), 22, 23, 36, and others.
[36] Hieron.: “But in that place where we have set down, _and thou gavest them, consecrating them to them_, for which the LXX translated, _and thou gavest them to appease_, or _to expiate_, Aq., Sym. and Theod. set down, _thou didst transfer_ and _didst lead them across_.”
[37] Thus Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex., and without the asterisk Comp., Ald., Codd. III, XII, 22, 23, 26, and others. Hieron.: “And what is said according to the LXX, that is, _above all thy fornication and thy abominations_.”
[38] Cod. Jes. Thus in the text Comp., Ald., Codd. III, 22 (without σοι), 23, 26, and others, and under the asterisk Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex. Hieron.: “This which is said a second time, _woe, woe to thee_, the common edition does not have, but it has been added from the edition of Theodotion.”
[39] Hieron.: “For which the LXX translated . . . _a dwelling of fornication_, Sym. and Theod. rendered πορνεῖον; Aquila, wishing to express the etymology of the Hebrew word GOB, set down βόθυνον, which we can call _a pit_.”
[40] The same: “For which the LXX translated ἔκθεμα, the Hebrew has RAMA, and Aq. as well as Sym. and Theod. rendered _a high place_, or _lofty_ [cf. on v. 39].” (On ἔκθεμα, which properly means _exposition_ (Phrynichus: Ἔκθεμα βάρβαρον· σὺ δὲ λέγε πρόγραμμα), Polychron. in Montef. notes: Ἔκθεμα λέγει, οἷον τὸ προαγώγιον, ἔνθα τὰς πόρνας τρέφειν εἰώθασι. Our Syrian renders ܡܘܬܒܐ, _seat_, with an unedited scholion: ܡܘܬܒܐ ܡܣܡܟܢ ܕܐܡܪ. ܐܬܪܐ ܕܐܣܝܪܐ[?] ܕܙܢܝܬܐ ܠܚܠܡܐ[?] ܐܝܟ ܕܠܡܦܪܢܣܘܬܐ[?] ܕܙܢܝܘܬܐ ◆.)
[41] Thus Comp., Codd. 26, 49, and others, and Syro-hex. “ἧς is wanting in Cod. Vat.”—_Mai._ Hieron.: “And what follows, ἠσέβησας, that is, _thou hast acted impiously_, according to the LXX is joined to the following chapter, according to the others it is the end of the preceding.”
[42] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܠܒܪ ܡܢ ܐܘܪ̈ܚܬܐ ܕܝܠܟܝ ܛܢ̈ܦܬܐ ◆.
[43] Hieron.: “What we translated according to Symmachus, _wicked_, Theod. set down the Hebrew word itself, ZEMMA.” Cf. on Cap. xxii. 9. xxiii. 29.
[44] Thus Comp., Codd. XII, 22 (from correction), 23, 36, and others, Syro-hex., and Hieron.
[45] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܠܒܢ̈ܬܐ. ܡܛܠ ܕܠܐ ܣܒܥܬܝ ◆.
