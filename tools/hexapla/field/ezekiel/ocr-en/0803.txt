III, p. 421), cited by Nobilius: Τὸ, ὁ βασιλεὺς πενθήσει, συνήθως ἱστόρηται (ἠστέρισται), τάχα διὰ τὸν σωτῆρα οὐκ ἂν νομισθέντα πενθεῖν, οὐχ ἑρμηνευσάντων τῶν Ο΄ τὸ ῥητὸν, ἢ μὴ εὑρηκότων τότε ἐν τῷ Ἑβραϊκῷ τὴν λέξιν. (Drusius on the passage: "Origen: τὸ, ὁ βασιλεὺς πενθήσει, συνήθως ἱστόρηται, which they emend to ἠστέρισται, _it has been marked with an asterisk._" To whom the emendation, which is excellent, should be attributed we do not know; but to this day the absurd reading ἱστόρηται, which they translate in Latin, _it is narrated_, has been silently propagated in the editions of Origen.) Hieron.: "_The king shall mourn, and the prince shall be clothed with grief_, or according to the LXX, _with destruction;_ who, intending I know not what, passed over in complete silence this which we have expressed from the Hebrew language, _the king shall mourn_."
