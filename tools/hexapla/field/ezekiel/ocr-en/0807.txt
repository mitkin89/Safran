=== Blatt 0807 · S. 791 · –Cap. IX. 10.] EZECHIEL.
--- Fußnoten
[10] (Forts.) _Thes._ s. v., who thinks that μέλαν καὶ κάλαμος corresponds to קֶסֶת alone. But it may be that τὸν Ἑβραῖον, whoever he was, confused בַּדִּים and בְּדְיוֹ (ἐν μέλανι; see Hex. ad Jerem. xxxvi. 18).
[11] Codd. 86, 88. Thus Cod. 86 on Cap. x. 6, 9, according to De Reg.
[12] Cod. 86, according to De Reg. Thus in the text Cod. Jes. Cod. 62: ἀπὸ τοῦ χερουβί.
[13] Codd. 86, 88: Ἀ. Σ. τῶν οὐδῶν. Cf. on Cap. x. 18. xlvii. 1. Hieron.: "Where we have translated, _the threshold of the house,_ the LXX and Theod. put τὸ αἴθριον, that is, the _vestibule_ _under the open sky._"
[14] Cod. Jes. Thus in the text Codd. 22, 23, 36, others, and under the asterisk Codd. 87, 88. Syro-hex.: ※ μέσην τὴν π. ↙ μέσην Ἱερ.
[15] Cod. 86 (with θαῦμα for θαῦ), according to De Reg. (Parsons from Codd. 86, 88: Ἀ. Θ. καὶ σημειώσεις τὸ θαῦμα. Σ. καὶ σημειώσεις σ. Nobil. from Origen incorrectly gives: Ἀκύλας καὶ Θεοδοτίων· σημείωσις τοῦ θαῦ ἐπὶ τὰ μέτωπα.) Somewhat differently Hieron.: "For _sign,_ which the LXX, Aq. and Sym. translated, Theod. put the Hebrew word itself THAU."
[16] Thus Codd. III, 42, 86, others, and Syro-hex. in the margin.
[17] Codd. 86, 88.
[18] Cod. Jes. Cod. 86, according to De Reg.: Θ. τὰ θαῦμα (sic).
[19] Parsons from Cod. 88 gives: Οἱ Γ΄. ἁγιασμοῦ. Less probably Cod. 86, according to De Reg.: Οἱ Γ΄. τοῖς ἁγίοις μου.
[20] Nobil. It seems to be a scholium. Presently Cod. 87, Syro-hex.: ἀπὸ τῶν ἀνδρῶν ※ τῶν πρεσβυτέρων ↙; where Cod. 106 alone rejects τῶν πρ.
[21] Cod. 86, according to De Reg., and thus in the text Cod. 62. Parsons wrongly copied from Codd. 86, 88: Ἀ. συνμιάνατε.
[22] A scholium in Nobil. Hieron.: "For _courts,_ which in Hebrew are called ASEROTH, the LXX put _ways._"
[23] Cod. Jes. (with πατάξετε). Thus in the text under the asterisk Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex.
[24] Thus Codd. 22 (with ἔκοπτον for ἔτυπτον), 23, 36, others. Cod. XII in the margin: καὶ ἐξελθόντες ἔτυπτον τὴν π.
[25] "Theodotion adds: ὑπελείφθην ἐγώ."—_Nobil._ Thus in the text Codd. 22, 23, 36, others, and under the asterisk Codd. XII, 87, 88, and Syro-hex. Cod. 62 alone prefixes the copula.
[26] Cod. 86, according to De Reg. Thus (with ποιήσεις) Cod. 238 in the margin.
[27] Cod. 86, according to the same. Origen in Nobil.: ἀντὶ τοῦ λαῶν [πολλῶν om. Codd. 87, 106, Syro-hex., and Hieron.], αἱμάτων οἱ λοιποὶ πεποιήκασι.
[28] Codd. 86, 88. Cf. Hex. ad Jesai. lviii. 6, 9.
[29] Cod. 86, according to De Reg.: Ἀ. σὺν (τὴν γῆν). Οἱ Γ΄. καὶ (οὐκ). Cod. 62 in the text: σὺν τὴν γῆν, καὶ οὐκ ἔστιν, expressing Aquila, as it seems.
