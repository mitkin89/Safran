XII marks: ※ ἐκεῖ—πύλης ↙, with the second asterisk absent, as it seems; Cod. 87: ※ ἐκεῖ πλ. τὴν ὁλ. ÷ ἐν δὲ τῷ αἰλὰμ τῆς π. τῆς δ. ἔκρυσις, δύο τρ. ἔνθεν ↙, καὶ δύο τρ. ἔνθεν; Cod. 88: ※ ἐκεῖ—ὁλοκαύτωσιν ↙. ÷ ἐν δὲ τῷ—τῆς πύλης ↙ τῆς δ. ἔκρυσις, ※ δύο τρ. ἔνθεν, καὶ δύο τρ. ἔνθεν ↙; lastly Syro-hex.: ※ ἐκεῖ—ὁλοκαύτωσιν ↙. ἐν δὲ—δύο τρ. ἔνθεν ↙, καὶ δύο τρ. ἔνθεν; where the latter metobelus has nothing to which it can refer. Hieron.: "In this passage (vv. 35–43) the Hebrew verity differs greatly from the edition of the LXX translators. For what we have said: _There shall they wash the burnt offering, and in the porch of the gate two tables on this side;_ and again: _That the burnt offering and the sin offering may be slain upon them,_ is not found in the LXX, but in most codices has been added from Theodotion." (As regards ἔκρυσις (ܡܕܟܝܢܐ[?] ܕܡܚܬܐ[?]), or ἐκρύσεις, I should not think, with Schleusner in _Nov. Thes._ s. v., that it is to be referred to the remote יָדִיחוּ, but to שֻׁלְחָנוֹת, as if from שָׁלַח, _he sent forth_ (water), whence the name of the fountain Siloah (שִׁלֹחַ).)
