Σ. ※ οὕτως ἐρεῖς πρὸς αὐτούς."—_De Reg._ Hieron.: "It should be known that eight verses, more or less, from the place we have given: _Ye who eat with the blood, and lift up your eyes toward your uncleannesses,_ down to the place where it is written: _Thus shalt thou say unto them, thus saith the Lord God,_ are not found in the LXX, who omitted these too along with many other things; or, if they were translated by them, they have gradually been removed through the fault of copyists."
