[10] “Theodoret has the reading of the Hebrew translator from the Codex Regius.”—_Montef._ It is not read in the edited Theodoret.
[11] Hieron.: “The time _of lovers_, or according to the LXX, _of those turning aside_ [דּוֹרִים[?]], and according to the second translation of Aquila, συναλλαγῆς, that is, _of betrothal_ and of the time of marriage; but according to the first edition of the same and that of Theodotion, μαστῶν, which means _of breasts;_ for which Sym. rendered ἀγάπης, which signifies _love_.” Also Syro-hex.: Σ. ἀγάπης (ܕܚܘܒܐ).
