(ܘܡܣܬܟܠܢܬܐ[?], not, as Norberg copied, ܘܡܣܟܢܬܐ[?], _wretched_, which is no word at all. Drach saw the truth). [38] Cod. 86, according to De Reg. Montef. edited: Ἀ. Σ. βλασφημία, from Jerome, whose words are: "_And thou shalt be a reproach and a blasphemy_ . . . for which in the LXX is read: _And it shall be_ (_thou shalt be_) στενακτὴ, that is, _lamentable;_ and from Theodotion there has been added καὶ δηλαϊστὴ, a word of which we have no knowledge. For which three (?) other translators rendered _blasphemy_."
