Hieron.: “And I wonder greatly why the common copies have _a hundred and ninety years_, and in some it is written _a hundred and fifty_; whereas clearly both the Hebrew, and Aquila, Symmachus and Theodotion hold _three hundred and ninety years_, and among the LXX themselves, where they have not been corrupted by the fault of scribes, the same number is found.”
