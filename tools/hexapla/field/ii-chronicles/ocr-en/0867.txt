CAP. XXXIV.
[1] The clause καὶ ἀπὸ τῶν περιβωμίων is rejected by Codd. II, III, XI, 52, 55, 60, 64, 119, 158, rightly; for it does not express the Hebrew וְהַפְּסִלִים, but is a double version of the preceding word וְהָאֲשֵׁרִים, and plainly betrays Symmachus as its translator. Cf. Hex. on 4 Reg. xvii. 16. xxi. 7. xxiii. 4, 7.
[2] So Comp. (omitting the second ἀπὸ), Codd. 19, 108 (as Comp.). In this reading too the clause καὶ ἀπὸ τῶν βωμῶν, which is a double version of the Hebrew מִן־הַבָּמוֹת, is due, unless I am mistaken, to Symmachus. Cf. Hex. on 3 Reg. xii. 32.
