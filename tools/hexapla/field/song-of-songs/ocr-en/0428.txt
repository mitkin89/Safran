Syro-hex. ܘ. ܕܪܣܝܣܐ܀, i.e. S΄. _of plates_, on the authority of Castell, who appeals to this very passage, where Pesch. has ܥܡ ܪܣܝܣܐ ܕܣܐܡܐ. But ܪܣܝܣܐ is also the passive participle of the verb ܪܣ, _he sprinkled;_ and therefore it corresponds quite fittingly to the reading of the uncertain translator, μετὰ ῥαντῶν.
