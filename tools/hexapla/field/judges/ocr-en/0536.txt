XXXVII. in Judic.: “_And the number of those who lapped with their hand, with their tongue, was three hundred men._ Most Latin codices do not have _with their hand_, but only _with their tongue._ The Greek, however, has both, _with their hand, with their tongue_, so that it is understood that they snatched up the water with the hand and threw it into the mouth.” Cf.
