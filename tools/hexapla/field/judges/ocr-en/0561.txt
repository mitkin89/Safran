It is, unless I am mistaken, another version of the following clause מִגָּדִישׁ וְעַד־קָמָה, or rather מִקָּמָה וְעַד־גָּדִישׁ, “_from the standing corn to the shocks._” Accordingly, for these words, καὶ ἐκάησαν—καὶ ἐλαίας, the Vet. Lat. has only: “_and he set fire to their ears of corn, and to what had been reaped, and also to the vineyards and olive groves._”
