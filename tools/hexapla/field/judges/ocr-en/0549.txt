=== Blatt 0549 · S. 437 · –CAP. X. 18.] JUDICES.

[16] Cod. 85 in the margin. So in the text Comp. (omitting οἱ before ἀλλόφυλοι, and καὶ before ἐξέθλιψαν), Ald. (as Comp., with οὐχ for οὐχὶ), Codd. III (with οὐχὶ Αἰγ. καὶ Ἀμ.—καὶ Σιδ. καὶ Μαδιὰμ καὶ Ἀμαλὴκ ἐξέθλιψαν κ. τ. ἑ.), X, XI, 15 (with Μαδιὰμ for Χαναὰν, and omitting καὶ before ἐξέθλιψαν), 18 (the same), others, and Syro-hex., from which we have taken the obelus.
[17] Cod. 85, according to Montef. For these words, καὶ ἀπὸ τῶν Φυλ. καὶ ἀπὸ Σιδ., Parsons’s amanuensis copied only καὶ ἀπὸ τῶν Φυλιστιδίων (sic).
[18] So Comp., Ald., Codd. III, X, XI, 15, 18, 19, others (among them 85 in the margin), Syro-hex.
[19] So Ald., Codd. X, XI, 63, 121, against Syro-hex.
[20] So Comp., Ald., Codd. III, X, XI, 15 (with ἐὰν), 18 (the same), 19 (with ἐὰν ἀρέσκει), others (among them 85 in the margin with ὅσα ἀρέσκει), Arm. 1, Syro-hex.
[21] So Syro-hex., and without obelus Comp., Codd. III, 19, 108; and also (with ἐξελοῦ ἡμᾶς κύριε) Codd. 44, 54, 59, others. Arm. 1: πλὴν κύριε μόνον ἐξ. ἡμᾶς.
[22] So Syro-hex. (the metobelus being absent), and without asterisk Ald., Codd. X, 16 (with δὴ ἡμᾶς), 29, 30, others (among them 85).
[23] So Comp., Ald., Codd. III, XI, 15, 18, 19, others.
[24] So Syro-hex. (with the metobelus only), and without obelus Ald., Codd. III (with εὐηρέστησαν), 15, 18 (as III), 19 (omitting ἐν), 44 (with εὐηρ. κύριος ἐν), 54, others, Arm. 1. The word μόνῳ is wanting in Comp., Codd. III, 19, 54, others, Syro-hex.
[25] So Comp., Ald. (without τῷ), Codd. III, X (with ὁ Ἰσρ.), XI, 15, 18, 19, others, Syro-hex. (which seems to have taken Ἰσραὴλ as a nominative, rendering: ܘܐܬܟܪܝܬ ܢܦܫܗ ܕܐܝܣܪܝܠ ܒܥܡܠܐ[?].). On ὠλιγώθη Cod. 85 in the margin gives: ὠλιγοψύχησεν.
[26] So Cod. 44 alone (in which the whole verse reads thus: καὶ παρενέβαλον καὶ ἀνεβόησαν ἐν Γαλαὰδ καὶ ἐν Μασσηφὰ), in agreement with Syro-hex., which renders the former clause: ܘܐܙܥܩܘ ܒܢܝ̈ܐ ܕܓܠܥܕ[?].
[27] Cod. 108. The translator seems to have rendered וַיִּצְעֲדוּ; compare Hex. on 2 Reg. vi. 13.
[28] So Comp., Ald., Codd. III, XI, 15, 18, 19, others (among them 85 in the margin), Arm. 1, Syro-hex.
[29] Cod. 85. The reading ἐν τῇ Μασσηφὰ is in Comp. (with Ἀμασσηφὰ), Ald., Codd. III, 15, 18, 30, others, Syro-hex. (with ܒܡܨܦܐ).
[30] Bar Hebraeus: ܒܡܨܦܐ ܐܘ̄ ܒܒܝܬ ܕܘܩܐ[?].
[31] So Comp., Ald., Codd. III, XI, 15 (with ἄρξηται), 18, 29, others (among them 85 in the margin), Arm. 1, Syro-hex.
