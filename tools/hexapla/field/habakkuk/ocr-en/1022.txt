=== Blatt 1022 · S. 1006 · HABACUC. [Cap. II. 14–
--- Fußnoten
[22] (Forts.) ἔνδεσμος ξύλου· ὅθεν ἐστὶν ἐννοεῖν, ὅτι τάχα που τὰς τῶν οἰκιῶν μίτρας, καὶ τὴν ἐν αὐταῖς ἀναπλοκὴν τῶν ξύλων, ἤτοι σύνδεσμον, κανθάρους ὠνόμαζον κατ᾽ ἐκεῖνο καιροῦ, διὰ τὸ οἷον πολλοῖς ἀντέχειν ποσὶ τὴν ἐπικειμένην αὐτοῖς ὀροφήν.
[23] Hieron.: “I found, besides the five editions, that is, of Aquila, Symmachus, the LXX, Theodotion, and the Fifth, in the twelve prophets two other editions as well, in one of which is written: _For the stone_ etc.; and in the other: _For a stone_ etc.”
[24] Thus Comp., Codd. 22, 36, 42, 51 (with -ψει), 62 (with καλύψαι), and others, Hieron., Syro-hex. (ܠܡܚܦܝܘ[?] ܝܡ̈ܡܐ).
[25] Hieron.: “Aquila, ἐξ ἐπιτρίψεως [Vallars.: ‘On the authority of the Regius MS. we have restored ἐπιτρίψεως, in which reading the other Barberini MS. cited by Montef. also agrees. Formerly it was ἐκπέμψεως’] χόλου σου, which we can render, _from the sending forth of thy fury._” Cod. 86 cites: Ἀ. ἐξ ἐπιρίψεως (not, as Montef., ἐπιτρίψεως) χόλους (sic); which reading seems to stand nearer to Jerome's _sending forth._
[26] The same: “For _turbulent overthrow_ Sym. rendered: καὶ ἀφιῶν κ. τ. ἑ., that is, _and sending forth his fury without judgment._” Also Cod. 86: Σ. ἀφιῶν κ. τ. ἑ.
[27] The same: “Theod. ἀπὸ χύσεώς σου, which means, _from thy pouring out._”
[28] The same: “The Fifth ed. ἐξ ἀπροσδοκήτου κ. τ. ἑ., which signifies, _from the unexpected overthrow of thy wrath._”
[29] The same: “In another edition I found: οὐαὶ τῷ ποτίζοντι κ. τ. ἑ., which in our tongue means: _Woe to him who gives his friend a flying whirlwind to drink._”
[30] The same: “But elsewhere too I have read it rendered: _Woe to him who gives his neighbour to drink_ ἔκστασιν ὀχλουμένην, that is, _turbulent madness._”
[31] Cod. 86. Parsons transcribed from Cod. 42: Σ. αἰσχύνην. S. Cyril. in XII Proph. p. 544 A: Ἐκδεδώκασι γὰρ οἱ ἕτεροι τῶν ἑρμηνευτῶν ἀντὶ τῶν σπηλαίων, τὴν γύμνωσιν.
[32] Thus Comp., Ald., Codd. 22, 23, 51, and others, against Syro-hex., which reads: πίε καὶ σύ· καὶ σὺ καρδία κ. τ. ἑ. Hieron.: _and be moved_ (omitting καὶ σείσθητι).
[33] Cod. 86. Hieron. renders the Hebrew: _and fall asleep._ Both interpreted הֵעָרֵל as הֵרָעֵל, _to be seized with dizziness._ Cf. Hex. on Psal. lix. 5. Jesai. li. 17.
[34] Cod. 86. Aquila's αἷμα either belongs to the Hebrew מִדְּמֵי, Ο΄. δι᾽ αἵματα; or is miswritten for ἀδικία.
[35] Cod. 86. By what error I know not, our Syriac cites on θηρίων: Ἀ. ἀνθρώπων (ܕܒܢܝ̈ܢܫܐ).
[36] Cod. 86.
[37] Thus Comp., Ald., Codd. 22, 26, 36, and others, Hieron., Syro-hex.
[38] Syro-hex. ܬ. ܟܕ ܓܒܠ[?] ܀
[39] Hieron.: “And he makes images _dumb,_ or _deaf:_ for κωφὰ among the Greeks signifies both; although Sym., rendering ἄλαλα, seems to have understood _dumb_ rather than _deaf._”
[40] The same: “Whence Aq. also renders the Hebrew more expressively, saying: _And his spirit is not in the bowels,_ or _in the midst of it._”
