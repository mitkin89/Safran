CAP. I.
[1] Hieron.
[2] The same: “Where in the Hebrew is written RAU BAGGOIM, and we rendered, _look among the nations,_ and the LXX put, _see, ye despisers,_ apart from Aq. and Sym. and Theod., who agree with our rendering, I found in a certain other anonymous (ἀνωνύμῳ) edition, _ye shall see the slanderers,_ and in another, likewise without the author's title, _ye shall see those turning aside._”
[3] Cod. 86 cites: καὶ ἴδετε, with ⅄ over ἴδετε. Cf. Hex. on Mich. i. 15. Thus Codd. 36, 62, and others.
[4] Thus Syro-hex. with a little note: “÷ This obelus was not placed in the Hexapla.”
[5] Hieron.: “Sym., for what we said, _for a work is done in your days,_ rendered, _for a work shall be done in your days;_ the rest similarly.”
[6] Theodoret. For δόγματι ἑαυτοῦ, which is read in Schulz's Cod. C, and Parsons' Codd. 42, 240, the editions have δόγμα τὸ ἑαυτοῦ, which is less sound syntax. [8] Hieron.: “In that which . . . the LXX rendered: _And his horsemen shall ride_ . . . Sym. rendered: _his horsemen shall be poured out_ [as if from פָּשָׂה, _it spread itself._ Cf. Hex. on Levit. xiii. 5], that is, they shall rush down and be dashed to the ground.”
[9] Theodoret. Hieron. renders the Hebrew: _Their faces are a burning wind,_ suppressing the difficult word מְגַמַּת.
