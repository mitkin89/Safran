Hieron.: “But it must be known, as we said above, that where the LXX put in the plural number, _that thou mightest save thine anointed ones,_ there is in the Hebrew LAJESUA ETH MESSIACH; which Aq. rendered, _for salvation with thine Anointed;_ not that God went forth to save the people and to save his Anointed; but that he came for the salvation of the people with his Anointed . . . And the Fifth ed. rendered similarly: _Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, for salvation with thine Anointed._”
[35] Hieron.: “But Theod., truly as a poor man and an Ebionite, and Sym. too, of the same doctrine, following a poor sense, rendered in Jewish fashion: _Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, that thou mightest save thine Anointed;_ and: _Thou wentest forth to save thy people, to save thine Anointed._ I am about to say an incredible thing, but nevertheless true: these half-Christians rendered in Jewish fashion, and the Jew Aquila translated like a Christian.”
