=== Blatt 0997 · S. [981] · OBADIAS.
--- Fußnoten
[1] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܬܗܪܐ[?] ◆. Cf. Rördam on Jud. v. 22.
[2] Theodoret.
[3] Cod. 86 (with the reading ἀγγελείαν). Cf. Hex. on Jerem. xlix. 14. "The noun צִיר the LXX here rendered περιοχὴν, which Hieron. gives as _fortification_; but more in accordance with the mind of the Greek translator it is rendered in the Syro-hex. version, on the testimony of De Rossi, ܥܘܩܬܐ[?], _distress_. For the Greek Alexandrian translator took צִיר as the singular of the noun צִירִים, _pangs._"—_Rosenm._ He is mistaken: for ܥܩ[?] and ܥܘܩܬܐ[?] constantly alternate with πολιορκεῖν and πολιορκία. See Hex. on Jerem. lii. 5. Ezech. xxi. 20. More correctly S. Cyril. in XII Proph. p. 356 B: τὴν περιοχὴν, ἤτοι πολιορκίαν, ἤγουν συγκλεισμόν.
[4] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܐܛܥܝܟ ◆. Cf. on v. 7.
[5] Cod. 86. Cf. Hex. on Jerem. xlix. 9.
[6] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܬ. ܐܛܥܝܘܟ ◆.
[7] Thus Codd. 22, 23, 51, and others.
[8] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܠܚܡܟ ܢܣܝܡܘܢ ܥܠ ܐܣܪܐ ◆ ܣ. ܗܢܘܢ ܕܐܟܠܝܢ ܥܡܟ ܢܥܒܕܘܢ ܢܘܟܪܝܘܬܐ. ܕܠܐ ܢܗܘܘܢ ܬܚܝܬܝܟ ◆ ܬ. ܠܚܡܟ ܢܣܝܡܘܢ ܘܐܣܪܐ ܬܚܝܬܝܟ ◆. For Aquila Middeld. gave ἐπὶ τὸν σύνδεσμον, because מָזוֹר is σύνδεσμος in the same translator at Hos. v. 13. But in that very place Hieron. notes two renderings of Aquila, σύνδεσμος and ἐπίδεσις, the latter of which I would believe stood in our passage; and that the Syrian, reading not ἐπίδεσιν but ἐπὶ δέσιν, rendered ܥܠ ܐܣܪܐ.
