=== Blatt 0999 · S. 983 · –Ver. 21.] OBADIAS.
--- Fußnoten
[34] (Forts.) a Jew, 'This,' he said, 'is the region to which Hadrian transported the captives. When therefore our Christ comes, then that captivity too will return to Judaea.'" Cf. Gesen. in _Thes. Ling. Hebr._ p. 969. S. Cyril. in XII Proph. p. 362 D also mentions the _Bosphorus_, but mixed true things with false: Παραταθήσεται δὲ, φησὶ, καὶ ἕως Ἐφραθὰ, ἤγουν ὡς ἐκδέδοται παρὰ τῶν ἑτέρων ἑρμηνευτῶν, καὶ μέχρι Βοσπόρου, ὅ ἐστι τὰ νοτιώτατα τῆς ἕω μέρη. For it is as certain as can be that the other translators rendered according to the Hebrew, ἡ ἐν Σαφαράδ.
[35] Syro-hex. ܗܠܝܢ ܕܫܪܟܐ ܕܬܝܡܢܐ ◆.
[36] Thus Codd. III, XII (which marks ανδρες σε- with dots), 26, 49, and others.
[37] Cod. 86.
[38] Hieron.: "But whereas we rendered, _and saviours shall come up_, and the LXX translated, _those who have been saved_, in the Hebrew is written MOSIM; which is to be understood not passively, as by Aq. and the LXX and Theod., σεσωσμένοι, or ἀνασωζόμενοι, but, according to Sym., σώζοντες, that is, actively, _saviours_."
