For my part I have no doubt that my predecessor devised this whole reading on the model of v. 28, forgetting that in the Hebrew there one reads not רֶמֶשׂ but חַיָּה. In our passage I am inclined to think that Aquila rendered: καὶ ἐν παντὶ κινουμένῳ (cf. Hex. ad Psal. cxlviii. 10) κινουμένῳ ἐπὶ γῆς.
