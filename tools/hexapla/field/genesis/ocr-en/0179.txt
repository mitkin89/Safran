[10] Montef. transcribed from Cod. X: Ἀ. τροφὴν κατάλογον (perhaps κατάλληλον) τοῦ ὄχλου. Holmes., however, cites from the same without name: τροφὴν κατάλογον τοῦ ὄχλου. Scharfenb. in _Animadv._ p. 46 claims the reading for Symmachus, appealing, for טַף, ὄχλος, to Hex. ad Exod. x. 10. xii. 37. He also assigns τροφὴ for לֶחֶם to Symmachus, not Aquila. Cf. Hex. ad Psal. ciii. 14. Eccles. ix. 11. He goes on: “As for Montef.'s conjecture that κατάλογον, which certainly does not suit this passage, should be replaced by κατάλληλον, _suitable_, there seems to be no need of so far-fetched an emendation, if only one separates what has run together through the scribe's fault, thus: κατὰ λόγον τοῦ ὄχλου.” But in fact κατάλληλον is not Montfaucon's emendation, but the actual reading of the codex, at least according to the old Bodleian collation, in which I clearly read: Ἀ. τροφὴν κατάλληλον. Ο΄. σῖτον κατὰ σῶμα. How this new witness is to be reconciled with those just mentioned I cannot say. One thing I see: neither Symmachus nor any other translator could have admitted the construction κατάλληλον τοῦ ὄχλου in place of the only legitimate κατ. τῷ ὄχλῳ.
