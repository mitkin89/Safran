Hieron.: “_Heap_ in the Hebrew language is called GAL, while AAD means _testimony._ Again, in the Syriac language _heap_ is called IGAR, and _testimony_ SEDUTHA. Jacob, therefore, called it _heap of testimony_, that is, GALAAD, in the Hebrew language; but Laban called the very same thing, that is, _heap of testimony_, IGAR SEDUTHA [ܝܓܰܪ ܣܳܗܕܽܘܬܳܐ], in the speech of his own people. For he was a Syrian, and had exchanged the ancient language of his fathers for the speech of the province in which he lived.”
