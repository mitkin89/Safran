Hieron.: “The Hebrew word CHABRATHA, in what follows [Cap. xlviii. 7] . . . , the LXX translators, intending I know not what, rendered _hippodrome;_ and immediately there, where the Hebrew says: _And they buried her on the way to Ephrata, that is Bethlehem_, the LXX again put _hippodrome_ for _Ephrata;_ whereas, surely, if CHABRATHA is a hippodrome, Ephrata cannot be a hippodrome.” The same, in his Commentary on Mich. iv. 9: “She (Rachel), because she was _on the way_ and _in the hippodrome_, that is, _the race-course of the horses_ which are sold in Egypt, bore a _son of sorrow_, whom his father afterwards called _son of the right hand_.”
