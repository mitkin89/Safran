[2] Nobil., Codd. X, 127. Evidently our translator, judging the ordinary meaning τοῦ נִסָּה, _he tempted_, to be unworthy of God, had recourse to the word of similar sound נָשָׂא, _he lifted up_. Cf. Psal. iv. 7 in the Hebrew and the Hexapla. In vain, therefore, does L. Bos in _Prolegom. ad LXX_, Cap. III, think that ἐδοκίμασε should be read for ἐδόξασε. Cf. Scharfenb. in _Animadv._ p. 21.

[4] Hieron.: “Where it is now said, _go into the high land_, the Hebrew has MORIA, which Aq. rendered τὴν καταφανῆ, that is, _bright_; Sym. τῆς ὀπτασίας, that is, _of the vision_. The Hebrews say, then, that this is the mountain on which the temple was afterwards built, on the threshing-floor of Ornan the Jebusite, as is also written in the Chronicles [2 Paral. iii. 1] … which is therefore interpreted _illuminating_ and _shining_, because there is DABIR, that is, the oracle of God, and the law, and the Holy Spirit, who teaches men the truth and inspires the prophets.”
