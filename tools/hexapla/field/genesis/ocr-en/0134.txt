=== Blatt 0134 · S. 22 · GENESIS. [CAP. V. 29–
--- Fußnoten
[23] (Forts.) _And M. lived, after he begot L., seven hundred and eighty-two years, and begot sons and daughters. And all the days of M. were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died. And L. lived a hundred and eighty-two years, and begot Noah._ From the day of Methuselah's birth, then, to the day of Noah's birth there are three hundred and sixty-nine years: add to these the six hundred years of Noah, since the flood took place in the six-hundredth year of his life: and so it comes about that Methuselah died in the nine hundred and sixty-ninth year of his life, in the year in which the flood began.” For the rest, in v. 28 Arab. 2 alone, as it seems, has _two_ for _eight_ in accordance with the Hebrew verity, against the Syro-hex.

Finally our Syriac: ܣ. ܒܢܝܐ ܕܡܫܬܠܛܢܐ܀[?] Hieron.: “The Hebrew word ELOIM is of common number; for both _God_ and _gods_ are called by it alike: on which account Aq. ventured to say in the plural number _sons of the gods_, understanding by _gods_ the _saints_ or _angels._ For _God stood in the congregation of the gods; and in the midst he judgeth the gods_ (Psal. lxxxi. 1). Hence Sym. too, following a sense of this kind, says: _The sons of the mighty, seeing the daughters of men_, and so on.”
