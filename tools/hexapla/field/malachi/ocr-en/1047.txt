=== Blatt 1047 · S. [1031] · [MALACHIAS]
--- Fußnoten
CAP. I.
[1] Hieron.: “What _the burden_ signifies, that is, _the weight_, which in Hebrew is MASSA, and by Aquila is called ἆρμα; or what λῆμμα, that is, _the taking up_, which both the LXX and the other translators rendered, we have said in the other prophets.”
[2] Cod. 86. Hieron.: “Accordingly, apart from the LXX, the other translators rendered the name _Malachi_ just as it is read in the Hebrew.”
[3] The obelus is in Syro-hex. Hieron.: “LXX: _Lay it to your hearts._ This is not found in the Hebrew, but I think it was added from Haggai [ii. 16], in which we read: _And now lay it to your hearts from this day and upward._”
