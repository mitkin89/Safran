[2] Thus Codd. III, 22, 36, 40, and others, and Syro-hex. in the margin (ܘܢܬܟܢܫܘܢ). “Barb. συναχθήτωσαν; in the margin συγχυθήσονται, and Δ΄. ἀβώτῳ ξηρασίᾳ. But these seem to refer to τῆς ἐρήμου, Cap. i. 19, 20.”—_Walton._ “According to the arrangement in the codex, before συναχθήτωσαν there seem to belong: Δ̂. ἀβάτῳ τηρασίᾳ (sic).”—_Cozza._ It is, as it seems, a scholion on συγχυθήσονται, preceded by a siglum whose meaning is unknown. Since, however, the subject is not _drought_ but approaching _darkness_, perhaps ἀορασίᾳ should be written for ξηρασίᾳ.
