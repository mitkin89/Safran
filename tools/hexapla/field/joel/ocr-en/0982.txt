=== Blatt 0982 · S. 966 · JOEL. [Cap. III. 2–19.]
--- Fußnoten
CAP. III.
[1] Syro-hex. in the text: ܘܐܚܬܘܢ[?] ܐܢܘܢ, against the Greek books; but in the margin: ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܘܐܚܬ ܀.
[2] Nobil. Cf. on v. 14.
[3] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܘܥܠ ܥܡܐ ܕܝܠܝ ܗܘ ܕܡܦܠܓܝܢ ܗܘܘ ܒܦܣܐ. ܘܝܗܒܝܢ ܗܘܘ ܛܠܝܬܐ ܚܠܦ ܙܢܝܬܐ ܀.
[4] Thus Ald., Codd. III, XII (corrected), 68, and others, and Syro-hex. Another reading, καὶ τί ἐμοὶ καὶ ὑμῖν, is in Comp. (with καίγε τί), 22, 36, and others.
[5] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܐܦ ܡܢܐ ܐܢܬܘܢ ܠܝ ܀ ܣ. ܐܠܐ ܘܡܢܐ ܐܢܬܘܢ ܠܝ ܀ ܬ. ܐܦ ܬܘܒ ܐܢܬܘܢ ܠܝ ܀.
[6] Hieron.: “For _Galilee_ in the Hebrew is written GALILOTH, which Aq. translated θῖνας, Sym. _borders_.” Cf. Hex. on Jos. xxii. 10. Ezech. xlvii. 8.
[7] Syro-hex. Also Cod. XII in the margin: Οἱ Ο΄. κεφαλήν.
[8] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܠܣܒܐܝ̈ܐ[?] ܀. Hieron.: “The Hebrew word SABAIM, which Aq. and Sym. translated just as it is written, the LXX rendered _captivity_, which would better be _captives_.”
[9] Syro-hex. in the text: ܚܝܠܬܢܐ; but in the margin: ܐ. ܡܚܝܠܐ ܀ ܣ. ܬ. ܐܝܟ ܗܢܘܢ ܫܒܥܝܢ ܀. Montef. cites from Cod. Jes.: Ἀ. Ο΄. ὁ ἀδύνατος. In the text, besides our Syrian, Codd. XII (corrected), 86, Hieron. stand for δυνατὸς.
[10] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܟܢܘ̈ܫܝܐ ܟܢܘ̈ܫܝܐ ܀ ܬ. ܣܘܓ̈ܐܐ ܣܘܓ̈ܐܐ ܀. (The word ܟܢܘܫܝܐ alternates with the Greek συναγωγὴ, συστροφὴ, σύστημα, σύνοδος, and others; never, unless I am mistaken, with ὄχλος (ܟܢܫܐ), which was Middeldorpf's preference.)
[11] Hieron.: “Again, where we said _in the valley of cutting_, following the translation of Aquila and Symmachus and the Fifth ed., the LXX and Theod. translated τῆς δίκης and τῆς κρίσεως, that is, _of the cause_ and _of judgment_; for which in the Hebrew is HARUS.” On Aquila cf. Hex. on Jesai. xxviii. 22. Syro-hex. on δίκης in the second place cites: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. _of swift judgment_ (ܕܕܝܢܐ ܡܥܓܠܐ[?]), which can be rendered in Greek τῆς κρίσεως τῆς συντόμου (cf. Prov. xiii. 23. xxiii. 28 in Syro-hex.), an expression which represents the two versions not badly.
[12] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܕܟܘ̈ܒܐ[?] ܀.
[13] Hieron.: “For _torrent of cords_, or _of thorns_, Sym. rendered _valley of thorns._” Cf. Hex. on Mich. vi. 5.
[14] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܡܛܠ ܥܘܠܐ ܗܘ ܕܒܒܢ̈ܝܐ ܕܝܗܘܕܐ. ܕܕܡܐ ܙܟܝܐ ܗܘ ܕܐܫܕܘ ܀. Wrongly Middeld.: ὑπὲρ τῆς ἀδικίας τῆς τῶν υἱῶν (ܗܘ ܕܒܢ̈ܝܐ) Ἰούδα. Hieron. renders the Hebrew: _because they acted unjustly against the sons of Judah._
[15] Syro-hex., which notes: “÷ This obelus was not in the Hexapla.” Thus without the obelus Codd. XII, 23, 26, 36, and others.
