[5] Jerome: "_And Israel shall not be gathered_, that is, shall not return to the Lord. And I greatly wonder how the common edition [LXX] has overthrown, by another interpretation, a most powerful testimony against the faithlessness of the Jews, saying: _I shall be gathered, and shall be glorified before the Lord;_ whereas Theod. and Sym. agree with our interpretation. But about Aquila I do not wonder, that a man most learned in the Hebrew tongue, and expressing word for word, in this place either feigned ignorance, or was deceived by the perverse exposition of the Pharisees, since he chose to interpret, _and Israel shall be gathered to him_, that is, to God."
