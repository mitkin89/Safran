V, 8: Ἀλλ᾽ οὐχ ὡς ἔνιοί φασι τῶν νῦν τολμώντων μεθερμηνεύειν τὴν γραφὴν, ἰδοὺ ἡ νεᾶνις ἐν γαστρὶ ἕξει, καὶ τέξεται υἱὸν, ὡς Θεοδοτίων ἡρμήνευσεν ὁ Ἐφέσιος, καὶ Ἀκύλας ὁ Ποντικός. Hieron.: “A virgin is called in Hebrew BETHULA, which is not written in the present passage; but in place of this word ALMA is put, which all except the LXX translated _young girl_. Moreover ALMA is with them an ambiguous word; for it means both _young girl_ and _hidden_, that is, ἀπόκρυφος . . . Therefore ALMA means not only _girl_ or _virgin_, but with ἐπιτάσει _a hidden virgin_ and _secluded_, who has never been exposed to the sight of men, but has been guarded with great care by her parents.” Cf. Hex. on Gen. xxiv. 43.
