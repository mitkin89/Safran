=== Blatt 0466 · S. 450 · JESAIAS. [Cap. IX. 21 (20)—
--- Fußnoten
[32] Syro-hex.
CAP. X.
[1] Curter. Syro-hex. in the text: ※ܓ. ܐܦ ↙ ܚܛܦܝܢ[?]. Then for these words: Ο΄. ἐκκλίνοντες —— εἰς προνομὴν, Montef. printed “from a certain MS. of St. Basil on Isaiah”: Ἄλλ. διαστρέφοντες τὸ δίκαιον κρίμα ἐπὶ τῶν πτωχῶν, καὶ διαρπάζοντες τὰ τῶν πενήτων τοῦ λαοῦ, ὥστε εἶναι αὐτοῖς χήραν εἰς κατάβρωμα, καὶ ὀρφανὸν εἰς κέρδος. The passage is Opp. T. I, p. 556 C, and the words are not those of another interpreter, but of the holy Father himself in explaining the passage, as Garnier rightly perceived.
[2] Curter., who has in the text χρήματα for κρίματα, which is the reading of Codd. XII, 22, 36, and others.
[3] Theodoret.
[4] Curter. Parsons. cites from the Moscow Slavonic: _do not bow down under the bond._
[5] Curter. Thus Syro-hex. in the text, and without asterisk Comp., Ald., Codd. III, 22, 23, 26, 36, and others.
[6] Curter. has in the text θυμός; but in the margin: Οἱ Ο΄. ἡ ὀργή. The reading θυμὸς, or ὁ θυμὸς, or ὁ θυμὸς αὐτοῦ, is in very many books, against Syro-hex.
[7] Curter. Parsons. cites from Codd. XII, 93: Ἀ. Θ. τοῦ ποιῆσαι. But anyone can see that ποιῆσαι must at least be denied to Aquila.
[8] Curter., corrected by Montef.
[9] Idem. (In the text χώρας is read by Codd. III, XII, 22, 24, 26, and others. Syro-hex. in the margin in middle-sized characters: ܘܡܠܟܘܬܗܘܢ[?] ܐܬܪܘܬܐ.)
[10] Thus Syro-hex., which in the printed text and in both codices has ܘܠܝܒܪܘܣ[?], for which Bernstein. in the _Zeitschrift_ etc. T. IV, p. 201 conjectures ܘܠܝܒܘܣ, καὶ τῇ Ἰεβούς. In the Hebrew and Greek codices there is no help.
[11] Euseb. The pronoun, which was missing, has been supplied from Codd. 22, 48, 51, and others, which supply τῆς χ. μου after ἰσχύϊ.
[12] Curter. Cod. A. in the text: ܒܐܝܕܐ ※ ܕܝܠܝ[?] ↙; but in the margin: ܟܠܗܘܢ, which Middeld. wrongly understood of the word πάντα to be inserted into the text. More clearly Cod. C. in the margin: ܟܠܗܘܢ ܕܥܡܐ[?] ※ ܕܝܠܝ[?] (↙) ܐܡܪܘ.
[13] Curter. Cf. on Cap. xxxviii. 14. Syro-hex., after ἀντείπῃ μοι, cites in the margin: ܬ. ܘܦܬܚ ܦܘܡܗ ܘܡܨܦܪ[?]. The same words are read without asterisk in Comp., Ald., Codd. 22, 36, and others, the greater part of which have στόμα without the article.
[14] Thus Comp., Ald., Codd. III, XII, 22, 26, and others.
[15] Cod. 88 (with ※[?] for ÷). Syro-hex. ܐܝܟ ܗܘ ÷ ܗܟܘܬ ↙ ܐܢ.
[16] Euseb., Procop.
[17] Procop.
