Jerome: "Where we have said, _I will lay thy stones in order_, in the Hebrew is written BAPHPHUCH, which all except the LXX have similarly translated, _I will lay thy stones in stibium_, in the likeness of an adorned woman, who paints her eyes with stibium, so as to signify the beauty of the city."
[31] Procop. Jerome: "And where we, following the LXX, have said _jasper_, in the Hebrew is written CHODCHOD [cf. Hex. on Ezech. xxvii. 16], which Sym. alone translated χαλκηδόνιον."
