=== Blatt 0457 · S. 441 · –Cap. VI. 13.] JESAIAS.
--- Fußnoten
[9] Hieron. in Epist. XVIII to Damasus, 17: “Aq. and Theod.: _And one of the seraphim flew to me;_ Sym.: _And one of the seraphim flew to me_ [masculine].”
[10] Thus Syro-hex. in the text. The pronoun is in Codd. 24, 36, 48, others.
[11] Hieron. ibid. 18: “Aq. and Theod.: _And in his hand a stone in tongs, which he had taken from the altar;_ Sym.: _And in his hand a stone in tongs_ [plural], _which he had taken from the altar._” But the same in his Commentary on the passage: “And in his hand he holds the stone, which, according to the LXX and Theod., he took up with _tongs_ [singular], according to Aq. and Sym., who followed the Hebrew, with _tongs_ [plural].” And this seems more correct. (In Montefaucon's Greek we have corrected ψῆφος for ἄνθραξ, on the authority of Hieron., who says: “That _stone_, which is rendered by the LXX alone ἄνθραξ, that is, _a live coal_.”) On the words τῶν Ο΄, ὃν τῇ λαβίδι, Curter. notes: Οἱ Γ΄. ὁμοίως.
