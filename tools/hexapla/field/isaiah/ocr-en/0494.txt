Barber. “Here Theodotion either derived the word ראה from רוה, or interpreted altogether metaphorically.”—_Montef._ To Schleusner indeed the reading ἐν τῇ μέθῃ seems to belong to בַּשֵּׁכָר, and ἠσωτεύθησαν to תָּעוּ or שָׁגוּ, with ὑπερόγκως taken in for embellishment. But ὑπέρογκος is interchanged with derivatives of פָּלָא at Deut. xxx. 11. Thren. i. 10. Dan. xi. 36; and in our passage Jerome expressly connects ὑπερόγκως with פְּלִילִיָּה, translating the Hebrew: “(_They knew not_) _the seer, they were ignorant of judgment_, or MARVELLOUSLY.”
