[6] Jerome p. 282: “_A faithful planting_, or, as Aq. and Theod. rendered, _beautiful;_ or, as Sym., _good_.” The same p. 283: “Someone may wonder why, for _beautiful_ and _good plantings_, as Aq. and Theod. and Sym. rendered, we have said _a faithful planting_. The Hebr. word NEEMANIM, if it is written with the letter ALEPH, means πιστοὺς, that is, _faithful;_ but if with AIN, and it is pronounced _neamenim_, it signifies _beautiful_.”
