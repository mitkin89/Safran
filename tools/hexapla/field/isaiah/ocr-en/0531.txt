"It is, as it seems, Symmachus', who puts ματαιότης for תֹּהוּ Psal. cvi. 40."—_Montef._ The printed Syro-hex.: ܐ. ܒܝܫܐ ܘܣܪܝܩܘܬܐ ܗܢܘܢ ܕܓܒܠܝܢ ܠܟܘܢ[?] ܀. Middeld. notes: "In ܒܝܫܐ I conjecture that the translator slipped, confusing κακὸν and κενόν. For that [κενὸν] occurs rather often in the Greek fragments for the Hebr. רוּחַ. [He is mistaken.] Besides, I think the scribe erred in writing the name of Aquila, from whose genius and character the interpretation which the Syriac seems to have assigned to him is utterly foreign." Both MSS. have not ܒܝܫܐ, but ܒܛܠܐ[?]. For my part I should believe that for ܐ. ܒܛܠܐ[?] we must read ܕܠܐ ܒܛܠܐ[?], from which results the faulty anonymous reading: ἄοικοι κ.τ.έ.
