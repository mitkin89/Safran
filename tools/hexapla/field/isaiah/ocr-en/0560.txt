[63] Jerome: "My spirit also, which _shall go forth_ from me, or which, according to the Hebrew, Aq., Sym., and Theod., _surrounds all things_ (for this is what περιειληθήσεται and περιβαλεῖ signify), is the quickener of all." On which passage Vallarsi: "Falsely up to now, instead of these two words, which the older Ambrosian codex depicts thus in uncial letters, ΠΕΡΙΕΙΛΗΘΗΣΕΤΑΙ and ΠΕΡΙΒΑΛΛΕΙ (sic), one only had prevailed, which Erasmus made by guesswork, περιελεύσεται, and in the recent edition of the Hexapla too this is thrust upon us, just as if the three translators had used that same word." Namely Montef., following Drusius, had printed: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. περιελεύσεται; for which Doederlein had arrived by conjecture at περιειλήσεται. Without doubt περιειληθήσεται should be claimed for Aquila, and περιβαλεῖ for Theodotion, comparing the Hex. on Job. xxiii. 9. Psal. cxlii. 4. But to which of these Symmachus should be joined cannot be determined for certain.
