CAP. XI.
[1] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܝܬܝܪܘܬܐ ܀ ܣ. ܡܫܬܒܚܢܘܬܐ ܀ On Aq. cf. Hex. on Job. xxxvii. 4. Jerem. l. 44. On Sym. cf. Hex. on Psal. xlv. 4 (where ἐνδοξασμὸς is in our author ܡܫܬܒܗܪܢܘܬܐ[?]). xlvi. 5. The rarer form ܡܫܬܒܚܢܘܬܐ is used for εὔκλεια in Sap. Salom. viii. 18 in Syro-hex.
[2] Cf. on v. 11. Hieron.: “What the LXX rendered at the end of the chapter: _And I will feed the sheep of slaughter in the land of Canaan,_ is not in the Hebrew, and is rendered by none of the other translators.”
[3] Hieron.: “He took to himself two rods, or two σκυτάλας, that is, _staves,_ which in Hebrew are called MACALOTH.”
[4] Euseb. in Dem. Evang. p. 482. In Sym. either μίαν seems to need deleting, or τὴν μὲν μίαν should be read in the former clause.
[5] The same ibid. p. 483. Cf. Hex. on Jud. xvi. 16. The readings of the three translators, not differing from one another in sense, our Syriac has thus drawn together into one: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. καὶ ὠλιγοψύχησα ἐπ’ αὐτούς (ܘܐܬܥܝܩܬ ܢܦܫܝ ܒܗܘܢ). Cf. our remarks in _Otium Norvic._ p. 74.
[6] Thus Ald., Codd. 68, 87*, and others. Cf. Jon. i. 11, 13 in Cod. Alex. Theodoret. explains the other: Θηριωδῶς μοι προσῆλθον οἱονεὶ βρυχώμενοι, καὶ τὴν ἐμὴν διψῶντες σφαγήν. (Whence perhaps Suidas s. v. should be emended: Ἐπωρυόμενος· φωνῶν. I correct φονῶν, a very common confusion.)
[7] Euseb. ibid. p. 483. On Aquila (whom Hieron. imitated, rendering _varied_) cf. our remarks in Hex. on Amos ix. 13.
