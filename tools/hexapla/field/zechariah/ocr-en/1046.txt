=== Blatt 1046 · S. 1030 · ZACHARIAS. [Cap. XIV. 20, 21.]
--- Fußnoten
[18] Hieron.: “The Hebrew word MESULOTH Aq. and Theod. rendered βυθὸν [מְצוּלָת], that is, _the deep;_ Sym. περίπατον σύσκιον, that is, _a shady walk._ The LXX alone rendered χαλινὸν, that is, _bridle_ . . . And when I asked the Hebrew what it signified, he told me that we ought not to read MESULOTH, but MESALOTH, which signifies _the trappings of horses_, and _warlike adornment;_ and that, except in this place, this word is found in no volume whatever of the Holy Scriptures.”
[19] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܡܬܟܪܟܢܐ[?] ܀ Hieron.: “For _Canaanite_ Aq. rendered _merchant_, whom we too have followed in this place.” Cf. Hex. on Jesai. xxiii. 28. (For μετάβολος, which reading is anonymous in the margin of Cod. 86, our author put ܡܬܟܪܟܢܐ[?] in Jesai. xxiii. 2, 3.)
