[2] The former reading is in Ald., Codd. XI, 29, 52, 64, and others (among them 243, with Ο΄. σκῦλα in the margin); the latter in Comp., Codd. 19, 56, 82, 93, and others.
[3] Cod. 243. Montef. copied from the Regius: Ἀ. εἰς τὰς π. Σ. εἰς τὰ στρ. Cod. 108 cites: Σ. εἰς τὰ στρ. Nobil., Cod. 92 in the margin, and Anon. in Cat. Niceph. T. II, p. 433: Εἰς τὰς κοιλίας, εἰς τὰ στρατόπεδα. Ἑβραῖοι, εἰς τὰ σκῦλα. (Since no one has yet given a probable account of the three readings, εἰς τὰ σκῦλα, εἰς τὰς κοιλίας, εἰς τὰς κοιλάδας, all of which seem to have flowed from a single writing ΕΙΣΤΑΣΚΟΙΛΑ, may we be pardoned for bringing forward a conjecture which, so far as we know, is new. Namely, in the Hebrew archetype the Greek translator found אֶל־מַעַכְרוֹת instead of אֶל־מַעַרְכוֹת, for which, according to the meaning of its root עָכַר, “_he troubled, he cast into calamity_,” he put εἰς τὰ σκῶλα (which is the reading of Cod. 247), i.e. “_upon sharp stakes_” (Hesych.: Σκῶλα· ξύλα ὠξυμμένα). We only add that in the passage Jud. xi. 35, for the Hebrew הָיִית בְּעֹכְרָי, the other edition τῶν Ο΄ reads εἰς σκῶλον ἐγένου ἐν ὀφθαλμοῖς μου.)
