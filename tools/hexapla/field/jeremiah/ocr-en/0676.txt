--- Fußnoten
[47] (Forts.) ܐܬܗܦܟܘ ܡܥ̈ܝ ܕܠܓܘ ܒܓܠܠܗ[?] ◆. On the phrase ܡܥ̈ܝ ܕܠܓܘ cf. our Hex. on Psal. lv. 9. Dan. x. 16. Mich. vi. 64.
[48] Syro-hex. reads and marks: στῆσον σεαυτῇ ※ σκοποὺς (ܕܘ̈ܩܐ), ποίησον σεαυτῇ ↙ τιμωρίαν. After σιὼν Codd. 36, 49, others add σκοπούς. In Codd. 48, 88, others, σιωνὶμ is written for σιὼν, which may be Theodotion’s.
[49] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܠܒܝܬ[?] ܕܘ̈ܩܐ ◆. Hieron.: “The Hebrew word SIONIM is rendered either as _watchmen_ or as _watchtowers_, as Aq. and Sym. translated.”
[50] Theodoret. Pesch. renders ܐܬ̈ܐ.
[51] Thus Cod. 88, Syro-hex., and without the asterisk Codd. 22, 36, others. Montef. cites from Cod. Jes.: Ο΄. ποίησον ※ Ἀ. εμῒ σεαυτὴν, noting: “Perhaps σεμι or σμι σεαυτήν; so that he expressed the Hebrew word in Greek letters, as he not rarely did.” In the Oxford papers it is noted on ποίησον: “Cod. XII in the margin ἐμποίησον. ※ Ἀ. σεαυτή.” Certainly Montfaucon’s invention will please no one.
[52] Montef. in the Hebrew Lexicon to the Hex. Orig. p. 547: “LXX τιμωρίαν, where they seem to have looked to the sound of the Hebrew word rather than to its meaning, which we have noted not infrequently elsewhere.” Spohn has no doubt that τιμρωρὶμ was put by the Elders instead of τιμωρίαν.
[53] Codd. 86, 88. Syro-hex. ܐ. ܡܡܪܪܘܬܐ ◆.
[54] Hieron.
[55] Thus Cod. Jes. in the margin, and in the text without the asterisk Codd. 22, 36, others.
[56] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܗܝ ܕܡܬܟܒܫܐ[?] ◆. For the Hebrew סָלַל and סָלָה the same translator put ἀποσκολοπίζειν Psal. lxvii. 5. cxviii. 118. Jesai. lvii. 14.
[57] Syro-hex. (where it is wrongly referred to τιμωρίαν) ܣ. ܫܒܝܠܐ ◆.
[58] The same: ܐ. ܣ. ܒܐܘܪܚܐ ܗܝ ܕܐܙܠܬܝ ܐܬܦܢܝ ◆. The same cites in the text: ※ ἐπιστράφηθι, παρθένος Ἰσραήλ ↙.
[59] Hieron.: “Where we have said, _How long will you be dissolved in delights,_ Sym. put, _How long will you be sunk into the deep._” Syro-hex. cites: Ἀ. [rather Σ.] καταβιβασθήσῃ εἰς βύθον (ܬܬܛܒܥܝ ܠܥܘܡܩܐ), or rather, in one word, καταβυθισθήσῃ.
[60] Codd. 86, 88. Cf. on Cap. xlix. 4. Syro-hex. continues Aquila (Symmachus): ܗܝ ܕܡܬܗܦܟܐ[?] ◆, i.e. ἀποστρεφομένη.
[61] The obelus is in Syro-hex.
[62] Codd. 86, 88. The redundant words, ἐν τῇ σωτηρίᾳ, are recognized also by Syro-hex., which cites in disordered sequence: ܐ. ܢܩܒܬܐ ܚܕܬܐ ܒܐܪܥܐ. ܒܦܘܪܩܢܐ ܬܬܟܪܟ ܠܓܒܪܐ[?] ◆, i.e. θήλεια καινὸν ἐν τῇ γῇ, ἐν τῇ σωτηρίᾳ περικυκλώσει ἄνδρα. Less probably Olympiodorus in Cat. Ghisler. T. II, p. 686: Ἀκύλας καὶ Σύμμαχος, ὅτι ἔκτισε κύριος καινὸν ἐν τῇ θηλείᾳ, ἐκδεδώκασιν; a reading which S. Athanas. in _Collect. Nov. PP._ T. II, p. 11 ascribes to Aquila alone, against Jerome, who, rendering the Hebrew, _For the Lord has created a new thing upon the earth: a woman shall encompass a man,_ adds: “Thus Sym. and Aq. rendered it in accordance with our edition. But what the common edition [LXX] meant in this place I could say, and find some sense, were it not sacrilege to argue about the words of God by human sense.”
[63] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܒܐܪܥܐ. ܢܩܒܬܐ ܬܬܟܪܟ ܠܕܟܪܐ ܚܕܬܐ ◆. Here too the word ܚܕܬܐ seems, by some accident, to have been transposed.
[64] Hieron. in continuation: “But Theod., himself also agreeing with the common edition, rendered: _The Lord has created a new salvation; in salvation man shall go about;_ putting the singular for the plural.”
[65] Cod. Jes. Thus in the text Codd. 22, 36, others. Syro-hex. marks: κύριος ※ τῶν δ. ↙ ὁ θ. Ἰσραήλ.
