=== Blatt 0629 · S. 613 · –Cap. XV. 18.] JEREMIAS.
--- Fußnoten
[30] (Forts.) should think here of the other edition of Aquila, ἑταιρισθήσεται, we at any rate will not object. Cf. Hex. on Psal. lxiv. 14.
[31] Codd. 86, 88, the former of which reads χαλκὸς. Syro-hex. gives it in two ways: Σ. μὴ κακώσει (ܕܠܡܐ ܡܒܐܫ ◆). Σ. σίδηρος ἐν σ. ἀπὸ β. καὶ χαλκόν (or χαλκός). Jerome renders the Hebrew: "_Shall iron be allied with iron from the north, and brass?_" noting: "Symmachus: _Shall iron harm iron from the north, and brass?_ The LXX and Theodotion: _If iron shall know, and a covering of brass?_ The cause of the variety is plain. For the word JARE . . owing to the ambiguity of pronunciation means both _friendship_ and _malice;_ and if DALETH be read for RES . ., it signifies _knowledge_ and _understanding_."
[32] Codd. 86, 88 continue Aquila: ἰσχύν (ἰσχύς Cod. 88) σου καὶ θ. σου. The same: Ἀ. οὐκ ἐν ἀλλάγματι. Syro-hex. in continuation: ܚܝܠܐ (ἰσχὺν) ܕܝܠܟ ܘܠܣܝܡܬܐ ܕܝܠܟ ܠܒܙܬܐ ܐܬܠ ܠܐ ܒܚܘܠܦܐ ◆.
[33] Codd. 86, 88 continue Symmachus: τὴν ἰσχύν (ἡ ἰσχύς Cod. 88) σου καὶ τοὺς (τοὺς omitted by Cod. 88) θ. σου. The same: Σ. ὑπὲρ οὐδενός. Syro-hex. in continuation: ܚܝܠܐ ܕܝܠܟ ܘܠܣܝܡܬܐ ܕܝܠܟ ܠܚܛܘܦܝܐ ܐܬܠ ܚܠܦ ܘܠܐ ܡܕܡ ◆.
[34] Codd. 86, 88. Cf. on Cap. xvii. 4.
[35] Codd. 86, 88: Ἄλλ. (Ἄλλ. omitted by Cod. 86) ※ κύκλῳ ἐν τοῖς ἐ. σου. The word κύκλῳ is wanting in Codd. 22, 48, others, and Syro-hex., which have ἐν πᾶσι (πᾶσι omitted by Codd. 88, 233, Syro-hex.) τοῖς ἐ. σου.
[36] Cod. Jes., and under the asterisk Cod. 88. Syro-hex. marks: ※ σὺ ἔγνως, κύριε ↙.
[37] Codd. 86, 88: Ἀ. Σ. ἐκδίκησόν με. Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܥܒܕ ܠܝ ܬܒܥܬܐ ◆. Anyone can see that the former reading is Aquila's, the latter Symmachus'.
[38] Cod. Jes., and under the asterisk Syro-hex. Codd. 22, 36, others: μὴ εἰς μ. σου βάλῃς με.
[39] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܐܫܬܟܚܘ ܡ̈ܠܐ ܕܝܠܟ ܘܐܟܠܬ ܐܢܝܢ. ܘܗܘܬ ܡܠܬܐ ܕܝܠܟ ܠܝ ܠܚܕܘܬܐ ◆.
[40] The same: ܣ. ܡܫܬܟܚܝܢ ܗܘܘ ܡ̈ܠܐ ܕܝܠܟ ܬܘܪܣܝܐ ܠܝ. ܘܗܘܬ ܡܠܬܐ ܕܝܠܟ ܠܝ ܠܚܕܘܬܐ ◆. Differently Jerome: "_And I ate them_, that is, they were turned into food for me; or according to Symmachus, _I received them._"
[41] Cod. Jes. Thus under the asterisk Syro-hex., and without the asterisk Codd. 22, 36, others.
[42] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܘܐܫܬܒܗܪܬ ܡܢ ܩܕܡ ܐܝܕܐ ܕܝܠܟ ◆. Cf. Hex. on Psal. xxvii. 7. Jesai. xiii. 3.
