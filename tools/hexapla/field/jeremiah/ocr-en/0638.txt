Jerome: "But I wonder what the LXX meant by putting διάπτωσιν, that is, _ruin_, for THOPHETH, and πολυάνδριον, which means _a multitude of men_, for _valley_; unless perhaps because the people fell there, and a multitude of men was slain, either spiritually, in the error of idolatry, or literally, by the Babylonian army."
