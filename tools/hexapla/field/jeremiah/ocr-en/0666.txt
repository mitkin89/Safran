[24] Cod. Jes. Thus in the text under the asterisk Cod. 88, Syro-hex., and (with φησὶ κύριος, ※ καὶ ἐπιστρέψω, and at the end ἐκεῖθεν) Codd. 22, 36, others. Hieron.: “_And I will be found by you_ (or _I will appear to you_), _says the Lord._ ※ _And I will bring back the captivity——which I sent out from Jerusalem to Babylon_ (v. 20). Up to this point it is not found in the LXX; I have marked it beforehand with asterisks.” Montef. also cites v. 15, which is read in the LXX today, from Cod. Jes. under ※ Θ., against all the books and the Syro-hex.
[25] Cod. Jes. All the others, with Syro-hex., have ἐπὶ πάντα τὸν λαὸν τὸν κατοικοῦντα. Next Codd. 22, 36, others: τοὺς ἀδ. ὑμῶν τοὺς μὴ ἐξελθόντας; Codd. 88, 233, Syro-hex.: οἱ ἀδελφοὶ ὑμῶν οἱ μὴ ἐξελθόντες.
[26] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܠܐ ܡܬܐܟܠܢܝܬܐ ◆ ܣ. ܐܚܪ̈ܝܬܐ ◆. (For τὰ σουαρεὶμ Codd. 36, 49 have τὰ ἄωρα, a reading which Spohn ignorantly claims for Aquila in place of the Syriac ܠܐ ܡܬܐܟܠܢܝܬܐ.) Hieron.: “_And I will make them like bad figs;_ which Theod. rendered _sudrinas;_ the second [edition], _very bad;_ Sym., _the last;_ they are called in Hebrew SUARIM, but through the fault of copyists, in place of the middle syllable, or the letter ALPHA, the Greek DELTA crept in, so that SUDRIM is read instead of SUARIM.” (For _secunda_, which is in the Sangerm. and all the others, Martianay, apparently on his own conjecture, wrote _scilicet._ Hody (_De Bibliorum Textibus_ etc. p. 584) suspects that the passage should be restored thus: _which Theod. rendered_ sudrinas; _the first edition of Aquila_ . . . ., _the second_, pessimas.) On τὰ σουαρεὶμ Chrysost. in Cat. Ghisler. T. II, p. 648 notes: τουτέστι, τὰ σεσηπότα; Theodoret., however: Σουαρὴμ δέ ἐστι τῇ Ἑβραίων φωνῇ, τὰ ἐν ταῖς ὕλαις φυόμενα, τουτέστιν, τὰ ἄγρια, τὰ ἀνεπιμέλητα, τὰ φυτουργίας οὐκ ἀξιούμενα, τὰ εἰς βρῶσιν ἄχρηστα.
[27] Cod. Jes. The reading ἐξαποστέλλω (ἀποστέλλω Cod. 88) is in all the others, which, except Cod. 233, have καὶ τὸν λιμὸν, and οὐ βρωθήσεται. At the end Montef. wrongly copied ὑπὸ πονηρίας from Cod. Jes.
[28] Cod. Jes. For διώξω ὀπ. αὐτῶν Codd. 22, 36, others (not 88, 233) have ἐκδιώξω κατόπισθεν αὐτῶν. Then πάσαις ταῖς β. all, except Cod. 233; and καὶ εἰς ὅρκον all, except Cod. 233 and Syro-hex.
