--- Fußnoten
[30] Codd. 86, 88. In what sense Aquila may have used the noun σκελισμὸς (σκελλισμὸς Cod. 88), unknown to the lexicographers, from σκελίζω, "_to straddle, to trip up_" (see Hex. on Job. viii. 3. Prov. xix. 3), I do not know, at least in a sense that could be reconciled with the Hebrew אֱלִיל, "_that which is of no worth_". In addition, Syro-hex. on οἰωνίσματα gives: ܐ. ܒܕܝܐ ◆ ܣ. ܣܪܝܩܘܬܐ ◆. Norberg renders A. "_delirium_", on the authority of Castell, who gives: "ܒܕܝܐ, _babbling, delirium_. 2 Pet. ii. 3." But in the passage cited, for λόγοι πλαστοὶ the Syrian gave ܡ̈ܠܐ ܕܒܕܝܐ, i.e. "_feigned words_", from ܒܕܐ, "_he feigned, invented_" (e.g. 3 Reg. xii. 33 in the Pesh., where the Hebrew has בָּדָא, the LXX ἐπλάσατο). And since the same translator at Psal. xcv. 5. Jesai. xxxi. 7 put ἐπίπλαστοι and ἐπίπλαστα for the Hebrew אֱלִילִים, why should we not assume that the Syrian here read in his copy: Ἀ. ἐπίπλαστον. Σ. ματαιότητα; so that the reading σκελισμὸς, "_a tripping up, deceit_", would be referred to the other edition of Aquila? Unless it should seem more likely that the words ܒܕܝܐ and σκελισμὸς, in the sense of "_deception_", could be interchanged with each other.
