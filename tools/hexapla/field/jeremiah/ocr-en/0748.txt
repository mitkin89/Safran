[94] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܢܟܕܘܢ[?] ◆. Norberg translates, _let them be weakened_, against the usage of the verb ܟܕܐ[?], which in Pael means _he cut short_, but in Peal is not used except impersonally. "Why should it not be changed to ܢܦܪܘܢ[?], from ܦܪ[?], which in Lev. xi. 21 in the Pesch. is _he leaped, sprang up?_ Sym. translated, I think, as he usually does, by γαυριᾶν, in the interpretation of which verb and of the noun derived from it our translator varies. [For γαυριᾶν and γαυριᾶσθαι our Syrian regularly puts ܐܬܚܬܪ[?] (cf. on Cap. xv. 17), and for γαυρίαμα ܚܬܝܪܘܬܐ[?]. Only once (Hex. on Psal. xcv. 12) is γαυριάσει with him ܬܬܪܘܙ[?].] But in Num. xxiii. 24 γαυρωθήσεται is translated ܢܫܘܪ[?], which stands for σκιρτᾶν Psal. cxiii. 4, 6. Mal. iv. 2, and for διασκιρτᾶν Sap. Salom. xix. 9. The diacritical point would then, I think, come from the form of the verb "ܦܪ[?]."—_Ceriani._
