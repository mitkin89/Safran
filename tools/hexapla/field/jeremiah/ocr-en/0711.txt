Σ. καὶ πορευθέντες. Ἀ. ἐν τοῖς φραγμοῖς Χαμάαμ. Ἀ. Σ. τοῖς ἐχόμενα πρὸς Βηθλεέμ; where at least πρός seems to need deleting. Moreover I have no doubt that Jerome had this passage, not Cap. xlix. 3, in view in the Book De Situ etc. (Opp. T. III, p. 227): “Gebarth, for which Aq. renders _in the hedges;_ but Sym. _in the colonies._” Rosenmüller on the passage: “Aquila in the Syro-hex. codex: _in the enclosures of Chamab;_ as if he had read בִּגְדְרוֹת כָּמוֹהָב. Josephus agrees in part, who (Antiqq. X, 9, 5) has: εἴς τινα τόπον, μάνδραν λεγόμενον. For גְּדֵרָה denotes a _fold_, and at Zeph. ii. 6 it is so rendered by the Alexandrian.” The other Syrian renders: ܒܐܕܖ̈ܐ ܕܟܡܗܡ, _in the threshing-floors of Chemham._
