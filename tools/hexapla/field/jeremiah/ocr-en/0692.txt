[16] Cod. Jes. Syro-hex.: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. τὸν σταθμόν (ܠܣܦܐ[?]). I do not know what Chrysost. means in Cat. Ghisler. T. II, p. 754: τοῦ φυλάσσοντος τὸν σταθμόν· ὁ Ἑβραῖος, τοῦ φ. τὴν ἐσθῆτα τῆς λειτουργίας.
[17] Parsons, on the words ὅς ἐστιν ἐγγὺς κ. τ. ἑ., cites from Codd. 86, 88: Ἄλλος· (ἄλλος omitted by Cod. 86) ἐφεστὼς τῷ πρ. It seems to be a scholion.

[21] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܓܘ̈ܢܐ ◆, with the Greek CΚΥΦΟΥC written beside it. “In my judgment, read ܐܓܘ̈ܢܐ.”—_Norberg._ “I would not change it. For σκύφος our translator renders ܣܩܘܦܘܣ[?] in Aq. at Gen. xliv. 5; ܟܣܐ[?] in the same at Exod. xxv. 33; here ܓܘ̈ܢܐ, so that he seems to have been altogether uncertain how to render it exactly. The word ܐܓܘ̈ܢܐ, which Norberg wanted, is not known even to Bernstein in his larger Lexicon, but only ܐܓܢ̈ܐ, which our translator also often uses, but for κρατὴρ, not for σκύφος.”—_Ceriani._
