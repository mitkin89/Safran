[30] Hieron.: “Where we, following Sym., have put _they are troubled,_ and in the Hebrew is written HOMA, the LXX and Theod. put μαιμάσσει, and what this word means I do not know to this day [Chrysost. in Cat. Ghisler. T. I, p. 396: μαιμάσσει ἡ ψ. μου, τουτέστι, θορυβεῖται ἐν ἀγῶνι τυγχάνουσα. For μαιμάσσει our Syrian put ܡܢܗܡܐ[?], _gnashes,_ examples of which word Michaelis missed in _Lex. Syr._ p. 662]; Aq., however, put ὀχλάζει, which itself also denotes tumult. Hex. on Psal. xli. 6. Prov. vii. 11), but ἠχοῦσι. Cf. Hex. on the very similar passages Jesai. xvi. 11 (where for ἠχήσει Syro-hex. has ܢܪܥܡ[?]), Jerem. xxxi. 20.
