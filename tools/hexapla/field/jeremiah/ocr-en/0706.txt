[22] Syro-hex. here and Cap. xl. 2: ܐ. ܪܝܫ ܢܟܘ̈ܣܐ[?] ܀ The same Cap. lii. 12: ܐ. ܕܢܩܘ̈ܫܐ[?] ܪܝܫ ܀ (Note, however, the difference, that in our passage the index in the text is not over ܪܝܫ, as in Cap. xl. 2. lii. 12, but over ܡܠܦܢܘ̈ܬܐ[?].) With these should be compared the passages 4 Reg. xxv. 8. Dan. ii. 14, where for the same Hebrew our translator claims for Aquila ܪܒܐ ܕܢܟܘ̈ܣܐ, and ܡܠܦܢܐ ܕܢܟܘ̈ܣܐ, i.e. _master_ (or _teacher_) _of the slaughtered_ (not _of slaughterings_, which is Middeldorpf’s error). From all these, however, we have tormented ourselves in vain to extract a probable Greek expression.
