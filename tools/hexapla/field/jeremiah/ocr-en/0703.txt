[68] The former reading is in Comp., Codd. XII, 23, 26, others; the latter in Codd. 22, 36, others. P. L. B. Drach rejects the reading ἠδυνάσθησαν, noting: “Thus also the Complut., which our translator, following Drusius, renders _they prevailed._ But the verb ἀδύναμαι (a word otherwise barbarous: consult Lobeck on Phryn. p. 565) plainly and openly signifies the contrary, _I cannot._” A strange statement! That the forms ἠδυνάσθη and ἠδυνήθη from δύναμαι are used by the LXX without any distinction is known even to beginners.
