Jes., with an asterisk at the second reading." Parsons gives from the same book: ※ οἱ πορευθέντες ἐν σκολιότητι καρδίας αὐτῶν πονηρᾶς, which seem not to differ from the latter fragment of Montfaucon, and which Spohn rightly concludes belong to Aquila, comparing the Hex. on Cap. xi. 8. The former version (to which Θ., not Ἀ. Θ., should be prefixed) is the one read in the text of Syro-hex. under ※. Eccles. vi. 9) ἐν τῇ ἀρεσκείᾳ τῆς κ. αὐτῶν.
