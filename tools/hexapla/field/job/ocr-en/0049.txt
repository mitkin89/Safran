CAP. XX.
[1] Colb.: Ἐκ Θ. οἱ γ΄. According to Montef. the _third_ versicle is: μὴ ταῦτα ἔγνως ἀπὸ τοῦ ἔτι, ἀφ᾿ οὗ ἐτέθη ἄνθρωπος ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς; against the usage of the Colb. annotator. Syro-hex. too places the metobelus after ἔτι, but ascribes the words ἀποκρίνεταί μοι not to Theodotion, but τοῖς Ο΄. The same has for ἀπὸ τοῦ ἔτι ܡܢ ܡܩܕܡ[?], _from the beginning._
[2] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܗܕܐ ܝܕܥ ܐܢܐ ܡܢ ܥܠܡ܀ Montef. gives: Σ. ἀπ᾿ αἰῶνος, as Olymp., Colb., Orat.
[3] Polychron., Colb., one Reg.
[4] The same. For χαρμοσύνη Polychron. has χαρμονὴ.
[5] Colb. Syro-hex.: Οἱ λοιποί· ἔπαρμα (ܡܬܪܘܡܡܢܘܬܐ[?]) αὐτοῦ. The same has in the text ※ εἰς οὐρανὸν ↙.
[6] Colb., Syro-hex.
[7] In placing the asterisks the authorities go different ways. Namely, v. 9 Syro-hex. has under ※ Θ., Jerome and Cod. 248 under ※, against Montef. V. 10 Jerome alone has under ※. V. 11 is under ※ in Syro-hex., Jerome, Cod. 248, and Colb., whom Montef. followed. The whole of v. 12 Jerome, Cod. 248, and Montef. from Colb. have under ※; but Syro-hex. only the latter versicle, ※ κρύψει—γλῶσσαν αὐτοῦ ↙. Finally v. 13, ※ οὐ (οὐ om. Colb., Hieron.) φείσεται αὐτῆς, καὶ οὐκ ἐγκαταλείψει αὐτήν ↙, Cod. 248, and Montef. from Colb.
