--- Fußnoten
[21] (Forts.) the metobelus are read, and which Middeld. rendered by guesswork: “_for being full of words I am now about to bring them forth_” (!), are nothing but a scholium of the Syrian translator: “_To that,_ πλήρης γάρ εἰμι ῥημάτων (v. 18), _join these._” There remains the question to which of the three translators this remarkable fragment should chiefly be assigned. It cannot be Theodotion's, because he, on the testimony of Colbert. and the Syrian, continues after ἐπτοήθησαν: οὐκ ἀπεκρίθησαν ἔτι· ἐπαλαίωσαν ἐξ αὐτῶν λόγους κ.τ.ἑ., which are now read in the version of the LXX. The version is so scrupulous that it could be claimed for Aquila, were it not for the phrase ἀπόκρισιν ποιεῖσθαι for עָנָה, which the usage of that translator would hardly have admitted. There remains therefore Symmachus, to whom the Colbertine expressly assigns the phrase τὸ μέρος μου, and whom Hieron. seems to have followed in rendering vv. 11, 12.
