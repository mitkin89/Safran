=== Blatt 0060 · S. 48 · JOBUS. [Cap. XXVII. 1—
--- Fußnoten
CAP. XXVII.
[1] Syro-hex. in the text has: ※ Θ. ἔτι δὲ προσθεὶς κ. τ. ἑ., but omitting the metobelus which would mark how far the force of the asterisk extends.
[2] Olymp., one Reg. Montef. wrongly printed ἄρας, misled by Drusius. Syro-hex. gives in the margin: Θ. ἆραι (ܠܡܫܩܠ).
[3] Olymp., Colb., Orat., one Reg.
[4] Colb.
[5] Nicet. and “all.”
[6] Colb., one Reg. On v. 7, Ο΄. ἀλλὰ εἴησαν οἱ ἐχθροί μου, Schleusner, in _Opusc. Crit._ p. 163, gives from a MS. Catena: Σ. ἀπόλλυντο (ἀπόλλοιντο) οἱ ἐχθροί μου, which is a scholium of Olymp. in the Cat. of Nicet. p. 419.
[7] Nicet., Colb., one Reg. Cod. 248: Ἀ. καὶ Σ. πλεονεκτεῖ.
[8] Colb.
[9] “Thus all.”—_Montef._ Olymp. has κομίσονται.
[10] Colb., one Reg. Cod. 250 in the margin: ἀθῷοι, ἀναίτιοι.
[11] After ἀράχνη Colb. in the text adds ἃ συνετήρησεν, with Ald. and some books; then in the margin notes: ※ ἐκ τῶν Σ. Syro-hex., however, gives in the text: ※ ἃ ↙ συνετήρησεν. Jerome without the asterisk: “_which he kept._” In place of these words Cod. Alex. and two others have ὁ πλοῦτος αὐτοῦ.
[12] “The Colbertine with asterisks: Ἐκ Θ. οἱ ς΄, that is, _These six verses from Theodotion._ They are likewise marked with asterisks in the Latin Ed. of Jerome, but some asterisks have dropped out.”—_Montef._ Syro-hex. marks with asterisks versicles 1, 2, 3, and 6, these being excepted: ἐκ χειρὸς—χεῖρας αὐτῶν.
[13] Thus Montef. from Colb. It seems to be a scholium on the words that follow, καὶ ἀπελεύσεται.
[14] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܢܪܨܦ ܥܠܝܗܘܢ ܦܣ̈ܬܐ ܕܝܠܗܘܢ. Middeldorpf renders: _he will press together_ ἐπ’ αὐτοὺς τὰ ἴχνη αὐτῶν. (Syr. ܦܣܬܐ stands for ταρσὸς Jesai. i. 6, in a fragment of Aquila, who is wont to use this word for the Hebr. כַּף. The verb ܪܨܦ means _he pressed together, bound tight, trod in, crammed_, on the authority of Castell, who adduced no examples. In Syro-hex. Jud. iii. 23 it is used for the Greek ἐσφήνωσε (τὰς θύρας). Cf. Hex. on Jesai. ii. 6. In our passage Aq. perhaps rendered יִשְׂפֹּק by συγκροτήσει, which word the Syriac seems to have taken in the sense of _cramming_ rather than of _clapping_.)
[15] With the fragment of Aquila the Syriac joins the version of another interpreter, with no space intervening: ܢܛܦܩܘܢ[?] ܥܠܝܗܘܢ ܒܐܝ̈ܕܝܐ ܕܝܠܗܘܢ ܀; i.e. ψοφήσουσιν (Ezech. vi. 11. xxv. 6 in Syro-hex.) ἐπ’ αὐτοὺς ταῖς χερσὶν αὐτῶν. So rightly Middeld.
[16] Colb., one Reg. Thus without a name Codd. 255, 256, and 250 in the margin. Syro-hex. in the text has ܘܢܓܪܘܗܝ[?], καὶ συρεῖ αὐτὸν, which is the false reading of Ald. and three MSS. Jerome too: “_and he shall drag him._”
