Middeldorpf did not venture to express these in Greek; I shall nevertheless append his Latin, badly divided and ill suited to the Syriac. ※ “_A tyrant seized him, not a man. Nor did he set words against me; to your words I would not have opposed him. They used wicked speech. The answer was again snatched from them. I waited so as to say nothing, because they stood by; now they no longer make opposition. I too will take up my part, I too will bring forth my opinion↙. For being full of words I am now about to bring them forth._” He added only: “Concerning the author of this interpretation nothing can be determined.” It is truly surprising that this excellent critic did not notice that his Syriac agrees almost word for word with the Latin of Hieronymus cited by Montef. This is as follows: ※ “_The Lord cast him off, and not man._” ※ “_He has not yet spoken to me:_” ※ “_and in your speeches I will not answer him._” ※ “_They were astonished, they answered no more:_” ※ “_words failed them._” ※ “_Since then I waited, and they did not speak,_” ※ “_and they stood, and answered no more:_” ※ “_I too will answer my part,_” ※ “_and will declare my knowledge._” In the first line the Hebrew אֵל is in Syriac ܫܠܝܛܐ, in Greek ὁ ἰσχυρὸς, which is customary. But the verb ܐܚܕ, _seized_, gives offence, since it cannot fitly be adapted either to the Hebrew נָדַף, _drove away_, or to Hieronymus' _cast off_. I tried ܐܫܕܝ[?], which our Syrian put for ἀπέρριψε Psal. l. 13. lxx. 9, and frequently, were it not that this seems too remote from ܐܚܕ. Therefore I leave it undecided. [Later I was informed that in the MS. ܢܫܕܐ[?], ἀπορρίψει, stands written.] Let us pass to v. 4, where Middeld., joining ܕܒܘܠܐ with the preceding ܡܩܒܠܬܐ, understood it of _wicked speech_. The Hebrew is חַתּוּ לֹא עָנוּ עוֹד, which Hieron. expressed clearly: “_they were astonished, they answered no more;_” nor less well Theodotion: ἐπτοήθησαν, οὐκ ἀπεκρίθησαν ἔτι. In short, for the Syriac ܕܒܘܠܐ ܥܒܕܝܢ ܗܘܘ I confidently correct ܬܘܗܘ ܠܐ ܥܒܕܝܢ ܗܘܘ [thus in the MS.], ἐπτοήθησαν, οὐκ ἐποιοῦντο. ܬܘܗ is used for ἐπτοήθη Job. xi. 16. xxiii. 15.
