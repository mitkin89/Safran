--- Fußnoten
[38] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܘܒܡܘܚܐ ܕܓܪ̈ܡܐ ܕܝܠܗ ܢܫܩܐ܀ The version of Symmachus is the one which Montef. brought forward from Colb. and Orat., and before him Nicet. p. 369: ἀποθνήσκει, τῷ μυελῷ τῶν ὀστέων ἀρδόμενος, κατὰ Σύμμαχον. Accordingly Middeld. renders the Syriac: Ἀ. Σ. καὶ τῷ μ. τῶν ὀ. αὐτοῦ ἀρδόμενος. More accurate, unless we are mistaken, is the rendering which we have claimed for Aquila. The Syriac, however, contracted the two versions, as being not dissimilar in sense, into one.
