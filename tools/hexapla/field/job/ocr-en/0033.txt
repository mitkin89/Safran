CAP. XI.
[1] Nobil., Nicet. and “all.”
[2] Syro-hex. in the margin: ἢ οὐκ ἔστιν ὁ ἀνταποκρινόμενός (ܗܘ ܕܡܦܢܐ) σοι. The reading ἀνταποκρινόμενος is in Comp. and six MSS.
[3] Thus Codd. 137, 255. For ἐντρέπων Montef. printed ἀνατρέπων, whether by his own error or that of his Reg. is uncertain.
[4] Syro-hex. in the margin gives: Ε΄. ※ γεννηθήσομαι (ܐܬܝܠܕ), translating a faulty copy. Jerome: “_and without crime_ ※ _I was before him_ :” (more correctly, ※ _I was_ : _before him_): from which Montef. gave: ※ ἐναντίον αὐτοῦ.
[5] Schol. in Nobil. Cod. 248 has the same in the margin.
[6] These words, which today are read in all the copies τῶν Ο΄, Syro-hex. and Jerome have under the asterisk.
[7] “Ἀ. ἀπορρήτους σοφίας, if only the words are intact.”—_Nobil._ “Σ. ἀπορρήτου σοφίας. Thus one Reg.”—_Montef._ Syro-hex. gives: Σ. ἀπόρρητον (ܠܐ ܡܬܡܠܠܢܐ). Finally Comp. has ἀπόρρητα σοφίας, which we would gladly claim for Aquila as well as for Symmachus.
[8] Schol. in Nobil. Thus Cod. 161, and Cod. 248 in the margin.
[9] One Reg.
[10] After οὐρανὸς Cod. Alex. inserts: γῆ δὲ βαθεῖα. Thus Syro-hex. in the margin ܐܪܥܐ ܕܝܢ ܥܡܝܩܐ ܀.
[11] “Ἄλλ. μετεωρότητας οὐρανοῦ τίς κατεργάσῃ; Drusius [Nobil.], who gives this as a scholium. But it is in truth that of some interpreter.”—_Montef._ Without doubt it is Aquila's, who rendered גֹּבַהּ by μετεωρότης Ezech. i. 18, according to Cod. 88. For the rest, we have written τί for τίς following the emendation of Kreyssig. See Schleusner, _Opusc. Crit._ p. 155.
[12] Nobil. Drusius renders: “_doubts, how thou mayest reach them;_” Montef.: “_difficulties, how thou mayest approach them;_” both taking ἀπορήσεις as a noun.
