[11] First let us see what the Hexapla before us offer. Montef. printed: בצורות. Ο΄. δίνας (ἄλλ. θῖνας). Θ. ὀχυρώματα. יארים. Σ. ῥεῖθρα. Ο΄. ποταμῶν. He also notes: “Θ. ὀχυρώματα. Thus two Regii, Orat. and Drusius. Nicetas: Θ. ὀχυρώματα. Σ. ῥεῖθρα; wrongly. For that version of Symmachus belongs to the following word, as is indicated in the same MSS., and as the matter itself shows.” Namely, Nicetas testifies: ἀντὶ δὲ τοῦ θῖνας, Θ. μὲν ὀχυρώματα, Σ. δὲ ῥεῖθρα ἐξέδωκαν. Cod. 161, 255, 259 claim ὀχυρώματα for Theodotion. Another, Cod. 250, has in the margin: ὀχυρώματα, ῥεῖθρα. Our Syriac gives what we have represented in our text, partly in Greek, partly in Latin, to the best of our ability. The Syriac is: ܐ. ܒܐܛܝܡ̈ܬܐ ܬܪ̈ܥܐ ܨܕܩܘ ܀ ܣ. ܬܪ̈ܥܐ ܡܬܐܠܨܢ̈ܐ ܕܢܗܪ̈ܘܬܐ ܨܕܩܘ ܀ ܬ. ܚܘܣ̈ܢܐ ܀ As to the individual words, Syr. ܐܛܡ is in Castell _he stopped up, obstructed, shut,_ whence ܐܛܝܡܐ, _covered, closed._ The adverb ܐܛܝܡܐܝܬ is interchanged with the Gr. στιβαρῶς Habac. ii. 6. And since Aquila ten times renders the Hebr. צוּר by στερεὸς, it is not improbable that in our passage he wrote ἐν στιβαροῖς or ἐν στερεοῖς, although στερεὸς in other passages is interchanged with Syr. ܥܫܝܢܐ[?], and στιβαρὸς once (Ezech. iii. 6) with ܡܥܫܢ[?]. But we determine nothing. Symmachus rendered the word בַּצּוּרוֹת (as if from בָּצַר, _he restrained_) by ܬܪ̈ܥܐ ܡܬܐܠܨܢ̈ܐ, i.e. τὰ ῥεῖθρα τὰ συνεχόμενα (or στενοχωρούμενα); from which it appears that Montfaucon, following in the footsteps of Drusius, undeservedly referred Symmachus' ῥεῖθρα to יְאֹרִים, which that interpreter expressed by τῶν ποταμῶν.
[12] Thus Montef. without a note. The reading αὐτοῦ for μου is in Cod. Alex. and Jerome.
