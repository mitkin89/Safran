=== Blatt 0301 · S. 189 · LEVITICUS. –CAP. XIII. 30.]
--- Fußnoten
[15] Codd. Lips., 85, 130 (in the margin without name). Cod. 54 in the text: ἡ σημ. τὸ ἔκφυμα.
[16] Cod. Lips. Cod. X in the margin without name: ἐπιδόσει ἐπιδῷ. πλατύνουσα πλατυνθῇ. Nobil. cites confusedly: "A. and S.: ἐπιδόσει (not, as Montef., ἐπιδώσῃ) ἐπιδῷ τὸ ἐμφύσημα." Corruptly Codd. 85, 130: Ἀ. ἐπιδόσει (-σῃ 85) ἐπίδομα; from which, however, Montef. thought he had derived the _true_ reading of Aquila: Ἀ. ἐπιδώσῃ ἐπίδομα. The anonymous reading, πλατύνουσα πλ. ἡ λεπὶς, is without doubt from the Samaritan version, which in Hebrew characters has thus: ואם פתה תפתה קלפתה, that is, _if in spreading the scale_ (of the scab) _shall have spread itself_. Cf. on v. 8.
