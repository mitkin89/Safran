=== Blatt 0309 · S. 197 · –CAP. XVIII. 25.] LEVITICUS.
--- Fußnoten
[5] (Forts.) F, cf. Hex. ad Jerem. xxvii. 19.) Cod. 85 in the margin has only: ἀνὴρ ἀνὴρ πρὸς πᾶν λίμμα; Cod. 130 in the margin: πρὸς πᾶν λίμμα. "I conjecture that it is Aquila's, not only because, as Montef. points out, Cod. Basil. at v. 17 expressly names Aquila [but see on v. 17], but also because of the manner of translating peculiar to this interpreter … But that what Codd. Coislin. and Lips. add to these words, σύνεγγυς γένους αὐτοῦ, _near his kin_, was added by some uncertain author for the sake of explanation, and is to be referred not so much to the version of Aquila as to that of the LXX interpreters, πρὸς πάντα οἰκεῖα σαρκὸς αὐτοῦ, I am led to believe by an unpublished scholium of S. Cyril on Cap. xx. 20 in Cat. Niceph. p. 1098: σάρκα λέγων τὴν ἐγγὺς κατὰ γένος."—_Scharfenb._ For σύνεγγυς I wonder whether τὸν ἐγγὺς should be read, according to the Complutensian reading, πρὸς πάντα οἰκεῖον σ. α.

[18] Hesych.: "Because of which it is wicked that this woman, being such, should be subjected to lust. _To have intercourse_, then, Symmachus rendered _to submit_ [perhaps ὑποπεσεῖν. S. Cyril. in Cat. Niceph. p. 1088: Ποιμένος δὴ οὖν ἀρετὴ, τὸ μὴ ἐπαφιέναι προβάτοις ἑτεροειδές τι ζῶον, ὡς ταῖς ὀθνείου τυχὸν ὀχείαις ὑποπεσεῖν. Cf. on Cap. xix. 19]."
