CAP. XVII.
[1] Nobil.: "Other copies do not have the words ἢ—ἐν ὑμῖν." So Comp., Codd. V, XI, 16, 19, and others (among them 85, 130, which have them only in the margin), Arab. 1, 2, Arm. 1.
