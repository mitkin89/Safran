[24] Hesych.: "_Blear-eyed, and having a white speck in the eye_ … But _suffusion_, which Aq. rendered, since it is not evident to all, but only to learned physicians, is rightly taken as a swelling; for this affliction is not recognized by all, but only by those who know how to distinguish what pertains to the mind." For _suffusion_ Montef. conjectures ὑπόχυσις, but what we have edited is offered by Cod. 58 in the margin (?) for ἔφηλος.

Cod. 130 in the margin (on ἢ πτίλλος τ. ὀ.): Ἀ. λεύκωμα; and so without name Codd. 54, 58, 85 (on ἔφηλος). Besides, on ἔφηλος Cod. 56 in the margin: ἔχων λεύκωμα; and Cod. 128 in the margin: λεύκωμα ἐν τοῖς ὀφθαλμοῖς. I wonder whether Symmachus rendered: ἢ λεύκωμα ἔχων ἐν τοῖς ὀφθαλμοῖς, and Hieron., as usual, imitated him, rendering: "_and having a white speck in the eye._"
