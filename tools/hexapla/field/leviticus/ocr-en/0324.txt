[43] Codd. X, Lips., in the margin: ἐνιαυτὸς ἡμερῶν τῇ πρ. αὐτῆς, ἡμερόδεκτον ἔσται ἡ λ. αὐτῆς. So in the text (with ἡμερόδεκτος) Codd. 54, 75; and also (with ἡμερόλεκτος) Codd. 74, 76, 84, 106, 134. A shortened, and perhaps mutilated, reading, ἐνιαυτὸς ἡμερόδεκτον ἔσται ἡ λ. αὐτῆς, is in Cod. 58, with which Cod. IV agrees, except for ἡμερόλεγδον for ἡμερόδεκτον. For my part I would believe that these words, τῇ πρ. αὐτῆς ἡμερόδεκτον (or –δεκτος, or –λεκτος, or –λεγδον), the edition of the Seniores being defective, were taken by Origen under the asterisk from another interpreter, perhaps Symmachus. Of the various spellings of the last word, ἡμερόλεγδον, _counting day by day_, seemed preferable, since it alone of all is Greek, and rests on the authority of the very ancient and very accurate Codex Sarravianus.
