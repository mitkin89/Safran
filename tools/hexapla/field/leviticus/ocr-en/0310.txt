Niceph. p. 1084: Θ. ἀπέβαλεν ἡ γῆ τοὺς ἐνοικοῦντας. Σ. ἐξήμεσεν. Ἀ. ἐξῆρεν ἡ γῆ τοὺς ἐν. From these it clearly appears that the rarer but sound word ἐξήρασεν (from ἐξερᾶν, _to vomit forth_, whence ἐξέραμα, _vomit_, 2 Pet. ii. 22) is to be claimed for Aquila; and for Theodotion, who translates less closely to the Hebrew, ἀπέβαλεν (Hesych.: "Not because it could expel them from itself, or _vomit them forth_, or, as Theod. rendered, _spit them out_"); not, as Codd. X, Lips. give them, with the interpreters' names interchanged: Ἀ. ἀπέβαλεν. Σ. ἐξήρασεν. Θ. ἐξήμεσεν. Cf. on Cap. xx. 22.
