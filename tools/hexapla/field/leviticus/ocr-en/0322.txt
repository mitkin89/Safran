VII in the text: ἄνθρωπος ἄνθρωπος; but in the margin by the second hand: ἀνὴρ ἀνὴρ] _when he shall have blasphemed his God, and shall have taken iniquity upon himself, and naming the name of the Lord, let him die the death._ He did not say _naming_, but _denominating_, in order to show that this man, of whom there is now question, at the same time blasphemes and names." For ἐπονομάζων cf. Hex. ad Gen. xxx. 28. For the rest, instead of these words, καὶ λήψεται ἀνομίαν αὐτῷ, which agree with the Latin of Hesychius, Aquila's usage all but demands: καὶ ἀρεῖ (or ἄρῃ) ἁμαρτίαν αὐτοῦ.
