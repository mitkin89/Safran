[29] Codd. 85 (on the testimony of Holmes.), 130, in the margin without name: ζεστὴν πεπλατυμμένην. Somewhat differently Codd. X (without Σ.), Lips., in the margin: Σ. πεπλατυσμένην ζεστοτέραν. The former reading Montef., from Cod. Basil. (?), claims for Aquila, both here and at Cap. vii. 12; that the latter is Symmachus's is expressly attested by Hesych., who has as follows: "_Which is fried in a pan, sprinkled with oil_ . . . And he added also _kneaded_, which the LXX rendered thus: _Kneaded_ [πεφυραμένην] _he shall offer it in morsels_ [ἐρικτά? Then the author passed over the words θυσίαν ἐκ κλασμάτων] _a sacrifice of sweet savour to the Lord;_ calling the same kneaded savour _morsels_. And they are understood to have said _morsels_, as proceeding in manifold ways on account of the sufferings coming about part by part; but _kneaded_, as gathered into one, whence for _morsels_ [rather for _kneaded_] Sym. rendered _spread out_." For מֻרְבֶּכֶת the Graeco-Ven. translator gave ἐζεσμένην.
