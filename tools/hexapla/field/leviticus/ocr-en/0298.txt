[3] To הַשָּׁפָן τὸν δασύποδα is commonly fitted, contrary to the usage of the Seniores, for whom this animal is constantly χοιρογρύλλιον. Cf. Deut. xiv. 7. Psal. ciii. 18 (where see Hex.). Prov. xxx. 26. The fact is that in the text of Ed. Rom. vv. 5, 6 are transposed, which in Comp., Codd. IV, 108, 128, Arab, 1, 2, are read according to the Hebrew order.
