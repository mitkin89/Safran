CAP. XXVII.
[1] Hesych.: "On account of which Aq. and Theod., for what was said, _shall have made a vow_, rendered [add from the MS.: _if he shall have made something wonderful;_] but Sym., _if he shall have surpassed_." Codd. X, Lips., 85 (with -σει), 130, in the margin: θαυμαστώσῃ. Cf. on Cap. xxii. 21. "I suspect that Symmachus's reading is corrupted, and perhaps _separaverit_ (_shall have separated_) would be better read for _superaverit_ (_shall have surpassed_)."—_Montef._ The emendation is very easy, but in no way to be admitted. See Hex. ad Psal. cxxxi. 1. cxxxviii. 6.
[2] So in the margin Codd. X, Lips. The word τάξασθαι seems to belong to another version, ἐν τῷ τάξασθαί σε, perhaps Aquila's. Cf. Hex. ad Job. xiii. 18. Psal. v. 4. Jesai. xl. 18.
[3] Cod. X in the margin.
[4] So Codd. IV, 58, 75.
[5] Hesych.: "Now he speaks of one who has sinned much, and therefore Aq. rendered, for _poor_, _if he has exceeded_." Cf. on Cap. xxv. 35, where Hesych. translated the Greek reading ἐκπέσῃ by _exciderit_, as Montef. supposes he wrote here too. But in our passage _excessit_ or _excesserit_ (_excess'it_) seems to fit his context better. For my part I would believe that he had the word ἐκπέσῃ before his eyes, and attributed to it the meaning, fairly frequent among later writers, _of exceeding measure and bounds_. So S. Chrysost. Opp. T. I, p. 160 C: εἰς μείζονα κακίαν ἐξέπιπτε. T. VIII, p. 487 C: εἰς θηριωδίαν ἐκπίπτων. T. IX, p. 721 C: τουτέστιν, ἵνα μὴ ἐκπέσωμεν. T. X, p. 57 A: ποῦ οὐκ ἂν ἐξέπεσε;
