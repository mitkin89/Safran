Montef. edited: Ἀ. μυηθέντας, noting: “Basil. has ἀμυηθέντας [ἀμυνθ.], wrongly, as is clear, for Ἀ. μυηθέντας.” On the contrary, Aquila, to be consistent with himself, would without doubt have put τοὺς ζευγισθέντας.

[9] Nobil. (with πυρίνιον), Codd. X, Lips., 57 (as Nobil.), 73 (the same), 85 (with τέγος without article), 130 (the same). Cod. 58 in the margin without name: τὸ πόρνιον. Cod. 108: Σ. εἰς τὸ πορνεῖον. Procop. in Octat. p. 423: “For Καμινεῖον (sic) Aq. explained as _roof_, but Sym. as _brothel_ [more correctly _lupanar_, as Hieron.].” The spelling πυρίνιον, which to Schleusner in _Nov. Thes._ s. v. means _brothel_, as it were a _fire-place_, where men burn with the fire of lust, I should believe to be a mere σφάλμα of the scribes. But πυρεῖον, which Montef. affirms is found in “some codices”, is found in none of ours. (For κάμινον Cod. 54 by correction has σκηνήν; Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand: εἰς τὴν τένδαν.)
[10] Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand. The same in the right margin, with the reading erased: διὰ τ. . . αἰδοίου. Graeco-Ven.: πρὸς τῇ κύστει ταύτης. For the rest, I know not what human slip befell Gesenius τῷ πάνυ in _Thes. Ling. Hebr._ p. 909, who affirms that Aquila, Symmachus, and Onkelos interpret קֹבָה in this place as being the same as קֻבָּה, _chamber._
