[11] (Forts.) hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܕܡܠܚܐ܀ Hieron.: “But this very thing too which is said, _as a heap of the threshing-floor_ . . . we think the LXX rendered ἁλὸς, that is, _of salt;_ but that by unskilled persons, who thought of a θιμωνίαν, that is, a _heap,_ of corn or of produce, there was put for ἁλὸς, by adding the two letters ω̄ and ν̄, as if in keeping with the produce, ἅλωνος, that is, _of the threshing-floor._”

CAP. III.
[1] Cod. 86. Hieron.: “_Woe, provoking city!_ which in Hebrew is said more expressively MARA, that is, παραπικραίνουσα.”
[2] Cod. 86.
[3] Hieron.: “_Wolves of Arabia,_ or _of the evening,_ as Sym. rendered.”
[4] The same: “Her prophets _raving,_ or _astounded,_ which in Hebrew is called PHOEZIM, and Aq. rendered θαμβευταί.” (Formerly θαμταὶ or θαμπταὶ was printed, for which the Cod. Sangerm. offers CAAΛBRUTAI (sic), from which Montef. ingeniously extracted ΘΑΜΒЄΥΤΑΙ, appealing to Gen. xlix. 3, where for פַּחַז Aq. put ἐθάμβευσας.)
[5] Thus under asterisk Cod. 238. Cod. 23 marks: ※ εἰς φῶς ↙, καὶ οὐκ κ. τ. ἑ. These words are absent from Codd. III, XII, 26, 49, and others, and Syro-hex., which has in the margin: ※ܒܢܘܗܪܐ ܘܠܐ ܐܬܛܫܝ. ܘܠܐ ܝܕܥܘ[?] (ἔγνωσαν) ܥܘܠܐ ܒܬܒܥܬܐ܀
[6] Thus Syro-hex., the obelus, as it seems, being placed unsuitably. Perhaps one should mark: ÷ ἐν διαφθορᾷ ↙ κατέσπασα κ. τ. ἑ.; for these words, ἐν διαφθορᾷ, are wanting in Ald., Codd. 87*, 91, and others, and Hieron.
[7] Hieron.: “Where we rendered, _In the day of my resurrection in the future,_ and all translated, _for a testimony,_ the Hebrew who instructed me in the Scriptures asserted that LAED in the present place ought rather to be understood as εἰς ἔτι, that is, _in the future._”
[8] Cod. 86, on the testimony of Parsons.
[9] Thus Comp., Ald., Codd. 22, 23, 36, and others, Hieron., Syro-hex.
[10] Theodoret. (Thus Cod. 97 from the same, with the spelling μεταναστρέψω.) Hieron.: “Where we rendered, _I will restore to the peoples a chosen lip,_ for _chosen_ the LXX said _unto its generation,_ so that _of the earth_ is to be understood. And hence the error arose, that the Hebrew word BARURA, which Aq. and Theod. rendered _chosen,_ Sym. _pure,_ the LXX read as BADURA.”
