[9] Euseb. in the Onomasticon, p. 284: Μάψαρ Τύρου. Ἀκ. ἅρμα Τύρου. Σύμ. ὀχύρωμα Τύρου; or, in Jerome's rendering: "Mabsar of Tyre: for which Aq. and Sym. render _fortified Tyre_." Montef., however, adduces from his Regius: Ἀ. Σ. φρᾶγμα (sic) Τύρου, whence he conjectures that in Eusebius φράγμα should be restored for ἅρμα, with the approval of Wesseling in _Probabil._ p. 217. To us, indeed, the reading of that Regius is highly suspect; both because the word φράγμα nowhere occurs in the Greek Bible, and because we have hit upon a far easier emendation of the Eusebian reading, namely δίαρμα, which we have claimed for Aquila for the same Hebrew in Hex. on Jesai. xxxiv. 13. The same translator also put διηρμέναι for בְּצוּרוֹת at Deut. iii. 5. Jesai. xxxvi. 1 (in the _Auctarium_).
