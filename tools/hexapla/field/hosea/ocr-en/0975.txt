[16] Hieron.: “In the place where we and the LXX rendered, _I will protect thee, Israel_, in the Hebrew is written AMAGGENACH, which Aq. translated ὅπλῳ κυκλώσω σε, that is, _I will surround thee with a shield._ While we thought this was to be understood in a good sense and to signify protection, the edition of Symmachus suggests to us the contrary sense, as he says ἐκδώσω σε, that is, _I will deliver thee up._ Also from the translation of Theodotion not prosperity but adversity is shown, ἀφοπλίσω σε, which means _I will strip thee_, and take from thee the ὅπλον, that is, the _shield_ with which I had previously protected thee.”
