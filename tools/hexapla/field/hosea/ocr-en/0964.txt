On Sym., for ὁρμήματός μου, which seems to have been wrongly taken over from the LXX, we would gladly read τῆς ὀργῆς μου, following Hieron. and Syro-hex.; the latter of whom attests: Σ. Θ. τὴν ὀργήν (ܠܚܡܬܐ[?]); the former: "For _wrath_ the LXX translated ὅρμημα, that is, _onset_ . . . But _wrath_ is more correctly to be understood, as all except the LXX translated." Next, Fischer in _Clavis Verss. GG. V.T._ p. 82 affirms that for ὑδροκελίαν one must write ὑδροκηλίαν, as if from ὑδροκήλη, which among the sons of the physicians is _a watery swelling of the bodies enveloping the scrotum._ If there were room for conjecture, we would try: (ἐπ᾽ αὐτοὺς ἐκχεῶ) τὴν ὑδρίαν (or ὑδροχόην) τῆς ὀργῆς μου. Cf. Apoc. xvi. 1.
