[11] Hieron.: "In the place where we have put _spiders' webs_, in the Hebrew is written SABABIM . . . not, as some wrongly think, SABABUM; which the LXX and Theod. rendered πλανῶν, that is, _seducing_ and _deceiving;_ Aq. _to the wandering_, or _the turned back;_ Sym. _inconstant_, or _unstable_, that is, ἀκαταστατῶν; the Fifth ed. ῥεμβεύων, _roving_ and _wavering._ We have learned from a Hebrew that SABABIM properly denotes the _threads of spiders_ flying through the air, which perish while they are seen, and dissolve into atoms and into nothing."
