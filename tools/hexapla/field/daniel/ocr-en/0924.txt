=== Blatt 0924 · S. [908] · [DANIEL]
--- Fußnoten
CAP. I.
[1] Cod. 87: εἰς Βαβυλῶνα εἰς γῆν Σεναὰρ, against Syro-hex., which has only ܠܒܒܠ. On Σενναὰρ Nobil. cites: Schol. Βαβυλῶνος; whence Montef.: Ἄλλος· εἰς γῆν Βαβυλῶνος.
[2] The former reading is in Ald., Codd. III, 34, 62; the latter in Codd. 42, 49, 232.
[3] Syro-hex. In Cod. 87, the asterisk being absent, the metobelus is placed both after αὐτοῦ and after σκεύη.
[4] Cod. 87. Syro-hex. ܠܐܒܝܣܕܪܝ[?]. Montef. cites from Reg. bombyc.: Ἄλλος· Ἀβδιεζδρὶ διδασκάλῳ εὐνούχων αὐτοῦ. Hieron.: “For _Asphanez_ I have found Ἀβριεσδρὶ [Cod. Vat. ABHICΔPI; Sangerm. _Abiesri_] written in the common edition.”
[5] Reg. bombyc. Syro-hex. in the margin without a name: ܡܠܦܢܐ[?] ܕܡܗ̈ܝܡܢܐ ܕܝܠܗ ⁘. “The readings which occur without the name of a translator are mostly Theodotion's, as will be clear to one who compares them. This one, however, since it follows the Hebrew text closely, I judge to be Aquila's.”—_Bugatus._ Cf. on Cap. ii. 14.
[6] Cod. 87, on the testimony of De Mag. Syro-hex. ܘܡܢ ܦܪܬܘܡܝܢ[?] ܓܒ̈ܝܐ. Cod. 88 cites: καὶ ἐκ τῶν ἐπιλέκτων καὶ ἐκ τῶν ἐκλεκτῶν.
[7] Hieron.: “For φορθομμὶν, which Theod. put, the LXX and Aq. translated _chosen men_, Sym. _Parthians_, taking the word as the name of a people; which we, following the edition of the Hebrews that is read κατ’ ἀκρίβειαν [i.e. Aquila's second], have rendered _tyrants_, chiefly because _of the royal seed_ precedes.” Nobil. cites: Schol. Πάρθων. Less probably Theodoret.: ὁ δὲ Σ. τὸ πορθομὶν ἐπιλέκτους ἡρμήνευσε.
[8] Reg. bombyc.
[9] Ed. Rom. Codd. III, 106, 233 reject ἐν τῷ οἴκῳ; ἐνώπιον, however (written above the line in Cod. II), is wanting in Codd. 22, 23, 26, and others.
[10] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܒܗܝܟܠܐ ⁘. Cf. Hex. on Psal. xliv. 9.
[11] The same: ܐ. ܟܬܒܐ ⁘. Cf. on v. 17.
[12] Thus Codd. 23, 36, and others, Theodoret.
[13] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܕܠܐ ܢܬܛܡܐ[?] ܒܝܕ ܬܘܪܣܝܐ[?] ܕܡܠܟܐ ⁘. Nobil. cites: Schol. μιανθῇ; and thus Cod. 233 in the margin.
[14] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܗܦܝ̈ܟܐ ⁘. Next, on καταδικάσητε Nobil. cites: Schol. καταδικασθῆναι παρὰ τοῦ βασιλέως ποιήσητε; whence Montef. edited: Ἄλλος· καταδικασθῆναι ποιήσητε.
