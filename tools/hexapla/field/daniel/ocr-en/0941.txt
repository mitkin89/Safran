(In the LXX version in Cod. 87 ἐλεηνὸς is written three times, which De Magistris wrongly rendered _having obtained mercy_, as if ἐλεηνὸς meant the same as ἠλεημένος, whereas nobody is unaware that it is a mere scribal error for ἐλεεινὸς, _pitiable_. Thus Tertull. adv. Psych. p. 985, and Hieron. adv. Jovin. Opp. T. II, p. 349: “_Daniel, thou art a pitiable man;_” which passages De Magistris himself pointed out.)
