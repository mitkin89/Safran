[17] The reading of Theodotion is the printed one, καὶ ἕως οὗ—αἰχμαλωσίαν; in its place a double version, καὶ ἕως ἄρχοντος τῆς δ. ἡδρύνθη, ἕως ὁ ἀρχ. ῥύσηται τὴν αἰχμ., is exhibited by Codd. 22, 36, 48, and others, Theodoret. It is established that the latter is τῶν Ο΄; the former, as it seems, is the genuine hand of Theodotion, and Chrysost. recognizes it alone. (Next, in the LXX, for εὐωδώθησαν the Syriac renders εὐωδώθη.)
[18] In v. 11, according to Theodotion, after ἐταράχθη Ald., Codd. III, XII, 23, 33, and others, and Chrysost. in his Comment. on the place add καὶ ἐγενήθη; but Theodoret. and Chrysost. in another place (Opp. T. I, p. 639 C), with Codd. 22, 34, 36, and others, have: ἐταράχθη παραπτώματι. καὶ ἐγενήθη κ. τ. ἑ. The reading καὶ ἐγενήθη seems to have crept in from the LXX text: but that the word παραπτώματι (not, as Montef., ἐν π.) belongs to the Hebr. בְּפֶשַׁע in v. 12, and is of some other translator, I know not whom, Drusius probably supposes.
