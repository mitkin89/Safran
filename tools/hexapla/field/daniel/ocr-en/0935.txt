[1] Syro-hex. In Cod. 87 these words, καὶ ἀπὸ τῶν μ. αὐτοῦ, have dropped out by the easiest slip of all. (In the summary of vv. 1–5, Βαλτάσαρ—ἔσταται, which is prefixed to the version τῶν Ο΄ both in Cod. 87 and in Syro-hex., for ἐν τῷ τόπῳ αὐτοῦ Tischendorf edited ἐν τῷ πότῳ αὐτοῦ from Segaar's emendation, against our Syriac. On this confusion cf. Jacobs. on Ach. Tat. p. 720.)
[2] Hieron.: “_But each_ of the princes who had been invited _drank according to his age:_ or as the other translators rendered: _The king_ himself _drank wine before all the princes whom he had invited;_” giving not the words of the others, but only the sense.
