CAP. VI.
[1] Hieron.: “For _princes_, as Sym. translated, Theod. rendered τακτικοὺς, Aq. συνεκτικοὺς. And when I asked who these _princes_, τακτικοὶ, or συνεκτικοὶ, were, I read it more clearly in the edition of the LXX, who said [v. 4]: _and two men whom he appointed with him, and a hundred and twenty satraps._”
[2] Thus Codd. 22 (with κατέναντι τοῦ βασιλέως erased at the end), 34 (with κατ. τοῦ β.), 36, and others. The reading savours of Aquila, if for τακτικοὺς you read συνεκτικοὺς with Codd. 23 (where συνεκτοὺς), 62, 147, in which, however, ὑπερνικῶν seems to be wanting.
[3] Thus Codd. 23, 62, 147. Perhaps it is Aquila's. Montef., after Drusius, ungrammatically edited: Ἄλλος· προσεδοκᾶτο καταστῆσαι αὐτόν.
[4] The former reading is in Codd. 22, 34, and others; the latter, which is perhaps Aquila's, in Codd. 23, 62, 147. Theodoret.: ἐκ τῆς β., ἢ ὥς τινα τῶν ἀντιγράφων ἔχει, ἐκ πλαγίων τῆς β.
[5] Hieron.: “For _suspicion_ Theod. and Aq. rendered ἀμβλάκημα [thus Codd. II (by the first hand), III, and others], which in Chaldee is called ESSAITHA. And when I asked a Hebrew what it meant, he answered that the force of the word means δέλεαρ, which we can call _enticement_, or σφάλμα, that is, _error_.”
[6] Thus Cod. 23, and without the asterisk Cod. 147.
