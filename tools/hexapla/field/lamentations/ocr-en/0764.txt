[27] The same: ܣ. ܡܛܠ[?] ܗܕܐ ܀ Montef. cites from Cod. Jes.: C οἱ Γ. χ̅ο̅ς ἀνάστατον. The same in the _Praelimin._ p. 69: "If that reading χ̅ο̅ς stands, I am driven εἰς ἀμηχανίαν: but I think that here χ̅ο̅ς is to be read for χ̅ῶ̅ς, and that by this word χωρὶς is signified, and so the sense will be that Symmachus, who alone in Lamentations had been placed by Origen in the Hexapla together with the LXX, . . . has ἀνάστατον, and that the three translators _separately_, and outside the Hexapla, namely Aq. and Sym. and Theod., exhibit the same reading." Whatever be decided about these sigla (for Montfaucon's explanation will please no one), there can be no doubt that Symmachus put ἀνάστατος for לְנִידָה, chiefly because of Gen. iv. 16, where for נוֹד, Ναΐδ, our Syrian together with the Greek witnesses gives: Σ. ἀνάστατος (ܡܒܕܪܐ[?], as ܬܒܕܪ[?] for ἀναστατώσει Dan. vii. 23).
