[21] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܐܦܩ ܒܦܣܦܣܐ. ܘܐܬܪ ܚܨܦܐ ܫܢܝ[?] ܕܝܠܝ. ܓܒܠܢܝ[?] ܒܩܛܡܐ ܀ "Sym. gave the second part well; in the first a double version occurs, nor . . . do I see how, even with the great freedom he allowed himself in translating, he rendered בֶּחָצָץ _potsherds._"—_Ceriani._ Unless perhaps our Syrian found in his Greek copy: Σ. ἐξέβαλε ψ. καὶ ἐξωστράκισε τοὺς ὀδόντας μου; where ἐξωστράκισε, _he cast out with potsherds_, means almost the same as ἐξέβαλε ψήφοις, and is to be regarded as a mere amplification.
