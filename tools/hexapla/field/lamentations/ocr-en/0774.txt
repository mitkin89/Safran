[2] The same in the margin in characters of medium size: ܨܒܥܐ ܣܘܡܩܐ ◆. "In the Syriac there are two indices referring to the margin, one after ܡܫܬܚܠܦ, ἀλλοιωθήσεται, and it is an index of addition; the other over ܟܣܦܐ, both of which are placed in the margin over ܨܒܥܐ."—_Ceriani._ On βάμμα cf. Hex. ad Psal. xliv. 10. Dan. x. 5. The reason for the epithet should perhaps be sought in the usage of later Hebrew, according to which כֶּתֶם is used only of a _blood stain_ (_Blutfleck_).
