=== Blatt 1015 · S. 999 · –Cap. VII. 20.] MICHAS.
--- Fußnoten
[26] (Forts.) I could not but banish the words ܠܐ [before ܕܕܝܢܘܬܐ] and ܡܚܣܢܬܐ twice [after ܣܚܘܪܬܐ]. The word ܕܕܝܢܘܬܐ is moreover a stumbling-block; for Aquila is accustomed to render the Hebr. חֹק by ἀκρίβασμα and ἀκριβασμὸς.”—_Middeld._ The expression ܣܚܘܪܬܐ ܡܚܣܢܬܐ seems in no way to need tampering with, although the Greek word of which it is a periphrasis is not altogether certain. If one must choose between περιοχή and πολιορκία, we should prefer the latter, since πολιορκεῖν τινα is in Syriac ܣܚܪ ܡܚܣܢܬܐ ܥܠܘܗܝ[?] 4 Reg. xvi. 4. xvii. 4; and πολιορκία is ܣܚܘܪܬܐ ܕܥܠ ܡܚܣܢܬܐ in Hex. on Psal. lxv. 11. As regards ܠܐ ܕܕܝܢܘܬܐ, following Bernstein, we have put in Greek ἀκρισία, disregarding the Hebrew, which means something quite different. Middeld. has rightly defined Aquila's usage; it may be, however, that in this place, contrary to his custom, he put not ἀκριβασμὸς (חֹק) but ἀκρίβεια (חֻקָּה), from which, by the omission of a very small stroke, AKPIBCIA results, which a corrector would readily exchange for ἀκρισία.
[27] Syro-hex. ܬ. ܡܘܪܟ ܦܘܩܕܢܐ ܝܘܡܐ ܗܘ. ܘܡܕܝܢܬܐ ܕܝܠܟ ܢܐܬܝܢ ܡܢ ܐܬܘܪ. ܘܡܢ ܡܕܝܢܬܐ ܕܣܚܘܪܬܐ. ܘܡܢ ܣܚܘܪܬܐ ܥܕܡܐ ܠܡܝܐ ܕܢܗܪܐ ܘܕܝܡܐ. ܘܡܢ ܛܘܪܐ ܠܛܘܪܐ ܀[?] For ܣܚܘܪܬܐ Middeld., who assigned περιοχὴν to Aquila, put συγκλεισμὸν, which is so rendered in the Syro-hexaplar text. But you may see ܣܚܘܪܬܐ interchanged with περιοχὴ in Ezech. iv. 2. xii. 13. Jerem. xix. 9, and often; and in this way the three words of Jerome are amicably distributed among the three translators.
