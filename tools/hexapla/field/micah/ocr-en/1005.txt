[20] Hieron.: “Further, what follows: _She that dwells in Sennam has not gone forth_, which is interpreted _going out_, or as Sym. rendered: _The abundant habitation has not gone forth_, is said of the same Samaria.” Somewhat differently Syro-hex.: Σ. ἡ κατοικία εὐθηνοῦσα (ܗܝ ܕܡܬܒܣܡܐ[?] ܡܥܡܪܬܐ). (In the passage of S. Cyril, εὐθηνοῦσαν must be restored for εὐσθενοῦσαν even against the books, as is clear from the holy Father's own explanation, in which it was formerly printed: εὐσθενῆ τε ὅτι πίων ἡ χώρα, καὶ λῃΐοις κατάκομος, πῶς ἔστιν ἀμφιβαλεῖν. For εὐσθενῆ τε one book has the solecism εὐσθενεῖται δέ; another, however, εὐθυνεῖται δὲ, slightly corrupted from εὐθηνεῖται δὲ, a verb in which both forms, active and middle, are sanctioned by usage. Add that _the fertility of the region_ and _abundance of crops_ belong to εὐθηνίαν, not to εὐσθένειαν.)
