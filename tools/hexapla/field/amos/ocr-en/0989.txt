But if, as it is in the Hebrew, according to Aq. τὴν ὁμιλίαν [Nobil. cites: Ἀ. τίς ἡ ὁμιλία] αὐτοῦ; according to Sym. τὸ φώνημα αὐτοῦ; according to Theod. τὸν λόγον αὐτοῦ; according to the Fifth ed. τὴν ἀδολεσχίαν αὐτοῦ, all of which mean _what his speech is_, it will be written with these letters: MEM, HE, which is pronounced MA, that is, _what_ or _which;_ then SIN, IOD, HETH, which we read SIA, that is, _speech_."
