Somewhat differently, but less probably, Hieron.: "And _ye shall be cast out_, he says, _into the places of Armenia_, which are called _Armona_. Finally Sym. rendered thus: _And ye shall be cast out into Armenia;_ for which the LXX translated _mount Remman_, Aq. _mount Armona_, Theod. _mount Mona_, and the Fifth ed. _a high mountain_." (Moreover, Montef., without naming his source, cites: Ἀ. εἰς Ἀρμανὰ ὄρος ἀπάγοντες, which reading he suspects to be corrupt. In fact it is not a hexaplar reading at all, but a mere fragment of the context of Theodoret on the words γυμναὶ γυνὴ καὶ ἀνὴρ κ. τ. ἑ., which he explains thus: πάσης περιβολῆς ἐστερημένους ἄνδρας καὶ γυναῖκας κατὰ ταυτὸν ἄγοντες, καὶ εἰς τὸ Ἀρμανὰ ὄρος ἀπάγοντες.)
