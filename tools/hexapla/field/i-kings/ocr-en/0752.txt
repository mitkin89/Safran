The former version should perhaps be claimed for Symmachus, with which Hieron. agrees: "_This is thy judgment, which thou thyself hast decreed._" (On ἔτεμες of our version cf. Hex. on Dan. ix. 26, where for the Hebrew מִלְחָמָה נֶחֱרֶצֶת the Greek Hexapla give: Ἀ. Σ. πόλεμος τέτμηται; but the Syriac: ܩܪܒܐ ܦܣܝܩ.
