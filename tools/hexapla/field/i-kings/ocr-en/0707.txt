=== Blatt 0707 · S. 595 · -Cap. II. 16.] III REGUM.
--- Fußnoten
[1] (Forts.) Comp., Ald., Codd. XI (with ἀνετ.), 19, 44, 52, 55, and others (among them Reg., 243). Syro-hex. in the text: καὶ ἀπεκρίνατο λέγων τῷ Σ. υἱῷ αὐτοῦ.
[2] So Comp., Codd. 19, 82, 93, 108, 243 (in the margin), Theodoret. Quaest. III in 3 Reg. p. 456.
[3] The former reading is in Ald., Codd. XI, 64, 71, and others; the latter in Codd. 19, 82, 93, 108, Theodoret.
[4] Cod. 243 (not, as Montef., ἔσο εἰς ἄνδρα). Cf. Hex. on Deut. xii. 23.
[5] So in the text Cod. 119. Syro-hex. in the margin: ◆ ܕܝܐܬܩܐ[?].
[6] So Codd. 19 (with τὴν ὁ. κυρίου, καὶ τὰ πρ. αὐτοῦ, καὶ τὰ κρ. αὐτοῦ, καὶ τὰς ἐντ. αὐτοῦ, καὶ τὰ κρ. αὐτοῦ, καθὰ γ.), 82 (omitting ἀκρ. αὐτοῦ καὶ τὰ κρ. αὐτοῦ, with καθὼς for καθὰ), 93, 108 (omitting ἀκρ. αὐτοῦ), 243 (in the margin, with καὶ τὰ ἀκρ. αὐτοῦ), Theodoret. (with καθώς).
[7] Syro-hex. in the text: ܠܡܛܪ ܬܘܩܢܐ[?] ܕܝܠܗ ܘܦܘܩܕܢܐ ܕܝܠܗ ܘܕܝܢܐ ※ ܣ. ܬ. ܘܣܗܕܘܬܐ ܕܝܠܗ ↙. ܗܢܘܢ[?] ܕܟܬܝܒܝܢ[?]. Over ܘܕܝܢܐ there is an index mark, but the marginal note is missing. At τὰ γεγραμμένα there is written in the margin ܐܝܟ, i.e. κατὰ (τὰ γεγρ.). In the text of the Ed. Rom., after τὰ κρίματα, Comp., Ald., Codd. III, 74, 106, and others (among them 243) insert καὶ τὰ μαρτύρια αὐτοῦ.
[8] So Syro-hex.
[9] So Codd. 19 (with εὐοδώσει), 82 (with ποιήσεις), 93 (with πάντα ποιήσῃς), 108 (with εὐωδώσῃ), 243 (in the margin, with ποιήσεις), Theodoret. (with ποιήσεις).
[10] So Comp., Codd. 19 (with πανταχοῦ and ἐπιβλέψεις), 82 (with ἐπιβλέψεις), 93, 108, 243 (in the margin), Theodoret.
[11] So Comp., Ald., Codd. III, 19, 52, 74, and others (among them 243), Syro-hex., Arm. 1.
[12] So Comp. (with τῇ ψ.), Ald., Codd. III, XI, 19 (as Comp.), 52, 55, and others (among them 243), Syro-hex.
[13] So Comp., Codd. 19, 82, 93, 108.
[14] Syro-hex. in the margin: ܠܐ ܢܬܦܣܩ ܠܟ. Cf. Hex. on Psal. xxxvi. 38.
[15] So Comp., Codd. 19, 82, 93, 108. For ἔταξε Syro-hex. renders rather freely ܚܫܒ, ἐλογίσατο, as Masius also notes in _Syrorum Pecul._ p. 19.
[16] So Syro-hex. These words are read in the Ed. Rom., but are wanting in Codd. II, XI, 55, 56, 64, 71, 158, 245.
[17] So Comp., Codd. 19, 82, 93, 108. Cf. Hex. on 2 Reg. xx. 22.

[19] So Codd. 19, 82 (with οὗτως), 93, 108.
[20] Syro-hex. ܥ. ܣ. ܠܡܚܢܝܡ. Cf. Hex. on 2 Reg. xvii. 24.
[21] So in the text Syro-hex., and without marks Comp., Codd. III, 74, 82, 92, and others.
