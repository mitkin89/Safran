Next, on Σεπφώρα Montef. cites from Regius 1871 (=Holmes. 64): Ἄλλος· ὀρνίθιον; and on Φουά: Ἄλλος· ἐρυθρόν; readings which he supposes to be Symmachus's, whereas they are mere etymological glosses taken from Philo Judaeus. On Φουὰ Cod. 64 notes in the margin: ἐρυθρ . .; Cod. X, however: ἐρυθρὸν ἢ ἐμφανές. On Σεπφώρα Cod. 64 has a scholium, not at this place, but at Cap. ii. 21: ὀρνίθιο . . . . λονή (perhaps ὀρνίθιον ἢ καλλονή; so that the former word refers to צִפֹּרָה (ii. 21), the latter to שִׁפְרָה (i. 15)).
