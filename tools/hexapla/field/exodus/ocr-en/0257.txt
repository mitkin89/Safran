Without doubt either the word ἀρχὴ has crept in from the version of the LXX, so that τῶν πρωτογεννημάτων is to be compared with the Hebrew בִּכּוּרֵי alone; or, which pleases me more, the whole reading belongs to רֵאשִׁית בִּכּוּרֵי (v. 26).
