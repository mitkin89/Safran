=== Blatt 0241 · S. 129 · —Cap. XXVII. 20.] EXODUS.
--- Fußnoten
CAP. XXVII.
[1] Cod. X. Cod. 108: Σ. Θ. κοσκήνωμα (sic). Syro-hex. cites: ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܐܝܟ ܕܟܪܒܠܐ, i.e. ὡς κοσκίνου, or, if you read ܐܝܟ ܟܪܒܠܐ, ὡς κόσκινον; indicating, as it seems, that by κοσκίνωμα is to be understood not so much a _sieve_ as _something like a sieve_. (On ἐσχάραν Montef. cites a scholion from the MS. Catenae: εἶδος καυκίου [cf. Hex. ad Gen. xliv. 2], ἐν αἷς [Niceph. ᾧ] ἀνέματτον σὺν τῷ ἐλαίῳ τὴν σεμίδαλιν, κ. τ. ἑ.; which Anon. in Cat. Niceph. p. 860 expressly refers to τὸν καλυπτῆρα αὐτοῦ (v. 3).)
[2] Cod. X in the margin without name.
[3] Syro-hex. (with the index wrongly attached to the latter ἀναφορεῖς (v. 6)): ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܡܨܝܕܬܐ ܀
[4] So Cod. 15, Arab. 1, 2, Arm. 1, Syro-hex.
[5] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܥܩ̈ܩܐ[?]. Cf. Neh. iv. 17 in the LXX. On ἀναφορεῖς Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand: ἀναβαστάζοντας.
[6] So Codd. 30, 129, 131 (which marks in the margin against ἐν τῷ: ὥστε). A double version, as it seems, is in Codd. 14, 16, 25, and others. Cod. 25 has in the margin against ἐν τῷ: ἕως, from which Montef. extracted a reading that is scarcely Greek: Ἄλλος· ἕως αἴρειν αὐτό.
[7] So Ald., Codd. VII, X, 14, 15, 16, and others, Syro-hex. Presently at v. 10, on עַמֻּדָיו, Ο΄. οἱ στῦλοι αὐτῶν, Montef., following Drusius, adduces from Theodoret: Οἱ λοιποί· σανίδες. But Scharfenb. in _Animadv._ p. 72 has excellently perceived that the passage of Theodoret belongs not here but to Cap. xxvi. 15.
[8] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܘܕܒܩ̈ܐ (ܘܕܒ̈ܩܐ) ܀ Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand: προσκολλήματα. Cf. on Cap. xxxviii. 10, 17. (On αἱ ψαλίδες (ܦܩ̈ܠܝܣ[?]) Syro-hex. cites a scholion: “Αἱ ψαλίδες are tongs (ܡܠܩ̈ܛܐ[?]) of bronze or iron or silver etc., which cut into and hold one thing in another.”)
