=== Blatt 0205 · S. 93 · –CAP. VIII. 14 (10).] EXODUS.
--- Fußnoten
[2] Syro-hex. in the margin, with a scholium: “These too are found (ܩܐܡܝܢ[?]) only in the edition of the Samaritans.” Arab. 1 has the whole passage in the margin; Arab. 2, however, in the text only: “_And M. and A. did_ (wrongly for _went in_) _to Pharaoh._”
[3] Syro-hex.
[4] In Cod. VII a second hand writes ῥεῖθρα above the word διώρυγας; another hand adds in the margin at διώρυγας: διανοίξεις γῆς; and at ἕλη: οἱ σύνδενδροι καὶ δίϋγροι τόποι [ἢ λίμναι added later].
[5] So Syro-hex., and without asterisk Ald., Codd. VII (in the margin by a second hand in uncial characters), 15, 18, and others.
[6] Syro-hex. in the margin, with a scholium: “These too are placed only in the edition of the Samaritans.”
[7] So Syro-hex., and without marks Codd. 15, 58, 72, Arab. 1, 2, Arm. 1. (The Syriac marks: ※ ܥܒ. ܕܝܠܗ ↙ ܣ. ܬ. ܐܝܕܐ[?], by mistake, as it seems, for ※ ܥܒ. ܣ. ܬ. ܕܝܠܗ ↙ ܐܝܕܐ[?].)
[8] The words τῶν Αἰγυπτίων are wanting in Codd. X (but they are in the margin by the first hand), 18, 59, 76, Syro-hex. Montef. published from Reg. 1871 (=Holmes. 64): Ο΄. οἱ ἐπαοιδοὶ ÷ τῶν Αἰγ. ἐν ταῖς ἐπαοιδίαις (sic): καὶ ἐν ταῖς φ. αὐτῶν. But Holmes's amanuensis copied from the same: καὶ οἱ ἐπαοιδοὶ ταῖς φ. αὐτῶν; and in the margin after ἐπαοιδοὶ, perhaps with the index ÷: τῶν Αἰγυπτίων ἐν ταῖς ἐπαοιδίαις (read ἐπαοιδαῖς) καὶ ἐν. Then, at φαρμακίαις (or φαρμακείαις), Cod. X writes in the margin: ἐπαοιδαῖς, and so in the text Ald. (with ἐν ταῖς ἐπ.), Codd. III (the same), 71, 83.
[9] Cod. 85, on the testimony of Montef. Cf. on Cap. vii. 11.
[10] Cod. X in the margin without name, and so in the text Cod. 30.
[11] So Comp., Ald., Codd. III, VII, X, 14, 15, 16, and others, Arab. 1, 2, Arm. 1, Syro-hex.
[12] The obelus is in Syro-hex., which wrongly marks: ÷ καὶ ἀπὸ—οἰκιῶν ὑμῶν ↙. The same notes in the margin: Ὁμοίως καὶ ἡ τῶν Σαμαρειτῶν, ※ καὶ ἀπὸ τῶν θεραπόντων σου, καὶ ἀπὸ τοῦ λαοῦ σου (↙), ἀκολούθως (ܢܩܝܦܐܝܬ) κεῖται.
[13] So Syro-hex., and without asterisk Cod. 15, Arab. 1, 2, Arm. 1.
[14] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܐܝܟ ܦܝܦܝ ܐܠܗܐ ܕܝܠܢ. (In Syro-hex. vv. 10, 11 are enclosed between two asterisks, with a marginal note: “From the cross (ܨܠܝܒܐ[?]), not, as elsewhere, ܐܣܛܪܝܣܩܘܣ or ܟܘܟܒܐ) to the cross, in the Hebrew (copies?), obeli are placed over them, because they are wanting in the edition of the Samaritans.” Ceriani has treated this rather obscure notation at length on the passage.)
[15] Cod. 85, on the testimony of Montef. Holmes's amanuensis copied it without name. Cod. 64 in the margin: . . ἔχωσαν (sic). Cf. Hex. on Psal. xxxviii. 8.
[16] “The Basle codex [and also Cod. 108] ascribes the reading κόρους κόρους to Aquila alone; the Chronicon Alex. p. 71, however, to Aquila and Symmachus.”—_Montef._ Holmes's amanuensis copied from Cod. 85: “θιμωνίας θιμωνίας. But in the margin: κόρους κόρους;” and from Cod. 64: . . ους κόρους. Besides, on συνήγαγον Cod. 57 has in the margin: συνέχωσαν κόρους. κόρους ἀνάπνευσις, ἄνεσις (sic). Cf. Hex. on Ezech. xlv. 13. Hos. iii. 2.
[17] Cod. X. On θειμωνίας (sic) in the first place Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand: σωρούς.
[18] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܩܘܛܢܐ ܩܘܛܢܐ܀ ܬ. ܐܝܟ ܫܒܥܝܢ. The meaning of the word ܩܘܛܢܐ[?] is unknown.
