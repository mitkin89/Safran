=== Blatt 0266 · S. 154 · EXODUS. [CAP. XXXVII. 12–
--- Fußnoten
[26] ※ Θ. ἐκ ξύλων ἀσήπτων—τὴν τράπεζαν ↙. So Syro-hex., and Cod. V, on the testimony of Montef., who however puts the metobelus (:) after ὑπὸ τὴν στεφάνην, continuing: καὶ ἐγένοντο οἱ δακτύλιοι ※ εἰς θήκας κ. τ. ἑ., without metobelus. On ἐποίησε (v. 11) Holmes's amanuensis notes: “From ἐ. . in ἐποίησε the rest is wanting in Cod. V up to Cap. xxxvi, to the end of the eighth verse [=xxxix. 1 in the Hebrew].” By _eighth_ the learned man meant, unless I am mistaken, _twelfth_, which verse has the same clause, καθὰ συνέταξε κύριος τῷ Μ., as v. 8; for the first readings copied by him are: “συμπεπορπημένους, agrees. καὶ ἐκκεκολαμμένους, wanting.” Therefore a large part of the codex, as described by Grabe in the Preface prefixed to the last chapters of Exodus, and copied by Montefalcon in the Hexapla (from p. 98, col. 1, line 11 to p. 100, col. 1, line 13 from the end), has either perished, which is quite incredible, or by some chance or other was passed over by Holmes's collator.
[27] So Ald., Codd. V, X (in the margin), 14, 16, 25, and others (among them 58), Arab. 1, 2 (on the testimony of Holmes), Arm. 1 (on the testimony of Alter). Syro-hex. ܡܩܛܦܢܐ[?], _snuffing vessels_, a word which our translator interchanges with ὑπόθεμα in Exod. xx. 37. “From the sense of the words it seems probable to me that the Syrian read ἐνθέμια, ἐνθέμιον, which are also confirmed by the authority of the older codices II, III, VII, and perhaps also V, which indeed reads otherwise in the Hexapla and in Grabe, but Holmes is silent, from which it seems to read as the Ed. Rom., which sides with those codices.”—_Ceriani._ Nevertheless, from Holmes's silence, for the reason stated above, nothing whatever can be gathered about the reading of Cod. V.

[30] So Syro-hex., Arab. 1, 2, Arm. 1. In Cod. 58 ἓξ is wanting.
