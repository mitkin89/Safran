[32] So Syro-hex., and without marks Codd. X, 14, 16, 18, and others, Arab. 1, 2, Arm. 1. These and the following words, vv. 23–28, Montef. copied from Cod. Basilianus, in which he expressly testifies that they are marked with asterisks, while Holmes's amanuensis is silent on this point. He also carelessly printed ἐπὶ τῶν ἄκρων (without δύο) in v. 23. Further, in the Bodleian collation of the Codex Coislinianus, which, as we stated in the Monitum to Genesis p. 5, Montefalcon had at hand when compiling his appendix, we found at this place of Exodus the little note: “Here the text agrees with the verses marked with asterisks in our edition;” from which one might conclude that those slips were copied out of the codex by Montefalcon with his own hand. Finally, besides the Basilian readings Montefalcon added under each verse the Complutensian text, which, as it is fabricated from the parallel passage Cap. xxxvi. 24–29 (Hebr. xxxix. 16–21), we have deliberately disregarded.
