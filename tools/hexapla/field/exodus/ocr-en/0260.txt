[31] These words, struck through with an obelus by Syro-hex., are wanting in Cod. 58. In the passage Num. xxxi. 50 the LXX rendered עָגִיל וְכוּמָז by περιδέξιον καὶ ἐμπλόκιον, an order which Codd. 19, 30, 75, 108, 118 also present here. (On ἐμπλόκια Cod. 56 in the margin: τραχηλικά; and on περιδέξια: βραχιόλια (βραχιάλια? Cf. Hex. ad 2 Reg. i. 10. Jesai. iii. 20). For כּוּמָז, here and at Num. l. c., Graeco-Ven. rendered νυμφόδεσμον and μητροπόρπην, without doubt from the tradition of the Talmudists, who teach: כומז זה דפוס (_figure_) של בית הרחם. See Buxtorf. in _Lex. Chald. et Talmud._ p. 1050.)

III, 19, 58, 82, 108, 118, Arab. 1, 2. After βύσσος, however, Cod. 58 inserts: καὶ τρίχες αἴγιαι (sic); Cod. 72: καὶ αἶγες; Arab. 1, 2: _and skins of kids;_ Arm. 1 (under ※): _and skins of goats._ Lastly these words, καὶ δέρματα κριῶν—ἤνεγκαν, are had thus by Comp., Codd. 29, 58, 71, 72 (without ἤνεγκαν), and others, Arab. 1, 2, Arm. 1.
