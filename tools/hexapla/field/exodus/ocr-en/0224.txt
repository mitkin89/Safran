=== Blatt 0224 · S. 112 · EXODUS. [CAP. XVIII. 1–
--- Fußnoten
CAP. XVIII.
[1] Syro-hex. (with ܝܬܪܘ in the text following the Pesh., as usual): ܐ. ܝܬܪܘ. ܣ. ܬ. ܐܝܟ ܫܒܥܝܢ.
[2] Syro-hex. in the text offers the peculiar reading κυρίου (ܕܡܪܝܐ), even adding ΠΙΠΙ; but in the margin: ܘܗܢܘܢ ܓ. ܕܡܕܝܢ. Cf. Ceriani on the passage.
[3] The same: ܐ. ܚܬܢܐ[?]. ܣ. ܚܡܘܗܝ. ܬ. ܐܝܟ ܫܒܥܝܢ. Montef. copied from Cod. 85: Ἀ. νυμφευτής. Σ. πενθερός. Cf. on Cap. iii. 1. On γαμβρὸς Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand: πενθερὸς, and so constantly in this chapter, as also in the text Ald., Codd. 59, 76, and others.
[4] Syro-hex. So without asterisk Codd. 15 (with τῷ Μωσῇ), 58, 72, Arab. 1, 2, Arm. 1.
[5] The same: ܐ. ܬ. ܒܬܪ ܫܘܕܪ̈ܐ[?] ܕܫܠܚܗ̇[?]. ܣ. ܐܝܟ ܫܒܥܝܢ. Montef. published: Ἄλλος· ἐξ ἀποστολῆς αὐτῆς, noting: "So one Regius codex, and Drusius [Nobil.: Schol. ἐξ ἀποστολῆς]; but another wrongly ἐξαποστολὴν." On ἐξαποστολὴ cf. Hex. on Jesai. xxvii. 8.
[6] So Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand (but another hand corrected μετὰ τὰς προῖκας). Cod. 108 in the margin: προῖκα (not, as Holmes., πρωικα). The version, or gloss, relates to that meaning of the Hebrew word which it has at 1 (3) Reg. ix. 16.
[7] Nobil. (with νυμφευτὴ), Cod. Basil. (in which, on the testimony of Montef., οὖν is wanting), and one Regius (which for Aq. wrongly writes Ἰοθὸρ for Ἰεθρὼ). Somewhat differently Cod. 130: Ἀ. καὶ ἦλθεν Ἰεθρὸ νυμφευτὴς Μωυσῆ (not Μωυσέως). (Σ.) ἦλθεν οὖν Ἰοθὸρ ὁ π. Μωυσέως.
[8] Syro-hex. So without asterisk Codd. 15 (with ἐν εἰρήνῃ), 128, Arab. 1, 2. Cf. Hex. on Jud. xviii. 15.
[9] Syro-hex. in the text: ※ ܬ. ܗܘ ↙ ܕܦܪܩܗ ܠܥܡܐ ܡܢ ܐܝܕܐ ܕܡܨܪ̈ܝܐ, where the metobelus is to be moved back to the end of the reading. Arab. 1, 2 (omitting _who_): _who delivered the people out of the hand of the Egyptians._
[10] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܕܐܬܪܘܪܒܘ ܥܠܝܗܘܢ܀ Cod. 108 cites: Ἀ. Σ. ὑπερηφανεύσαντο εἰς αὐτούς. Codd. 85 (on the testimony of Montef.), 130: Οἱ λοιποί· ὑπερηφανεύσαντο. Finally Nobil.: Schol. Ἄλλος· ὑπερηφανεύσαντο.
[11] Syro-hex. So without asterisk Codd. VII (written above by a second hand), 15, 72, 128 (with τοῦτο for τὸ), Arm. 1.
[12] Montef. published: Ἄλλος· τί τὸ ῥ. τοῦτο. Ἄλλος· τίς ὁ λ. οὗτος ὃν, noting: "So the Basle codex [Holmes's amanuensis agreeing], and it does not give the names of the translators. But the first rendering seems to be Aquila's, the second Symmachus's." Montfaucon's conjecture is confirmed by Codd. 57, 130; the former of which cites: Ἀ. τί τὸ ῥ. τοῦτο. Σ. τίς ὁ λ. οὗτος; the latter, as we have published it.
[13] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܘܥܡܐ ܗܢܐ.
[14] Nobil. Cf. Hex. on Ezech. iii. 17. Less probably Cod. X: Ἀ. διαστέλου; and also Cod. 85: Ἀ. διαστέλλου.
[15] Nobil., Codd. 85, 130.
[16] Codd. X, 130. Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܥܘܠܒܢܐ. Nobil. cites: Schol. Ἄλλος· πλεονεξίαν.
[17] So Comp. (in the second place only), Ald., Codd. III, VII, 14, 16, and others, against Syro-hex.
