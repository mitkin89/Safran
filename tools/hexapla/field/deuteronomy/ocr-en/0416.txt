[33] Hieron. in Comment. in Epist. ad Gal. (Opp. T. VII, p. 435): “Before we discuss the meaning and words of the Apostle, it seems right to set out briefly the testimony of Deuteronomy, from which the Apostle also took these words, and to compare it with the other editions. The LXX translators, then, rendered this passage thus: _But if there be in anyone a sin and a judgment of death, and he be put to death,_ (_and_) _ye hang him on a tree, his body shall not remain all night upon the tree, but ye shall surely bury him on that day, for cursed by God is every one that hangeth on a tree; and thou shalt not defile thy land, which the Lord God shall give thee for an inheritance._ Aquila: _And when there shall be in a man a sin unto_ [al. _and_] _a judgment of death, and he shall have been slain, and thou hang him upon a tree, his carcass_ [νεκριμαῖον. Cf. on Cap. xiv. 8] _shall not abide_ [Cod. 108: Ἀ. αὐλισθήσεται] _upon the tree, but thou shalt surely bury him on that day, for he that is hanged is the curse of God; and thou shalt not defile thy ground_ [χθόνα. Cf. Hex. ad Gen. ii. 6. iii. 17. Jesai. vii. 16], _which the Lord thy God shall give thee as an inheritance._” The same a little later: “But in that place where Aq. and Theod. translated alike, saying: _for he that is hanged is the curse of God,_ in the Hebrew it is put thus, CHI CALALATH ELOIM THALUI.” Procop. in Cat. Niceph. p. 1543: Ἀκ. καὶ Θεοδ. ἐξέδωκαν, κατάρα θεοῦ κρεμάμενος. Masius too, on the words ὅτι κεκατηραμένος κ. τ. ἑ.: “Aquila rendered it as it is in the Hebrew.”
[34] Hieron. in continuation: “Symmachus: _But if there be in a man a sin unto a judgment of death, and he shall have been slain, and thou hang him upon a tree, his corpse_ [cf. Hex. ad Jerem. xxvi. 23. xxxvi. 30] _shall not pass the night_ [perhaps νυκτερεύσει, or διανυκτερεύσει, comparing Hex. ad Psal. xc. 1] _upon the tree, but thou shalt bury him_ [al. _it_] _with burial on the very day, for he was hanged because of blasphemy against God; and thou shalt not defile thy land, which the Lord thy God shall give thee for an inheritance._” With Symmachus's explanation, _for he was hanged because of blasphemy against God_, agrees the Chaldee: _for he was hanged because he sinned before the Lord_ (אֲרֵי־עַל־דְּחָב קֳדָם־יְיָ אִצְטְלִיב).
