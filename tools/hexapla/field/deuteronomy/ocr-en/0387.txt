CAP. II.
[1] Cod. X in the margin, on the testimony of Holmes., who notes: "He refers it here, but it should perhaps have been referred to Ἡσαῦ." On the contrary, Σηείρ is interpreted τριχωτός, on the testimony of Euseb. in the Onomasticon, p. 336, from which Montfaucon's little note in the _Append._ on this place can be corrected: "Coislin. in the margin τριχιῶτος from τριχιούς [for so it should have been printed, not Ἀ. τριχιούς]." Presently, on καὶ εὐλαβηθήσονται (וְנִשְׁמַרְתֶּם), Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand: καὶ παραφυλαχθήσεσθε, which savours of Symmachus. Cf. Hex. ad Jon. ii. 8.
[2] Cod. 130 in the margin (with the spelling ἐρρησῆτε (sic), not, as Holmes. carelessly copied, ερρωσθητε). On the former reading, which seems to be Aquila's, cf. Hex. ad Jerem. l. 24; on the latter, perhaps Symmachus's, cf. Hex. ad Prov. xxviii. 25. (In the text there is a metobelus in Arab. 1.)
