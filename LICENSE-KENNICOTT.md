# Digital Kennicott Genesis data

Source: *The Digital Kennicott. A Digital Edition of Benjamin Kennicott's Vetus Testamentum Hebraicum*, edited by Sebastiano Crestani and Alberto Legnaioli, Universities of Turin and Florence. Contributor details are retained in the archived TEI header.

https://www.edition-digitalkennicott.unito.it/exist/apps/dike/api/document/001_gen.xml/

Changes: conversion to offline JSON/JavaScript, source glossary explanations in German, positional comparison to Westminster/OSHB, and UI presentation. Original text, witness sigla, reading groups and source annotations are retained. Safran is independent of the source project.
