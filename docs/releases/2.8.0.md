# Safran 2.8.0 – Peschitta and Syriac research (release candidate)

> **Status: draft / not released.** Features below reflect the current development branch. This document does not assert that a full test suite, platform builds, or installer smoke tests have passed.

## New and expanded

- **Peshitta Old Testament**: all 39 Tanakh books as Syriac reading texts (ETCBC/peshitta, Text-Fabric 0.2), retaining source numbering and word identifiers.
- **Verified source-bound morphology**: analyses from ETCBC/syriac (TF 0.92) linked only where the complete consonantal verse sequence agrees. The source morphology and its uncertainty remain visible; unmatched words are not guessed.
- **SEDRA 5.0 Syriac lexicon**: 35,892 lexical entries, 67,692 gloss records and 64,585 recorded word forms. Dictionary lookup, homonym distinction, roots, etymologies, word cards, and Western/Eastern vocalizations work offline; meanings are not invented or silently translated into German.
- **Peshitta–MT comparison**: reviewed Pentateuch word links; separate alignments for Prophets and Writings. Unlinked words remain unlinked. Reciprocal highlighting and grouped plus/minus presentation preserve verse boundary differences.
- **Text-critical Peshitta witness ('Syr')**: source-aware addition/omission signals across the Tanakh and a **Genesis pilot of 626 classified notes**. The signals are not automatically proof of an alternative Hebrew Vorlage.
- **Syriac reading UI**: bundled Estrangelo Edessa font, dictionary access and annotated-word popups.

## Data coverage and cautions

According to the checked-in `tools/peshitta/coverage.json`:

| Measure | Count |
|---|---:|
| Peshitta books | 39 |
| Source verse records | 23,076 |
| Source words | 308,874 |
| Words with ETCBC morphology attached | 177,344 |
| Words without attached morphology | 131,530 |

Morphology coverage is partial: 24 books have no attached analyses in this build, while other books have gaps. A dictionary lookup must **not** be presented as a verified contextual morphological analysis. Verse-number differences, nonmatching reading streams and ambiguous correspondence stay explicit.

The `ETCBC/peshitta` digital reading text does **not** include the critical apparatus of the printed Vetus Testamentum Syriace. The Genesis pilot's classified notes are editorial work, not a transcription of that apparatus.

## User-visible limits

- Full Syriac AT reading text does not mean full Syriac AT morphology.
- Word-level links and classified readings require scholarly verification where uncertainty is indicated.
- Source glosses are lexical clues, not independent verse translations.
- No new claim of complete semantic/syntactic searching or a fully digitized Peshitta critical apparatus is made.
