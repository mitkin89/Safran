# Safran 2.8.0 — Peshitta and expanded apparatus

This release expands the Syriac reading and comparison tools, adds new apparatus witnesses and editorial reading notes, and makes related dictionary material easier to find.

## Peshitta and Syriac dictionaries

- Peshitta reading texts for all **39 Tanakh books**, with the original word IDs and native verse numbering retained.
- Reviewed Pentateuch word links and separate alignments for Prophets and Writings, including shifted verse boundaries and Daniel 3 expansions. Unlinked words remain explicit; additions and omissions are visible in the interlinear view.
- The **Syr** apparatus distinguishes computed alignment signals from the Genesis pilot's **626 classified editorial notes**. Reconstructed Hebrew readings and uncertain judgments remain labelled.
- Syriac word cards can find comparison forms inside existing dictionary articles, including Levy and Gesenius, with highlighted original text and direct article navigation.
- Latin transliteration displays and the dictionary input-script selector have been removed. The offline SEDRA lexicon and Estrangelo Edessa font remain included.

## Expanded apparatus

- **Hexapla (Genesis):** Field's edition, translator readings, hexaplaric signs, inline footnotes, a synopsis within the LXX text, word analysis and dictionary access. Different readings and different interpretations are distinguished.
- **LXX:** classified editorial readings alongside the original Tov/Kraft material; source citations and uncertainty remain visible. Ketiv/Qere source rows are retained.
- **MA:** comparison of the Aleppo-based MAM reading text with the Leningrad-based Westminster text, with differences anchored to the MT words.
- Clearer apparatus sections, witness-label previews, Kennicott counts and compact reading lines. Samaritan additions and plene spellings are mapped to their corresponding MT positions.

## Statistics and loading

- Find similar chapters using vocabulary and repeated expressions.
- Interlinear data is split into per-book files and loaded when needed. Existing word-card renderings are retained.
- Fixed the apparatus regression test to check original Tov/Kraft citations explicitly rather than assuming they are the first LXX entry.

## Sources and coverage

Peshitta coverage: **23,076 source verse records**, **308,874 words**, and **177,344 words with attached ETCBC morphology**. Twenty-four books have no attached morphology in this build; gaps and textual mismatches receive no invented analysis.

The 35,892-entry SEDRA lexicon, recorded vocalizations and word families are lexical resources, not a newly vocalized Peshitta edition. The digital Peshitta source does not contain the printed *Vetus Testamentum Syriace* critical apparatus. Classified Peshitta and LXX notes include editorial work attributed to Claude (2026); they are not transcriptions of a complete scholarly critical apparatus.

ETCBC and SEDRA datasets retain **CC BY-NC 4.0**. Font, Field, CATSS, PaTraCoSy and other source notices remain in the repository and application; Safran's MIT license does not relicense third-party material.

Downloads for macOS, Windows and Linux are attached below. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/mitkin89/Safran#install) for unsigned-app installation instructions. Cross-platform automated checks and builds are verified for this release; manual clean-install and upgrade acceptance on all three operating systems has not been performed in this release session.
