# Safran 2.7.0 — Apparatus

The textual apparatus is now part of the official release, alongside a shared study sidebar, richer passage statistics and linked translation choices in the word card.

## Apparatus

- **Samaritan Pentateuch / Tal–Florentin:** original notes and readings alongside the Masoretic text, source citations, Hebrew/English context previews, word morphology and explicit differences in verse numbering.
- **Septuagint:** Tov/Kraft CATSS notes and separately labelled reading-text alignment flags. Original source codes, Greek context and reconstructed Hebrew remain distinguishable.
- **Biblical Dead Sea Scrolls:** manuscript readings with sigla, fragment/line citations, morphology and explicit reconstruction or uncertainty. Incomplete manuscript coverage is not treated as an omission.
- **Digital Kennicott:** offline readings, manuscript sigla and source previews for the available books.
- **Mishnah Berakhot pilot:** compare Kaufmann, Parma and Cambridge, with manuscript context, word/group alignment and unresolved positions kept explicit; a separate Tosefta parallel view is available.
- Whole-chapter and verse views, witness selection and direct navigation from securely located MT words to the reading text. Coverage depends on the source and book; this is not a complete critical edition of every text.

## One study sidebar

- Comments, Dictionary, Overview, Outline, Topics, Links, Apparatus, Map and Word Card share one sidebar, pin state and size.
- Back returns to the previous tool and its reading position. Close and Escape dismiss the whole sidebar.
- Labels remain visible until space requires symbols. The dictionary can use full height or a shared view with comments.

## Passage statistics and highlighting

- Compare a verse or chapter with its book or the whole Tanakh; chapter scope is the default.
- Explore unusually frequent and rare words, root families, verb forms, names, repeated word sequences and source coverage. Compare two installed chapters, including chapters from different books.
- Frequency choices highlight the matching words automatically; names highlight together, and individual entries can narrow the selection. Selecting a root, verb form or word sequence marks its occurrences.

## Word cards and ancient texts

- Find other Hebrew lemmas that share a German rendering in the chosen translation, with references and navigation back to the original card.
- Overview and forms table now use the same translation selection, including Back/Forward history.
- Syriac Peshitta texts with morphology, Hebrew consonantal transcription and Pentateuch word alignment. Alignment gaps and uncertain positions remain visible; the alignment is largely automatic.
- Greek word cards can show exact dictionary glosses when available.

## Reading improvements

- A narrower version picker with complete wrapped names, left-aligned labels, clear language groups, morphology/interlinear tags and Westminster first.
- English Samaritan translation grouped under English. Refreshed manuscript and translator portraits, with previews above the version picker.
- Clear column-menu actions for removing one edition, retaining one edition of a language or closing all other editions.
- Fixed overlapping hover hints and the language-dependent Ubuntu hover test.

Downloads for macOS, Windows and Linux are attached to this release. Existing unsigned-app installation steps still apply; see the [installation guide](https://github.com/mitkin89/Safran#install).
