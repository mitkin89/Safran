# Safran 2.6.0

More of the Bible word by word, the Qumran scrolls and ancient inscriptions under the Hebrew text, a word card that compares translations – and the first Talmud tractate with full morphology.

## Interlinear – more translations word by word
- **Philippson, complete Tanakh** (23,206 verses) and **S. R. Hirsch, complete Torah** (from Sefaria, 5,846 verses, with 123 Hebrew-checked transcription corrections).
- **Mendelssohn's German Pentateuch** (Dessau 1815) as its own source, distinct from the Taitsch and the Biur.
- **Raphael Breuer:** Joshua, Judges, Ezra, Nehemiah and the remaining Megillot (Song of Songs, Ecclesiastes, Lamentations).
- **Joseph Breuer:** Ezekiel 1–48 and **Jeremiah** – the Jeremiah translation and commentary are new in Safran.
- **Julius Hirsch:** all twelve Minor Prophets; **Wolf:** I and II Samuel.
- **Biblical Qumran scrolls:** one line per scroll under the Hebrew words, with differences from the Masoretic text marked – spelling, different reading, editors' reconstruction and plus (e.g. „אור“ in Isa 53:11).
- **Pronunciation:** a phonetic transcription of every word of the Tanakh (ETCBC *phono*) as an interlinear line, as its own version and in the word popup (בָּרָא → *bārˈā*).
- Approved corrections in Hirsch, Julius Hirsch (Hosea 9:2–3, Amos 7:15–17, Habakkuk 2:6 restored from print), Wolf, Breuer and Philippson.

## Word card
- **Compare two translations:** „⇄ Vergleichen mit …“ shows how two sources render the word in the same passages – e.g. חֶסֶד: Zunz „Huld“ → S. R. Hirsch „Liebe“ 60×, with the share that agrees and the verses side by side.
- **Qumran scrolls as a source:** what the biblical scrolls read where the Masoretic text has the word – same, different spelling or different reading.
- **Greek and Aramaic word cards:** Liddell & Scott and Pape articles open Greek word cards with forms and distribution; Targum words and Levy's headwords open Aramaic cards with attested forms from Onkelos, Neofiti and Pseudo-Jonathan.
- **Verse previews** mark the word in the Hebrew and in every aligned version (German, Septuagint, Targum, Qumran), and say when the chosen version does not contain the book.

## Talmud
- **Berakhot 2a–10b with morphology:** the William Davidson edition with 10,786 word analyses – Hebrew and Babylonian Aramaic, alternatives, subject clitics and grammatical references – plus an experimental German word alignment (Goldschmidt, Pinner).

## New texts and dictionaries
- **Ancient Hebrew inscriptions:** Mesha Stele, Siloam Inscription, Lachish Letters, Arad Ostraca, Ketef Hinnom Amulets (the priestly blessing), Deir Alla (Balaam), Kuntillet Ajrud and Mesad Hashavyahu – every word with lemma and morphology, linked to the related Bible passages.
- **Liddell & Scott, Greek–English Lexicon** (115,617 articles) and **Pape, Greek–German dictionary** (98,833 articles).
- **Qumran morphology** now covers nearly every word, including words with pronominal suffixes, and the biblical scrolls are clickable too.

## Reading and settings
- **Preview with three versions:** choose the Hebrew, the German (default Zunz) and an additional version (English, Septuagint, a Targum …) for the hover preview.
- **Tidier display panel**, with *Export* moved to the ⋯ menu.
- **Text updates:** changed bundled texts can replace your existing versions after confirmation, with a byte-exact backup – or all at once with *Use all new versions*.
- Windows build fixes (file names and line endings).
