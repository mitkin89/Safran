# Safran 2.7.1 — Syriac word cards

This release adds offline Syriac dictionary tools and word cards, improves dictionary lookup, and makes the study sidebar more consistent. The apparatus introduced in 2.7.0 remains included.

## Syriac and SEDRA

- **Offline SEDRA dictionary:** 35,892 lexical entries, multilingual meanings, source credits and recorded word forms from Beth Mardutho’s SEDRA 5.0.
- **Syriac word cards:** meanings, explicit root families, word origins and a forms overview with recorded Western and Eastern vocalizations. Words with the same spelling remain separate entries.
- **Latin input:** explicit SEDRA IV, CAL and Leiden input modes with a Syriac preview. Original lexical transliterations remain available in word cards. Input mode is retained in history, tabs and saved searches.
- **Estrangelo Edessa:** bundled Syriac font; larger text and expanded source analysis in Peshitta word popups.
- Dictionary previews now appear at the bottom of Syriac word cards. Opening a selected SEDRA entry preserves its identity. The morphology popup also prioritizes a unique recorded-form and grammar match when one is available.

The recorded lexical vocalizations are not a newly vocalized edition of the running Peshitta text. SEDRA data is distributed under CC BY-NC 4.0; source and font notices are included in the application.

## Dictionary lookup and readability

- **Whole headwords by default:** searching שיח no longer also returns שיחור. Use `שיח*` to search for word beginnings. Vowel-insensitive matching and separate homonyms remain available.
- Removed automatic prefix stripping, which could shorten שמיא to יא and produce unrelated results. The first-letter removal button remains an explicit action.
- Larger dictionary previews and expanded articles in word cards, with abbreviation explanations on hover.
- The full dictionary workspace keeps its width independently of saved sidebar geometry.

## Study sidebar

- Word Card follows Comments; Dictionary follows Outline.
- Double-click opens an available word card for the current text source, otherwise the dictionary. Repeated double-clicks no longer close a pinned dictionary.
- Escape dismisses a word popup without also closing the pinned tool behind it.
- Portraits are used in interlinear displays, including the updated Buber/Rosenzweig photograph.
