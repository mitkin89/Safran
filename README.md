Safran is a free desktop app that keeps your texts on your own computer. Pick the books and languages you want from the [Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org) library once, and read, search and annotate them afterwards without an internet connection. On top of that, Safran ships **345 German translations and commentaries** that are hard to find anywhere else, among them Louis Ginzberg's *Legenden der Juden* from his German manuscript, plus **seven dictionaries** with more than 88,000 entries. Safran is an independent project and not affiliated with Sefaria.

## Why Safran?

| | |
| --- | --- |
| 📴 **Works offline** | Texts, links, dictionaries, maps and audio live on your disk. Nothing is sent anywhere. |
| 🇩🇪 **German texts you won't find elsewhere** | Zunz, Philippson, Mendelssohn's Biur, Herxheimer, Hertz, Hirsch, Rashi after Bamberger, the Kautzsch Apocrypha, Midrash Rabbah, More Nevuchim, the Schulchan Aruch (Lederer), Ginzberg's *Legenden der Juden* … ready on first start. |
| 📚 **A dictionary shelf, not a single lookup** | BDB, Gesenius, Jastrow, Levy, Krauss and Hamburger's Real-Encyclopädie, searchable by headword *and* full text, with abbreviations explained on hover and sources that open in the reader. |
| 🔬 **Built for study** | Verse-by-verse alignment, morphology on click, a second reading window, Aleppo Codex, Dead Sea Scrolls, Septuagint, Bible map, outlines and Hebrew audio. |

## Quick start

1. **Download and open** the installer for your system from the [latest release](https://github.com/mitkin89/Safran/releases/latest). The app is not signed with a paid certificate, so the first start needs one extra click, see [FAQ](#faq).
2. **Choose your texts.** Open *Download* in the ⋯ menu, load the catalog once (about 22 MB), tick categories or single books, and pick the languages (Hebrew, English, German). The bundled German texts and the dictionaries are there before you download anything.
3. **Read.** Pick a book, type a passage into the jump field (`2:5`, `Daf 3a`, `gen. 1:1`), press Cmd+K for commentaries, and double-click any word to look it up in the dictionaries.

## Dictionaries

Open the *Wörterbuch* tab, press the 📖 button next to the commentary panel, or double-click a word in any text. Seven dictionaries ship with the app:

| Dictionary | Entries | Extras |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **BDB**, Brown-Driver-Briggs (unabridged) | 10,022 | also by Strong and BDB number; verses linked |
| **Gesenius**, Hebräisches Handwörterbuch (1915) | 10,118 | |
| **Jastrow**, Dictionary of the Targumim, Talmud and Midrash | 32,466 | |
| **Levy**, Wörterbuch über die Talmudim und Midraschim (vols. 1–4) | 19,503 | abbreviations on hover, sources linked |
| **Levy**, Chaldäisches Wörterbuch über die Targumim (vols. 1–2) | 7,232 | abbreviations on hover, sources linked |
| **Krauss**, Griechische und lateinische Lehnwörter (part II) | 6,329 | abbreviations on hover, sources linked, *s. X* cross-references clickable |
| **Hamburger**, Real-Encyclopädie für Bibel und Talmud (vol. 1, vol. 2 talmudic articles) | 2,753 | footnotes inline, sources linked |

- **Smart lookup:** vowel points and final-letter forms are ignored, the prefixes ו ה ב כ ל מ ש are stripped, and entries that start with your word are listed too. Suggestions appear while typing.
- **Full text:** tick *auch im Text* to search inside the articles, not only the headwords. Hits are highlighted.
- **Choose dictionaries:** the 📚 button selects which dictionaries are searched. The choice is remembered.
- **Headword list:** *☰ Stichwörter* opens the complete, filterable headword list of a dictionary.
- **Linked sources:** a citation such as *Rosh Hashanah 18b* opens the passage in the reader, if the text is in your library.
- **Bring your own:** add `.dsl` / `.dsl.dz` dictionaries (ABBYY Lingvo format) under *Wörterbücher verwalten*. Citations marked `{{ref:…}}` become clickable in Safran.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>First start on macOS and Windows: "damaged", "unidentified developer", SmartScreen</b></summary>

The app has no paid developer certificate (Apple's and Microsoft's certificates cost money), so the first start needs one extra step. The app is not broken. On macOS it is signed locally (ad hoc).

**macOS** – if you see *"Safran is damaged and can't be opened"* or *"the developer cannot be verified"*:

1. Drag **Safran** into the **Applications** folder.
2. Easiest: open the `.dmg` again and right-click **Safran freischalten.command** → *Open* → *Open*. Or run in the Terminal: `xattr -cr /Applications/Safran.app`
3. For *"developer cannot be verified"*: *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* (scroll down), or right-click the app → *Open* → *Open*.

**Windows** – SmartScreen may show *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How much disk space do I need?</b></summary>

Only what you choose. Rough figures: all merged Hebrew texts ≈ 2.8 GB, English ≈ 0.5 GB, German translations from Sefaria ≈ 0.13 GB. All JSON texts including every single version ≈ 7 GB. Sefaria's link data ≈ 680 MB (optional). Hebrew audio ≈ 3 GB in total, downloaded per book (optional). The storage location can be moved, for example to an external drive.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it really work offline?</b></summary>

Yes. You need a connection to download texts, links and audio, and for the optional update check. Everything else, including search, dictionaries, maps and the bundled German texts, works without internet.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What about privacy?</b></summary>

Safran has no account, no tracking and no server of its own. It downloads from the public Sefaria export and from GitHub releases, only what you ask for. Your notes, bookmarks, imported texts and dictionaries stay on your computer. Safran never uploads anything to Sefaria.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How is Safran related to Sefaria?</b></summary>

Safran is an independent, open-source project and not affiliated with Sefaria. The Sefaria texts are not part of the app; they are downloaded from the public [Sefaria export](https://github.com/Sefaria/Sefaria-Export) and keep their own licenses. Safran adds its own German texts, dictionaries and tools on top. The Tools menu can also prepare and check CSV files for a Sefaria upload.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which versions of macOS and Windows?</b></summary>

Safran is an Electron app; installers for macOS (`.dmg`) and Windows (installer and portable `.exe`) are attached to each [release](https://github.com/mitkin89/Safran/releases). For Hebrew text, install the free **SBL Hebrew** font from the Society of Biblical Literature; Safran uses it automatically.
</details>

## Contributing

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an [issue](https://github.com/mitkin89/Safran/issues). Please mention your system, the Safran version (⋯ menu) and, for text problems, the book and passage.
- **Texts:** export your own translations or commentaries with ⋯ → *Eigene Texte für GitHub exportieren …* and offer them as a pull request into `bundled/`. Please state the source and license.
- **Code:** [docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) explains running from source (`npm install`, `npm start`), building and tests. Interface strings live in `i18n.js` (English) and `i18n-he.js` (Hebrew).
