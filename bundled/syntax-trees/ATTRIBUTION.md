# MACULA source sentence trees

Source: MACULA Hebrew Linguistic Datasets, WLC/lowfat, commit `47db250bd55d0d8577f2a94fba114ef16c35b23c`, https://github.com/Clear-Bible/macula-hebrew/.

Licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://github.com/Clear-Bible/macula-hebrew/blob/47db250bd55d0d8577f2a94fba114ef16c35b23c/LICENSE.md.

The compressed files preserve the original sentence and constituent hierarchy, source role/class/rule labels, source word forms and lemmas. Safran adds consonant-verified mappings to its bundled OSHB Westminster text. Unmapped segments remain explicitly unmapped. Source sentence trees and BHSA clause relationships are distinct annotations.

Regenerate with `python tools/tanakh-syntax/build.py /path/to/the/pinned/WLC/lowfat`.
