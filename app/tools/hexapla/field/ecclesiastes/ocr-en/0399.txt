[18] Nobil. gives: Ἀ. κυλίκιον καὶ κύλικας. Syro-hex. ܐ. ܟܣܐ ܘܟܣ̈ܐ܀ Jerome: “For concerning the _men who pour wine_ and the _women who pour wine_ … Aquila gives a rendering quite different from the obvious one. For he named not human beings, that is, men and women, but kinds of small vessels, calling them κυλίκιον and κυλίκια, which in Hebrew is called SADDA and SADDOTH. Finally Symmachus, although he could not express it word for word, is not far from this opinion, saying: _kinds of measures and their settings._ Whether, then, jugs or goblets or mixing bowls, set out for service and adorned with gold and gems, we must believe that Solomon had them; and that from one κυλικίῳ, that is, _mixing bowl_, it was drawn into other κυλικίοις, namely smaller vessels.” Montef., on Martianay's conjecture, had printed: Σ. [μέτρων σχήματα καὶ ἐπιθήματα]. For _mensurarum_ (of measures) Vallarsi, on the authority of the Vatican MS. and another Palatine one by the first hand, edited _mensarum_ (of tables).
