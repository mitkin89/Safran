=== Blatt 1003 · S. 987 · –Cap. IV. 11.] JONAS.
--- Fußnoten
[8] (Forts.) the ground, it is quickly nourished and rises up into a tree; and within a few days you look up at a small tree where you had seen a herb. Hence we too, at the time when we were translating the prophets, wished to express that very name of the Hebrew language, because the Latin tongue had no such species of tree [on the contrary, it had the name _ricinus_. Cf. Gesen. in _Thes. Ling. Hebr._ p. 1214]; but we feared the grammarians, lest they should find licence to comment . . . and we followed the old translators, who themselves also rendered _ivy_, which in Greek is called κισσός; for they had nothing else to say." The same, in Epist. CXII to Augustine, expressly testifies that Aquila with the rest translated _ivy_.
