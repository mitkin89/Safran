[6] (Forts.) noting: "And indeed בְּצֵל signifies _in the shade_ or _in the shady place_; but why he rendered the other syllable צַח by τὸ σῶμα would, unless I am mistaken, be inquired into in vain." We have cleaned up the reading from Cod. 243*, in which the strokes of the letters δι ὑπὸ are somewhat obscure. The translator, however (provided it be a translator's), seems to be Symmachus, who put καύσων or καῦμα for צַח at Jerem. iv. 11.
