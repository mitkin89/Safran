Besides, Montef. published from the Regius a splendid hexaplar note: Ἄλλος· σεμνῶς ἄγειν, whose origin will be readily evident to anyone who looks at Procopius. Thus Aquila's _five_ renderings of the word לְהָסֵךְ, mentioned by Schleusner in _Nov. Thes._ s. v. διευθύνω, are reduced to about _two_, διευθῦναι and ἀποκενῶσαι, though one will hardly see how either of them is to be reconciled with the Hebrew archetype. To say what I think, the rendering ἀποκενῶσαι, both here and in the passage of Judges, is to be ascribed to Theodotion alone; the other, διευθῦναι, represents the Hebrew reading לְהַיְשִׁר, not לְהָסֵךְ.
