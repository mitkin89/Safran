Nobil. also cites: Ἀ. στρογγύλωμα τριχῶν. From which one might conjecture that the reading καὶ στρ. τριχῶν belongs to Aquila's second edition; this is the opinion of Scharfenberg, who, however, wrongly regards the other and undoubted version of the same translator, τὸ πᾶν πλῆθος (as if from כָּבַר, _he was much_), as a gloss.
