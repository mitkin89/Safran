[105] De Reg. cites from Cod. 86: Ἀ. μιμήσασθαι. But Parsons. from Cod. 88: Ἀ. μιμνήσασθαι (sic). Θ. μαθεῖν. Without doubt μιμήσασθαι must be claimed for Aquila, for the Hebrew בְּחַקֹּתֵךְ from the root חָקָה, _he delineated_, whence מְחֻקֶּה, _delineated_, for which the same translator set down μίμημα, Cap. xxiii. 14. The reading of Theodotion can be compared with the Hebrew לֶקַח, _doctrine_, yet it is not free from suspicion.
