--- Fußnoten
[19] Thus Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex., and without the asterisk Comp., Ald., Codd. III, XII, 22, 23, 26, and others.
[20] Cod. 86, on the testimony of De Reg. (Parsons. copied from the same: Ο΄. Θ. εκ πρ. μου εν σειμ. Ἀ. ※ ενετιειμ. Σ. ※ ενεπιζει.) Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex. in the text: ※ ἐκ προσώπου μου ἐσσίμ (ܐܣܣܝܡ) ↙; and thus without the asterisk Comp. (with ἐν σεὶμ), Codd. 62, 147 (both with ἐσσείμ). Theodoret. reads: ἀπὸ πρ. μου ἐν σεὶμ, σπεύδοντες κ. τ. ἑ., noting: τὸ δὲ σεὶμ τῇ Ἑλλάδι φωνῇ ἐν ἰσχύϊ δηλοῖ. Finally Montef. cites from Cod. Jes.: ἐκ πρ. μου (μου om. Parsons.) ἐσσίμ. Σ. ἐν ἐπείξει. Hieron. (who renders the LXX: _In that day messengers shall go forth from my face, hastening to destroy the hope of Ethiopia_): "And _messengers_ shall have come, according to Aquila and Theodotion, _siim,_ which Sym. rendered _hastening,_ and we have rendered _in triremes;_ for so we have received it from the Hebrews." But we pay no heed to the anonymous writer in Nobil., who assigns ἐγκαθισμὸς to Theodotion.

[29] Cod. Jes. Thus in the text Comp., Ald., Codd. III, 22 (with εἰς χεῖρας), 23 (the same), 26, and others, Hieron., and under the asterisk Codd. XII, 87, 88, Syro-hex. The latter, however, gives to τῷ ἀποδώσομαι the sense not of _selling,_ but of _handing over_ (ܐܫܠܡ[?]), both here and in Jud. iv. 2. Next Cod. 87 alone marks: ※ ἐν χερσὶν κ. τ. ἑ., without the metobelus.
[30] Thus Codd. 87, 88, and without the asterisk Comp., Ald., Codd. III, 22, 23, 26, and others, and Syro-hex. Hieron.: "There follows: _And I will destroy the images_ (which the LXX rendered _abominations_), _and I will cause the idols to cease_ (or _the nobles_). Cod. Jes. cites: ※ Θ. βδελύγματα καὶ καταπαύσω μεγιστᾶνας (not, as Montef., ※ Θ. καὶ ἀπολῶ βδελύγματα, καὶ καταπαύσω), against Cod. 86, which cites: Σ. Θ. εἴδωλα.
[31] Cod. XII (with ἐν τῇ γῇ), on the testimony of Parsons. Thus without the asterisk Comp., Codd. III (with Αἰγύπτῳ), 22 (with φόνον), 23, and others, and Hieron. Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex.: ※ καὶ δώσω φ. ἐκ γῆς Αἰγύπτου ↙.
[32] Cod. 86, on the testimony of De Reg. Parsons. copied: Ἀ. βανω. Σ. ενο. Θ. ενοι (sic). Hieron.: "It has its ancient name No, which Aq., Sym. and Theod. rendered just as it stands in the Hebrew. Instead of which, meaning I know not what, the LXX said _Diospolis,_ which is a small city of Egypt [but see Gesen. in _Thes. Ling. Hebr._ p. 834]. We, however, have put _Alexandria_ for No, by anticipation, which in Greek is called πρόληψις."
[33] Cod. 86, on the testimony of De Reg. Hieron.: "_And I will slay,_ he says, _the multitude of Alexandria,_ which again in the Hebrew is given as No, for which the LXX rendered not _Diospolis,_ as above, but _Memphis._"
