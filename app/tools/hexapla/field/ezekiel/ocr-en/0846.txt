The Syriac ܐܣܟܡܐ[?], _a covering_ (esp. of the head), in Greek κάλυμμα, ἐπικάλυμμα, περικάλυμμα, I fear can hardly be reconciled with the Greek περίβλημα. The other word ܢܨܝ̈ܦܐ[?], for Norberg _drooping turbans_, from ܢܨ[?], _he inclined_, without doubt belongs to χαλάσματα, which seem not to differ from χαλαστὰ, _little chains_, in the reading of Theodotion at Exod. xxviii. 14, where our Syrian likewise cites: ܬ. ܢܨܝ̈ܦܬܐ[?] ⁘.
