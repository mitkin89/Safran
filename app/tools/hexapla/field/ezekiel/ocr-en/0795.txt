=== Blatt 0795 · S. 779 · –Cap. V. 14.] EZECHIEL.
--- Fußnoten
[20] Hieron. in continuation: "_For they have rejected my statutes, and have not walked in my judgments._"
[21] Codd. 86, 88. For ἀφορμὴ Codd. 22, 36, others read μορφὴ, as does Theodoret., who explains: ἐπειδὴ γὰρ τὸν τῶν ἐθνῶν ἀνεμάξασθε χαρακτῆρα.
[22] Hieron.
[23] The same: "For what we have translated: _Because ye have surpassed the nations that are round about you_, Sym. translated: _Because your multitude was from the nations that are round about you._" Syro-hex. gives: Σ. τὸ πλῆθος ὑμῶν ἐγένετο (ܣܓܝܐܘܬܐ[?] ܕܝܠܟܘܢ ܗܘܐ).
[24] Codd. 86, 88 (without Θ.). Cod. 62 in the text: καίγε ἐγὼ καὶ ποιήσω. Cod. 87, Syro-hex.: καὶ ἐγὼ ποιήσω.
[25] Cod. 86, according to De Reg.
[26] Cod. Jes. Thus Comp., Ald., Codd. 22, 23 (with προσοχθήμασι), 26, 36, others, and under the asterisk Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex.
[27] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܡܛܢ̈ܦܘܬܟ[?] ◆. Cf. Hex. ad Jerem. xxxii. 34, where in the text of the LXX our translator gave ܡܛܢ̈ܦܘܬܐ[?] for τὰ μιάσματα.
[28] Cod. 86, according to De Reg.
[29] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܐܬܒܪܟܝ[?] ◆. That is, Sym. rendered אֶגְדַּע, _I will cut down_, which is read in some books. Hieron.: "_I will break_ (or according to the LXX, _I will cast away_) _thee._"
[30] Thus Codd. 22, 48, others, perhaps from Symmachus.
[31] Codd. 86, 88. The obelus is in Syro-hex., which marks: ÷ καὶ τὸ τ. σου ↙.
[32] Thus (with ※ for ※ Θ.) Syro-hex. Montef. from Cod. Jes. gives: ※ Θ. τὸ τ. σου ἐν μ. πεσεῖται. Hieron.: "This which we have put: _And the fourth part of thee shall fall by the sword_, has been added under asterisks from the edition of Theodotion." Besides, Montef. edited from the same codex: ※ Ἀ. καὶ τὸ τ. σου εἰς π. ἄνεμον σκορπιῶ αὐτούς. Codd. 22, 36, and others exhibit the passage thus: (Cod. 87: ※) καὶ τὸ τ. σου πεσοῦνται ἐν ῥομφαίᾳ (Codd. 87, 88: ἐν ῥ. πεσοῦνται) κύκλῳ σου (↙), the rest as in Syro-hex.
[33] Codd. 86, 88. Cf. on v. 2.
[34] Cod. 86.
[35] Cod. Jes. Thus in the text Comp., Ald., Codd. 22, 23, others, and under the asterisk Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex. Cod. 86 gives: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. Ο΄. παρακληθήσομαι.
[36] Cod. 86, and, omitting Ο΄, Cod. 88. The same words are placed in the text before the reading τῶν Ο΄ by Codd. 22, 23, 36, others, and under the asterisk by Codd. XII, 87 (with ὀνειδισμὸν for ὄνειδος), 88 (the same), and Syro-hex. (which marks: καὶ εἰς ὄνειδος (or ὀνειδισμὸν) ※ ἐν τοῖς ἔ. τοῖς κ. σου ↙). Hieron.: "And what follows, _and for a reproach to the nations that are round about thee_, is not found in the LXX, in place of which they added of their own, _and thy daughters round about thee._"
