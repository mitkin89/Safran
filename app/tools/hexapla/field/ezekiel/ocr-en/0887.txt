From which Montef. hammered out a hexaplaric note: ※ συναγωγὴ πολλὴ, πέλτας καὶ ἀσπίδας καὶ μαχαίρας πάντες αὐτοὶ ἔχοντες.

Then for πάντες περικεφαλαίαις καὶ πέλταις Codd. 22, 23, 36, and others exhibit: πάντες οὗτοι ἀσπίδας καὶ περικεφ. καὶ πέλτας ἔχοντες, which reading Montef., following Drusius, cites in mutilated form.

[11] Hieron.: "But that for _lions' whelps_, which in Hebrew are called CAPHIR, the LXX and Theod. should put _villages_, is an obvious error. For if you write it with the letter CHI, it is called _a lion's whelp;_ but if with CE, which in Hebrew is called COPH, it is called _a field_ and _a village:_ whence Capernaum too is called _a most beautiful field_." The error of the holy Doctor concerning the first letter of the Hebrew word כָּפָר was long ago pointed out by learned men.
