[8] Nobil. cites it as a scholion, but without doubt it is another rendering. Hieron. on Cap. xli. 15 twice put ἐκθέτας for the Hebrew אַתִּיקִים, _projections,_ as he himself explains, further noting: "But at Rome they call Ἐκθέτας the _sun-terraces_ projecting from the walls of upper rooms, or _Maeniana_ [_balconies_], after the man who first invented them, which some of the Greeks call ἐξώστρας (perhaps ἐξώστας)." The nominative is ὁ ἐκθέτης (not, which is Schleusner's error, ἡ ἐκθέτη), known also from the Glossographers: Ἐκθέτης, ὁ ἐξώστης, _projection, eaves, under-eaves, Maenianum._ Cf. Hex. on 3 Reg. vi. 4. The neuter ἔκθετον, from ἔκθετος, with this meaning is not known from elsewhere.
[9] Cod. Jes. In the text Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex. read and mark: ÷ ἐπὶ πήχεις ἑκ. τὸ μῆκος ↙, ※ εἰς τὸ ἐσώτερον ↙ ὁδὸν π. ἑ. Thus without obelus and asterisk (with μῆκος instead of τὸ μ., and ὁδὸς instead of ὁδὸν) Codd. 22, 36, and others. Hieron.: "And what was added by the LXX, _by a hundred cubits of length,_ is superfluous, because it is not found in the Hebrew: in putting this, they removed what is found in the Hebrew, _looking toward the inner parts, of the way of one cubit,_ which we have translated from the Hebrew verity."

Further, the Hebrew ἅπαξ λεγόμενον הֲגִינָה, which lexicographers compare with the Talmudic הָגוּן, _fitting, suitable,_ Symmachus more correctly, unless I am mistaken, as appears even from the noun הַגְּדֶרֶת placed in the construct state, took as a substantive, and put for it ܒܝܬ ܡܦܢܝܢܐ[?], _a place of withdrawal, a retreat,_ perhaps with regard to the root הָגָה, _he removed._
