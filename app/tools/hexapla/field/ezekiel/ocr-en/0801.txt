[34] "MS. Jes. ὡς περιστερὰ μελετ- (sic)."—_Montef._ More correctly Parsons copied from the same: ως περιστεραι μελετητι και παντας, in agreement with Cod. Alex., which writes: ως π. μελετητι· και παντας, wrongly for ὡς π. μελετητικαί· πάντας, which reading Codd. 22, 23, 36, others exhibit, and under the asterisk Codd. 87, 88, Syro-hex. (In the latter Norberg edited: ※ ܐܝܟ ܝܘܢ̈ܐ ܡܗܝܡ̈ܢܝܬܐ[?] ↙., translating, _like entrusted doves_ (ܡܗܝܡ̈ܢܝܬܐ[?]). Bernstein in the _Zeitschrift_ etc. T. III, p. 408 attempts ܡܗܡ̈ܝܢܝܬܐ[?], _negligent, careless_, suspecting that the Syriac translator found in his copy the false reading μελλητικαὶ, which is actually found today in Cod. 42. In vain; for the Greek word μελλητικὸς means not _negligent_ or _careless_, but _hesitating_. What is more, our excellent codex exhibits, most correctly, not ܡܗܡ̈ܝܢܝܬܐ[?], but a spelling longer by one little letter, ܡܗܡ̈ܗܡܢܝܬܐ[?], _moaning_. A similar passage is Jesai. xxxviii. 14, καὶ ὡς περιστερὰ, οὕτω μελετῶ (אֶהְגֶּה), where our translator indeed put ܐܬܗܓܐ[?] too slavishly (as in this very passage the translator of S. Cyril on Luke, Hom. CXXXI: ܘܗܘܝܬ[?] ܠܠ[?] ܗܓܝܐ[?] ܐܝܟ ܝܘܢܐ ܕܡܗܡ̈ܗܡܢܝܬܐ[?]), but the other Syrian ܢܗܓܐ[?].) Hieron.: "The LXX passed over in complete silence _doves of the valleys_, or, as Theod. translated, _meditating_."
