CAP. XXX.
[1] Cod. 86, on the testimony of De Reg. Thus in the text Codd. 22, 36, and others, and (with ὀλολύξατε) Codd. 23, 62, and others. Montef. published: Ἄλλ. ὀλολύξατε, noting: "Drusius from the Complutensian edition, as also other things." Rather from the Aldine, where ὀλολύξατε is _placed after_ the words ὦ ὦ ἡμέρα.
[2] The same. Codd. 87, 88 (with ὅτι ?), Syro-hex.: (ὅτι ἐγγὺς ἡμέρα) καὶ ἐγγὺς ἡμέρα (τοῦ κυρίου).
[3] The same. These words, καὶ ἐγγίζει, are inserted before ἡμέρα νεφέλης by Codd. 22, 23, 36, and others.
[4] Thus with the asterisk Cod. 87, Syro-hex.
[5] Thus Montef., without naming his authority. These words are absent from Cod. 106 alone.
[6] De Reg. cites for יוֹם in the first place: "Ἀ. πέρας. Θ. καιρός. Ο΄. ἡμέρα C. B. [Cod. 86]." Instead of πέρας, Codd. III, 62, 147 have καιρὸς πέρας; Comp., Codd. 23, 36, and others, καὶ καιρός.
[7] Thus Codd. XII, 87, 88, Syro-hex., and without the asterisk Comp., Ald., Codd. III, 26, 42, and others. Another reading, καὶ ληφθήσεται τὸ πλ. αὐτῶν, is in Codd. 22, 23, 36, and others, and Theodoret.
[8] After τὰ θεμέλια αὐτῆς Codd. 22, 23, 36, and others insert: καὶ καταλυθήσεται. Perhaps it is Symmachus's, comparing Hex. on Psal. x. 3.
[9] Cod. 86, on the testimony of De Reg. Less probably Parsons. copied Αἰθίοπες. Cf. Hex. on Psal. lxvii. 32. lxxxvi. 4.
[10] Cod. 86, on the testimony of De Reg. Parsons. copied: Ἀ. πᾶσα Ἀραβία. Σ. Ἀραβία.
[11] The same. Parsons. cites: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. καὶ Χουζά. Codd. 62, 147 in the text: καὶ χουν (sic). (We do not know what Codd. 26, 106 mean, which insert ἐπ᾽ αὐτήν after ἐπίμικτοι.) Hieron. is here in error when he says: "Concerning various peoples and _Chub,_ which Sym. rendered _Arabia;_" and, after much in between: "And _Chub,_ that is, _the Arabs,_ and all the rest of the rabble, whom the LXX rendered _hybrids_ and _mixed people._"
