[9] Nobil. from Chrysostom. In the printed editions of Chrysostom today one reads μὴ συναυλισθῶ, approved by Schleusner in the _Opusc. Crit._ p. 258, where he appeals to the _Catena PP. GG._, T. III, p. 730.
[10] Ἄλλος· κατελασάτω κ.τ.ἑ. "Thus one Reg., excellently, in accordance with the Hebrew word. Drusius, however [from Chrysost.], κατελεησάτω κ.τ.ἑ."—_Montef._ The Syro-hex. cites: ܐ. ܢܪܕܦܢܝ ܀ So he found κατελασάτω in his copy, which he seems to have taken in the sense of _driving away_ (as ܐܪܕܘܦ, ἀπελάσω, Ezech. xxxiv. 12 in the margin of the Syro-hex.), not of _beating_. Cf. our Hex. on Jesai. xxviii. 1.
[11] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܢܩܝܡܢܝ ܀ I do not know whether the translator was thinking of the Hebrew יַחֲלִימֵנִי, _let him make me recover_, ἀναστησάτω με.
[12] Nobil., Vat. The Syro-hex. cites: Ἀ. Σ. ἐν κακίαις αὐτῶν (ܒܒܝܫ̈ܬܗܘܢ).
[13] Nobil., Vat., Euseb. in Mai. l. c., and without name Chrysost. Theodoret.: Σ. ἔτι δὲ καὶ κ.τ.ἑ., with the agreement of Didymus Alex. in Mai. _P. N. B._, T. VII, P. II, p. 306, who has κακῶν for κακιῶν.
[14] Nobil., Vat.
[15] Chrysost.
[16] Thus Comp., Ald., Chrysost., Theodoret., Syro-hex., and the manuscripts.
[17] One Reg. Nobil. cites: Ἄλλος· ὑπεσπάσθησαν. Cf. the Hex. on Deut. xv. 1.
[18] Vat., Euseb., Didym. Alex., and without name Chrysost. Montef., after Nobil., printed ἐν χειρὶ, in opposition to the Syro-hex., which cites: ܣ. ܢܬܬܠܝܘܢ ܒܐܝ̈ܕܝ ܟܐܦܐ ܕܝܢ̈ܘܗܝ[?] ܀
[19] Nobil. cites: Ἀ. ὅτι εὐτρεπίσθησαν. Drusius's conjecture εὐπρεπίσθησαν, passed over by Montef., we have accepted without hesitation, comparing the Hex. on Prov. ii. 10. Hieron.: _for they are comely._
[20] Nobil., Euseb., Didym. Alex., Euthym. The Syro-hex. continues for Symmachus: ܟܕ ܫܡܥܘ ܡܠ̈ܝ ܗܢܝ̈ܐܬܐ ܀, i.e. ἀκούσαντες ῥήσεις μου ἡδίστας.
[21] Chrysost. Nobil. found _in another book_: Σ. ὅτι εὐπροσώπησαν. Schol. ὅτι ἐδορυφορήθησαν.
[22] Nobil. cites: "Schol. Οἱ Ο΄. Θ. ἠδυνάσθησαν. Some books have ἠδυνήθησαν [thus the first hand of Cod. Vat.], and the Latin editions [Psalt. Gall. and Rom.], _they were able._ The Old Psalter, indeed, and S. Augustine, _they prevailed._"
[23] Thus Nobil., except that with Chrysost. he has εἰς τὸν ᾅδην. Montef. corrected from one Regius εἰς στόμα ᾅδου. Two Regii and Chrysost.: ὁμοίως εἰ ἀποκλῶντι κ.τ.ἑ.
[24] Nobil., and without name Chrysost., whom the Syro-hex. has accurately rendered: ܣ. ܐܝܟ ܐܟܪܐ ܐܡܬܝ ܕܪܕܐ ܠܐܪܥܐ. ܗܟܢܐ ܐܬܒܕܪܘ ܓܪ̈ܡܝܢ ܒܦܘܡܐ ܕܫܝܘܠ ܀ One Reg. has εἰς ᾅδου; Euseb. and Didym. Alex.: ὡς ἐσκ. ἡμῶν τὰ ὀ. παρὰ τὸν ᾅδην.
