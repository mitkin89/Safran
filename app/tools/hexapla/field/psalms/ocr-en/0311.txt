[56] Nobil., Vat., one Reg. The other Reg., for ἦραν εἰς εἰκῆ, assigns to Aquila ἐπήρθησαν ματαίως, which belongs to the reading of Symmachus. (The word ἀπέννοια (as it were ἀπ-έννοια, מְ-זִמָּה, from מִן and זִמָּה, ἔννοια, Aq. on Prov. xxi. 27) is in no way to be tampered with. Rather, perhaps this word should be restored to the same translator at Psal. xx. 12, where for מְזִמָּה our Syrian assigns _pride_ (ܡܬܪܘܡܘܬܐ) to Aquila, perhaps for the Greek ἀπόνοιαν, which had crept into the place of the rarer ἀπέννοιαν.)
