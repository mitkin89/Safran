[27] Hieron. ibid.: “You say that _by hands_ is not found in the Greek, and I know it; but it was added in the LXX under an asterisk from the Hebrew and from the edition of Theodotion. Finally in the Hebrew too it is thus written, ZE HAIAM GADOL VARAB IDAIM, which Aquila rendered thus, αὐλὴ καὶ πλατεῖα χερσίν; and all the translators αὕτη ἡ θάλασσα ἡ μεγάλη καὶ εὐρύχωρος χερσίν.” Thus Vallarsi printed it, noting that the word αὐλὴν is found used for an ample and spacious place by Greek writers of good repute. This does not satisfy us; rather we would believe that αὐλὴ arose from μεγάλη, which this translator could not but put here for גָּדוֹל.
