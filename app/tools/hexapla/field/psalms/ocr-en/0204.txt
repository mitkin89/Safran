PSALM. LIX.
[1] Montef. printed from Euseb. and from one Regius: Ἀ. τῷ νικ. τοῖς ἀλλοτριωθησομένοις ἐπὶ κρίνων ταπεινοῦ κ.τ.ἑ., omitting μαρτυρίας, which Euseb. expressly recognizes, and taking up, on I know not what authority, τοῖς ἀλλοτριωθησομένοις, which seems to have migrated here from the LXX version τοῖς ἀλλοιωθησομένοις. Cf. on Psal. lxxix. 1.
[2] Euseb., who wrongly writes εἰς διαδοχὴν. Here too Montef., after Nobil. and Drusius, omitted μαρτυρία.
[3] Euseb. For ἐμπρήσας Schleusner in _Opusc. Crit._ p. 215 corrects ἐνέπρησας, by oversight, I think, for ἐνέπρησε. But since Sym., whether by chance or on purpose, has passed over the name of Joab, it seems rather that καὶ before ἀνέστρεψεν should be deleted, so that the defeat of the Edomites is claimed for David himself, in accordance with the sacred narrative, 2 Reg. viii. 13.
[4] Euseb., and Origen. Opp. T. II, p. 737. "Thus one Colbert.; but the other Colbert. ἔκοψας."—_Montef._
[5] Euseb., Origen., Nobil. and two Regii.
[6] Euseb., Nobil.
[7] Euseb. cites: Ε΄. καὶ περιεκύκλωσαν (sic) ἡμᾶς; but a little later, καὶ περιήγαγες ἡμᾶς. We have corrected it to περιεκύκλωσας.
