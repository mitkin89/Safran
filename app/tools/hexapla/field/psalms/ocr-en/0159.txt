[13] At the words ἐν ἀνθρώπῳ κ.τ.έ. our Syriac has added: ܣ. ܐܝܠܝܢ ܕܡܬܪܥܝܢ[?] ܀ Bugati's version is "_to those who are fed;_" which offends against the language on more than one count. For ܐܬܪܥܝ in the Ethpeel conjugation means "_he was fed_", but ܐܬܪܥܝ in the Ethpaal "_he thought, meditated_", and is interchanged with the Greek words λογίζεσθαι, βουλεύεσθαι, φρονεῖν (Zach. ix. 2), ἐνθυμεῖσθαι (Jesai. x. 7). Moreover, in the passage Psal. xxx. 14, for זָמָמוּ Parsons. cites from Cod. 264: Ο΄. ἐβουλεύσαντο. Ἀ. ἐνενοήθησαν. Σ. ἐνεθυμοῦντο. Having weighed these things, I think the Syriac note, which plainly belongs not to the whole clause but to the single word מְזִמּוֹת, should be rendered thus: Σ. ἃ ἐνθυμεῖται. Hieron. also renders: "_against the man who does what he devises._"
