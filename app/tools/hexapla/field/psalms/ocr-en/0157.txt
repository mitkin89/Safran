=== Blatt 0157 · S. 141 · –PSALM. XXXIV. 24.] PSALMI.
--- Fußnoten
[25] (Forts.) ܒܝܬ, or ܒܐܝܬ, "_he made domestic, made his own, claimed for himself_" (Gr. οἰκειοῦσθαι), from which comes the verbal noun ܡܒܝܬܢܐ, "_domestic_". But it is difficult to understand, if the Syriac had πεπλασμένοις before its eyes, why it did not render it by the proper word ܡܓ̈ܒܠܬܐ. For the rest, Hieron. imitated Symmachus here, rendering: "_in the pretence of feigned words._"

[31] "Our Syriac, as Bugatus renders, cites: Ἀ. '_sad of eye_' (ܕܡܟܡܪܝܢ ܒܥܝܢܐ ܀). Parsons's Cod. 268 has the following: Ἀκύλ. καὶ νεύματι διέσυραν ὀφθαλμῶν τὰ τούτου παιδεύματα; which anyone can see should be taken not so much for the very words of that translator as for an explanation of them. Even granting this, I hesitate over the word ܕܡܟܡܪܝܢ, which I suspect should be exchanged for ܕܡܟܡܪܝܢ[?], διασύροντες (cf. on Jerem. l. 45)."—This I once wrote in _Otium Norvic._ p. 14. Later I was taught by a man most skilled in these studies, R. L. Bensly, that the Syriac ܕܡܟܡܪܝܢ should be rendered not "_sad_", but "_vexing;_" and that Aquila in the passages Prov. vi. 13. xvi. 30 put κνίζειν for קָרַץ. Suidas: κνίζων, αἰτιατικῇ, λυπῶν ἢ τέμνων. The Syriac translator took it in the former sense.
