Moreover, in the passage Deut. xxxii. 14, where Montef. printed κερεϊνῶν from Coislin., the MS. has Ἀ. ριμων; and if this writing is to be enlarged by one syllable, it would be easier to restore καιρίμων than κερεϊνῶν. These things being weighed, there will perhaps be some who will not shrink from Schleusner's opinion that in the Hex. on Psal. xlix. 9 also, for κερεινοὺς (sic in Nobil.), a word scarcely known from elsewhere, καιρίους [or καιρίμους] should be written.
