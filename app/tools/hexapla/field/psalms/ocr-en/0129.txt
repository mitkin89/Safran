[81] "Two MSS." Hieron. in the Epistle to Sun. and Fret. 9: "In the Hebrew it has nothing else but this: '_My deliverer from my enemies._' The Septuagint, however, added '_wrathful._'" Rather, the LXX, joining מֵאֹיְבַי־אַף closely, rendered ἐξ ἐχθρῶν ὀργίλων: then for אַף מִן־קָמַי, "_also from those that rise up,_" they put only ἀπὸ τῶν ἐπανισταμένων. In Psalt. Gall. one reads: ÷ "_wrathful_" ↙, ※ "_and_" ↙ "_from those that rise up;_" nor can it be doubted that the translators other than the LXX expressed the copula.
