[14] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܥܝܢܐ ܕܝܠܝ ܟܪܝܬ ܡܢ ܡܘܟܟܐ[?] ܀, i.e., as Bugatus renders, _my eye is weakened_ etc. The same on Psal. cv. 15 rendered the Syriac ܟܪܝܘܬܐ, put by Aquila for the Hebr. רָזוֹן, _leanness_, in Latin by _weakness_. The lexicons which we have at hand give only ܟܪܝܐ, _weak, feeble_, from Bar Bahlul. The verb ܟܪܐ is interchanged with the Greek συνεψήσθη, _was scraped together, swept away_, Jerem. xlvi. 15. l. 45. See our remarks in _Otio Norvic._ p. 51.
