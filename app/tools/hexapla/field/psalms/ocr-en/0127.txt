=== Blatt 0127 · S. 111 · —PSALM. XVII. 30.] PSALMI.
--- Fußnoten
[28] (Forts.) "_he destroyed with lightning,_" ἐκεραύνωσεν, which with our translator is ܦܚܡ[?] in Jesai. xxx. 30. By all means compare Jerem. li. 34, where for the Hebr. הֲמָמַנִי the Syriac assigns to Aquila ܓܪܡܢܝ, i.e., as Norberg renders, "_he has gnawed me;_" in which sense Ephr. used it of the _moth_, Opp. T. II, p. 319, l. 28, and p. 391, l. 25, as Bernstein points out in the _Lex. ad Chrestom. Syr._ p. 79. For my part I have no doubt that in both places Aquila gave ἐφαγεδαίνωσε, "_he consumed,_" as he translated for the same Hebrew in Deut. vii. 23. 1 Reg. vii. 10. The latter passage is: καὶ ἐβρόντησε κύριος ἐν φωνῇ μεγάλῃ ἐν τῇ ἡμέρᾳ ἐκείνῃ ἐπὶ τοὺς ἀλλοφύλους, וַיְהֻמֵּם; where in the Hexapla is: Ο΄. συνεχύθησαν. Ἄλλ. ἐφαγεδαίνυσαν. Ἄλλ. ἐξέστησαν. According to Cod. 108 ἐξέστησαν belongs to Theodotion; the other reading seems to need emending thus: Ἀ. ἐφαγεδαίνωσεν (αὐτούς).

[31] "One codex."—_Montef._ It savours of a gloss, such as Theodoret. cites: ἀντὶ τοῦ, πέπεικεν ἅπαντας ὡς οὐρανόθεν μοι τὴν ἐπικουρίαν παρέσχε.
[32] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܓܪܦ ܠܝ ܀ ܣ. ܡܨܢܝ ܀ The Greek is very uncertain. ܓܪܦ has various meanings, e.g. εἵλησε, περιείλησε, ἐνετύλιξε, ἐκύκλωσε, περιεκύκλωσε, and the like, which seem foreign to the Hebr. מָשָׁה, "_he drew;_" Symmachus's ܡܨ, however, "_he sucked,_" occurs, unless I am mistaken, only once in this version. Bar Hebr. also cites: ܣ. ܘܡܨܡܨܢܝ[?], but, as Bernstein attests on the _Chrestom. Syr._ p. 163, in such a way that it is referred, less aptly, to יִקָּחֵנִי, not to יַמְשֵׁנִי.
