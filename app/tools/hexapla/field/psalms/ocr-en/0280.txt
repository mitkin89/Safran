In the word διανοίας lurks an error, which seems to have escaped both Theodoret himself and his editors. Yet comparing the passage Job. xii. 24, where for לֹא־דָרֶךְ the Hexapla attribute δι᾽ ἀνοδίας to Symmachus, anyone can see that here too ἐν ματαιότητι δι᾽ ἀνοδίας must be restored.
