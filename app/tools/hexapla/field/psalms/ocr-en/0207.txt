[8] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܐܝܟ ܫܘܪܐ ܕܡܣܛܐ[?] ܘܐܝܟ ܣܝܓܐ ܕܐܬܕܚܝ܀ So Bugatus printed it rather carelessly, rendering: _As a wall dug through_ etc. But for ܕܡܣܛܐ[?], which, unless I am mistaken, is no word at all, three codd. have ܕܡܨܛܐ[?] (Pesh. ܕܡܨܛܝܐ[?]), from ܨܛܐ, _he turned aside_, which our translator interchanged with the Greek σφάλλειν in Job. xviii. 7.
[9] One Coislin.
[10] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܒܪܡ ܟܕ ܐܪܝܡܘ ܐܬܚܫܒܘ ܠܡܕܚܐ ܡܨܛܒܝܢܘܬܐ ܕܐܟܚܕܐ ܒܕܓܠܘܬܐ܀ Bugat. renders: _Nevertheless when they lifted up, they thought to drive back good pleasure, because_ (or _that_) _together in falsehood._ But ܡܨܛܒܝܢܘܬܐ ܕܐܟܚܕܐ must be closely joined, in Greek perhaps συνευδόκησις, as in Sym. on Psal. lxiii. 2 ܡܠܟܐ ܕܐܟܚܕܐ renders σύσκεψις.
