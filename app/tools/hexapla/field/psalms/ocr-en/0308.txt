[10] Hieron. in the Epist. ad Sun. et Fret. 82: "_For there is no word in my tongue._ For which you say that you read in the Greek, _For there is no guile in my tongue;_ which the Sixth edition alone rendered. On this authority Montef. claimed λόγος, not δόλος, for Theodotion.
