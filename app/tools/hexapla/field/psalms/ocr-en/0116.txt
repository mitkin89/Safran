=== Blatt 0116 · S. 100 · PSALMI. [PSALM. IX. 25 (x. 4)–
--- Fußnoten
[42] "This passage, previously mutilated and lacking the names of the translators in Drusius [Nobil.], we have restored from one Regius, one Colbert., and Vat. Chrysostom, however, assigns the first reading to Symmachus, the second to Aquila."—_Montef._ He is mistaken. Chrysostom has: Ἄλλος δέ φησιν· ὅτι αἰνέσας——οὐκ ἐκζητεῖ. Ἄλλος· ὅτι ὕμνησεν (om. ἀσεβὴς)——οὐ μὴ ἐκζητήσῃ. Savil., as usual, added α΄ in the margin of the former passage, but c΄ to the latter. From our Syriac, to be cited presently, it is clear that the latter reading is Aquila's, the _whole_ reading, I mean, not as Montefalconius would have it, who assigns its second part, κατὰ μετεωρισμὸν κ.τ.ἑ., to Symmachus, and the second part of Symmachus's, ὅταν ὑψωθῇ κ.τ.ἑ., to Aquila. Syro-hex. exhibits only Aquila's version: Ἀ. ὅτι ὕμνησεν (ܫܒܚ) ἀσεβὴς ἐν πόθῳ (ܒܪܓܬܐ) ψυχῆς αὐτοῦ, καὶ πλεονέκτης (ܥܠܘܒܐ) εὐλογήσας διέσυρε (ܐܣܠܝ) κύριον. ἀσεβὴς ἐν μετεωρισμῷ (ܒܡܪܘܡܘܬܐ[?]) ψυχῆς (ܢܦܫܐ, perhaps ܕܢܦܫܐ, but ܕܢܦܫܗ[?] is wanted) αὐτοῦ. Now let us look at the details. In Aquila, ἀσεβὴς after ὕμνησεν, wrongly omitted by Chrysost. and Nobil., was supplied by Montef., in agreement with the Syriac. Next, διέσυρε τὸν κύριον Nobil. and Montef., against Chrysost. In Sym. παράνομος without the article is given by Nobil., Montef., against the same. Next, τῆς ψυχῆς αὐτοῦ the same wrongly omit. Then ὁ παράνομος, which is wanting in all, has been taken from the Hebrew. I almost forgot to note that Eusebius on this passage copied the single word διέσυρε from Aquila's reading.
