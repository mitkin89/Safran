[12] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܡܢ ܙܒܢܐ ܕܪܕܝܐ ◆ Bugati renders: _from the time of the flowing._ Cf. our note on Hos. ii. 22, in _Otium Norv._ p. 68. For the rest, in this version of the three translators it is manifest that the word χεῦμα belongs to Aquila alone. That the three translators rendered καιροῦ for καρποῦ is attested by Vat., noting: Οἱ Γ΄ ὁμοίως, i.e. τοῖς Ο΄, who, on Origen's testimony (Opp. T. II, p. 574), put ἀπὸ καιροῦ. Syro-hex., however, has in the text ἀπὸ καρποῦ.
