That is, they put Symmachus in place of Aquila."—_Montef._ Cod. 268, and without a name Cod. 264: Σ. ἀναιτίως ὑπώρυξαν. Aquila's version is very suspect; for neither does Eusebius mention that translator, and Syro-hex., if I see aright, cites: Ἀ. δωρεὰν ὤρυξαν (or ὑπώρυξαν; not διώρυξαν, which in our Syriac is ܚܦܪܘ) τὴν ψυχήν μου (ܡܓܢ ܚܦܪܘ ܠܢܦܫܝ ܀).
