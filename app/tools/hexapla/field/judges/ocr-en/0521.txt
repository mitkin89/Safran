[27] Cod. 85. “I hesitate over the version of Symmachus, which, although it does not disagree with the Hebrew if you interpret שְׂמִיכָה as _cushion_, is nevertheless suspect to me because of the unusualness of the word κοίμητρον, unknown to all the lexicographers I have consulted. Perhaps one should read ἐν κοιμητηρίῳ.”—_Scharfenb._ The form κοίμητρον is made by apocope from κοιμητήριον, like which are σκέπαστρον (Hex. on Job. xxiv. 15), σκάλιστρον (Hex. on Jerem. xliii. 10), ῥάντιστρον (Graeco-Ven. on Cant. Cant. vii. 2).
