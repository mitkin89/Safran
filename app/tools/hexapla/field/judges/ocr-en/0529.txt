[107] (Forts.) infers that it is not another version, but a scholium explaining the obscure rendering of the Elders, εἰς ἀποτομὰς κατακοπῶν. But since the word ἀχρειοῦν is commonly used by our writers in the sense of _destroying_, it is no very laborious matter to connect it in this place with the Hebrew הָלַם, _he struck_, and to exchange עֲמֵלִים for עָלְמִים.
