For Aquila Semler tries ἀναλώτοις, “_not consumed by age,_” in a sense wholly unheard of; Scharfenb. less unhappily χλωροῖς, so that it is fitted not to the combination of words, contrary to Aquila’s usage, but to the single word לַחִים. An easy correction would indeed be ἀλειώτοις, from λειοῦν, “_to smooth, to soften_” (cf. Hex. on Prov. xxviii. 23), but it is hard to reconcile with the Hebrew. Perhaps from ἀλέα, “_the heat of the sun,_” our translator, after his manner, coined the derivatives ἀλεόω and ἀλεωτὸς, so that the reading should finally be emended thus: Ἀ. (μὴ) ἀλεωτοῖς, “_not sun-dried._”
