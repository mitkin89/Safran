[45] “Basil. gives readings of the Three, without the names of the translators.” So Montef., who refers the readings to the Hebrew וַיַּעֲלוּ—בֵּית־אֵל (v. 22), and in the first copied περιεσώθησαν, in place of which Scharfenberg. proposed περιεκάθισαν, _they besieged,_ rightly comparing it with the Hebrew יָתִירוּ. The error of wrong reference must be charged to the codex itself, as Parsons also attests, who instead of περιεσώθησαν presents the true hand of the translator, doubtless Aquila, περιεσεύθησαν (sic), which represents the Hebrew וַיּוּתְרוּ. Cf. Hex. on Dan. x. 13. Besides, against κατεσκέψαντο Cod. 85 in the margin gives an anonymous reading: κατέκοψαν οἱ υἱοὶ Ἰωσὴφ τὴν Βαιθήλ; which learned men have rightly perceived to have arisen from a faulty spelling.
