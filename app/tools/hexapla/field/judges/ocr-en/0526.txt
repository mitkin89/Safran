Θ. εἰς τί ἐκάθισαν ἀν. τῶν Μωσφεθαΐμ; noting: “These readings of all the translators are given by Basil., which is supported by Euseb. [in the Onomasticon, p. 290]: Μοσθεφάμ. Ἀκύλας· τῶν κλήρων. Σ. μετεχμίων; Cod. 58 in the margin: τῶν κλήρων τῶν μεταιχμι (sic); and also Procop. in Cat. Niceph. T. (By the way we note that in the edited Septuagint text the word διγομίας, by which the Reubenites are compared to an ass lying down under two burdens, is by no means to be tampered with. Cf.
