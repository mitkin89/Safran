[1] So in the text Cod. 55, perhaps from another interpreter, with whom Graeco-Ven. agrees.
[2] Hesych.: "_According to the days of the menstrual separation_, or as the LXX: _According to the days of the menstrual purgation she shall be unclean._ But what this means the other interpreters have rendered more plainly: Sym. saying, _according to the days of the separation of affliction;_ Aq. _of the separation of misery;_ Theod. _of the separation of her pain._" For Aquila cf. Hex. ad Deut. xxviii. 60. On ἀφέδρου Codd. X, 85, in the margin without name: ὀδύνης. Cf. on Cap. xv. 33.
[3] So Ald., Codd. IV, 15, 16, 29, and others (among them 85, 130), Cat. Niceph., Arm. 1. "In others it is καθαρῷ, a reading which S. Augustine recognizes, but he notes that in some books _unclean_ is read."—_Nobil._ Vet. Lat.: _in her clean blood._ Hesych.: "But for sixty-six days she shall sit _in the clean blood_, that is, _in purification_."
[4] Cod. 85: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. καθαρίσεως. καθαρισμοῦ. ἀκαθάρτῳ. Less correctly Cod. 130: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. καθαρίσεται καθαρισμῷ ἀκαθάρτων. From these Codd. X, Lips. differ slightly, having thus: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. καθάρσεως καθαρισμοῦ (καθαρισμὸν Lips.).
