[10] Codd. X (with καικρατεωμένον), Lips. (with κεκρατεωμένον), 85. Hesych.: "The _scapegoat_, then, is so called as having been sent away, and not sacrificed. But for what reason is the one not sacrificed which has been assigned _to the strengthened one_, as Aquila rendered?" For the rest, we are not a little pleased by the conjecture of our predecessor C. F. Bahrdt, who, on account of the easy confusion of the little word καὶ written in abbreviated form with the siglum C or S, maintains that this entire reading is to be claimed for Aquila alone, reading: Ἀ. εἰς κεκραταιωμένον καὶ εἰς ἀπερχόμενον; that is, so that the Hebrew word is written divided, or at least rendered so: עַז, _strong_ (which for the same interpreter is κεκραταιωμένος at Jesai. xix. 4), and אָזֵל, _departing._ But what Scharfenb. in _Animadv._ p. 88 objects against Bahrdt's emendation, namely that Aquila counts the Hebrew words so carefully as neither to add nor to take away a single letter, is not to be pressed too far, as appears from that interpreter's version τοῦ ταπεινόφρονος ΚΑΙ ἁπλοῦ for the Hebrew מִכְתָּם (Psal. xv. 1); not to mention that our passage admits an easy correction: Ἀ. εἰς κεκραταιωμένον ἀπερχόμενον. Nor should another reading be passed over, which Montef. copied from Nobil. and the Royal Catenae (not from the Basilian, which is Bahrdt's error): Ἀ. [Cat. Niceph. p. 1065: Ἀκ. καὶ Θεοδ.] κλῆρος εἰς κεκρατημένος καὶ εἰς ἀπερχόμενος; which, though corrupted, to some extent favours the proposed emendation.

For the rest, on this passage Procopius in Octat. p. 356: "But necessity demanded that it be shown that God commanded that the goat which was carried off should also be offered to Him. That indeed is easily proved. For he says: _You shall take the living goat before the Lord, that expiation may be made through it, and it be banished into the desert._ Which indeed Aquilas interpreted in this way, so that the things said before are referred to the goat which was sent out into the wilderness." From which, however, nothing certain can be gathered about Aquila's version.
