[4] “So in the text Cod. 108.”—_Holmes._ There is an error in the number; for the reading of the said codex is τὸ δὲ λοιπόν.
[5] Codd. X, Lips. Here πυρῶν does not come from πῦρ, but from πυρὸν, a word invented, as it seems, by the translator himself, in place of which Graeco-Ven. at v. 9 put, more in keeping with the language, ἔμπυρον (=τὸ καιόμενον ἱερεῖον). Cf. Hex. ad Exod. xxix. 18. Here indeed both codices give πυρῶν; but in other places πύρον is written in the Coislinianus, πυρὸν in the Lipsiensis. There remains the spelling πυρρὸν, which Montef. seized upon from the Basilianus, though with a changed accent; which in Aquila, at any rate, a translator perversely diligent in the minutest things, is not to be wholly condemned, namely as expressing in some fashion the Hebrew שּׁ _with dagesh_ by the double letter ρ̄. But for πυρὸν, besides other witnesses, stand the passages of Hesychius which we shall adduce at Cap. iii. 16. x. 12.

Corruptly, as usual, the scribe of Cod. 130 in the margin without index: ’Α. ἁπαλόν. Θ. θ. οπον πίον (sic). Now let us look at Nobilius, who on νέα πεφρ. χίδρα ἐρικτὰ, in agreement with three Regii in Montef., and Cat. Niceph. ibid., cites: ’Α. [’Α. Σ. Niceph.] ἁπαλὰ λάχανα ὀσπριώδη. Θ. πίονα ἄλφιτα; where I would believe that Theodotion's ἄλφιτα belongs to גֶּרֶשׂ, because of the anonymous reading on χίδρων (v. 16) to be cited presently; πίονα, however, to כַּרְמֶל, in which, it is agreed, the notion of _fruitfulness_ is contained. Then follows a scholion of Eusebius, which Montef. copied more fully from his Regii: Χίδρα ἐστὶν, ὅταν ἀπὸ τῶν ἀκμασάντων ἀσταχύων λαβὼν διαθρύψῃ τις χερσὶν, οἱονεὶ ἀλέσας· τοῦτο γάρ ἐστιν, ἐρικτὰ προσοίσει τῷ θεῷ. ’Ερικτὰ δὲ τὰ ἐν μύλῳ πτισθέντα, καὶ τὸν φλοιὸν ἀποβαλόντα, ὀσπριώδη λάχανα. From this scholion, unless I am entirely deceived, is derived that absurd λάχανα ὀσπριώδη, which has accidentally attached itself to Aquila's reading ἁπαλά.
