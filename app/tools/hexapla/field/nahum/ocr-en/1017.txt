CAP. III.
[1] Hieron.: “Where we have put, _full of laceration,_ in the Hebrew there is PHEREC MALEA, which Aq. rendered ἐξαυχενισμοῦ πλήρης, that is, _full of neck-breaking;_ but Sym. ἀποτομίας πλήρης, which we may call _full of cruelty_ or _severity._ In another edition of his I found μελοκοπίας πλήρης, that is, of _cuttings of flesh_ and _pieces hacked limb from limb_ . . . Moreover the Hebrew interpreted PHEREC not as _neck-breaking_ . . . but as _helm,_ that is, κυβερνισμὸν.” The Hebrew, unless I am mistaken, is the one whom Hieron. had as his teacher in Hebrew learning (cf. on v. 8); whose explanation of the Hebrew word, however, is plainly fictitious.
