[6] Cod. 86. Syro-hex. cites: Ἀ. ἐξισώσει χάριν (ܢܫܘܐ ܛܝܒܘܬܐ), ἐξισώσει being wrongly taken as a verb instead of a noun.
[7] Cod. 86 cites: Σ. πρὸς χάριν αὐτοῦ; but Syro-hex.: Σ. πρὸς _concession_ αὐτῆς (ܠܘܬ ܡܘܗܒܬܐ ܕܝܠܗ̇). Here I should not believe that our Syriac read otherwise than in the Greek, but that it put ܡܘܗܒܬܐ (from ܝܗܒ, χαρίζεσθαι; cf. White on Act. Apost. viii. 16, who, however, is egregiously mistaken about the Peal form of the same verb), not ܛܝܒܘܬܐ, in order to express more aptly the phrase πρὸς χάριν τινὸς. Cf. Hex. on Prov. v. 3.

[9] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܡܢܘ[?] ܓܝܪ ܐܫܝܛ ܠܝܘܡܐ ܕܙܥܘ̈ܪܐ ܀ Cf. Hex. on Psal. cxxii. 4. Cant. Cant. viii. 1.
[10] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܕܐܢܟܐ ܀ ܣ. ܗܘ ܕܦܪܝܫܐ ܀ ܬ. ܕܡܢܝܢܐ ܀ Hieron.: “But this stone, that is, the mass, which among the Hebrews is written ABDIL, that is, _of tin,_ ἐτυμολογεῖται ἀποχωρίζων, that is, _separating_ and _sundering,_ as . . tin by means of fire dissociates metals that are mixed and adulterated with one another.” From which one might conjecture that Symmachus put τὸν λίθον τὸν ἀποχωρίζοντα (ܗܘ ܕܦܪܫܐ[?]). Theodotion's reading ܕܡܢܝܢܐ Middeld. renders _of separation, of distribution,_ a sense which I fear is fictitious.

CAP. V.
[1] Hieron.: “I see _a flying roll,_ which in Hebrew is called MEGELLA, and was rendered by Aq. and Theod. διφθέρα, by Sym. κεφαλὶς, that is, _a little head._”
[2] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܬ. ܡܓܠܬܐ ܕܦܪܚܐ ܀ ܣ. ܘܚܙܝܬ ܐܝܟ ܟܪܟܐ ܕܦܪܚܐ ܀ As regards Aquila, the Syriac ܡܓܠܬܐ, βιβλίον, put for the Greek διφθέραν, will detain no one. But a double reading of Symmachus is adduced; for κεφαλὶς (ܦܢܩܐ[?]) is one thing, εἴλημα (ܟܪܟܐ) another. Cf. Hex. on Psal. xxxix. 8. Jerem. xxxvi. 2, 14.
