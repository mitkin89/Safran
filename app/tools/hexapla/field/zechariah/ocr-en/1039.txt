For ADRACH signifies this, a name compounded of two complete words; AD signifying _sharp,_ RACH _soft and tender;_ and not, as it is wrongly read by some, _Sedrach._” We wonder that Jerome's interpretation, received no doubt from his Jewish teacher, did not commend itself to the ingenuity of Aquila, who in the title of Psalms LV—LIX resolved the Hebrew מִכְתָּם into two particles, which he made Greek by ταπεινὸς τέλειος.
