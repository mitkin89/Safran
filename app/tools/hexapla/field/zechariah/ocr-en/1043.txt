[7] Cod. 86. “But that μετακίνησιν seems to have been wrongly brought over from the LXX into Aquila.”—_Montef._ We almost suspect that the reading εἰς τὴν μετακίνησιν, whether it be Aquila's or the LXX translators', belongs not to לְחַטַּאת, but to לְנִדָּה, comparing the LXX on Esdr. ix. 11, and also Hex. on Lev. xv. 19; a supposition which is to some extent favoured by the copula prefixed to this reading both in the suspected version of Aquila and in some copies τῶν Ο΄.
