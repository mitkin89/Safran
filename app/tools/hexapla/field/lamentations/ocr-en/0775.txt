[21] On ῥομφαίας Syro-hex. gives in the margin in characters of medium size: ܕܦܪ̈ܫܐ ◆ (thus, not, as Middeld. edited, ܕܦܪܝܫܐ, wrongly translating, _struck_). Ceriani translates _of horsemen_, noting: "The index is one of those of readings of the translators and of variants, and is placed over ܕܣܝܦܐ. I should think the addition in this word should be placed after that word, as a variant reading. If you prefer a scholium, it is perhaps mutilated, and the index stands in the way." Since Middeldorpf's reading ܕܦܪܝܫܐ in Greek sounds ἀφωρισμένοι, I formerly thought it was a marginal note, attached to ܢܙܝܪ̈ܐ, Ναζιραῖοι (v. 7), either in the text of the LXX or in the reading of Symmachus. But whether the reading of the codex ܕܦܪ̈ܫܐ sometimes takes on the same meaning as ܕܦܪܝܫܐ is still in dispute. Cf. our remarks in Hex. ad Psal. xlvii. 3.
