[55] "On καμμύσαι Syro-hex. gives in the margin in characters of medium size: ܠܡܟܦܘ ܀, _for bending_, or _for blushing_; for the Syriac word in itself admits of both: or, if you prefer . . . _for bending, for a blush._ I prefer the last on account of the Hebrew, from which it does not entirely differ at least in sense."—_Ceriani._ The Greek word is, unless I am mistaken, κάμψαι, which is nothing but a corrupt spelling τοῦ καμμύσαι, or καμβύσαι, as Mai copied it from Cod. Vat. here and at Jesai. vi. 10. Similarly for ἐκόπασε our Syrian read and translated ἔκοψε Gen. viii. 1. Ruth i. 18; for the rarer ἐδέκασε the scribe of the papyrus wrote ἔδεξε in the _Notit. Cod. Sin._ p. 71, where Tischendorf, from a badly treated passage of Hesychius, invented a Greek δέκω with the meaning _to corrupt_, which does not exist.
