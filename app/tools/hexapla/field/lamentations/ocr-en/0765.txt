[40] Thus Cod. 88 (with με under ※, and ἐν ἐμοὶ under ÷), Syro-hex., which appends to ἐπεφύλλισε a scholium in characters of medium size: […] ܀ "The first hexaplar mark is wrongly placed in the Syriac, since ἐταπείνωσε or φθεγξάμενος too, as a different rendering of the Hebrew word הוֹגָה (cf. on v. 5), ought to have been enclosed under ÷."—_Ceriani._ The reading ὃ ἐπεφύλλισέ μοι is in Codd. 22, 36, others, and Theodoret.
[41] Cod. Jes. Montfaucon's reading, ἐφαυλίσθη, was corrected by Parsons. But that a more serious corruption lies hidden here is made probable by our Syrian, who notes on ὃ ἐπεφύλλισέ μοι: ܣ. ܐܝܟ ܥܘܠܠܬܐ ܐܬܥܘܠܠܬ ܀, i.e., Σ. ὡς ἐπιφυλλίδες (cf. Hex. ad Jerem. xlix. 9) ἐτρυγήθην, or, as Ceriani shrewdly detected, in one word, ἐπεφυλλίσθην, which could easily be corrupted into ἐφαυλίσθην.
