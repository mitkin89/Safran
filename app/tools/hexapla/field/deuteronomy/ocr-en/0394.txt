[16] In the Masian Syro-hexaplar Codex, at the end of Deuteronomy, there are some Syriac words very similar to those which Ceriani edited at Exod. xx. 17; and that they belong to this place is attested both by the Hebrew-Samaritan text itself at Deut. v. 21, and by the note subjoined to them, in which the scribe indicates that they are found in the Samaritan copy as a supplement to the _ten words_ (i.e. the Decalogue) of Deuteronomy. The very few variations between the two supplements (if you except ܠܟܢܥܢܝ̈ܐ[?] for ܠܟܢܥܢܝܐ[?] near the beginning) Ceriani carefully noted at the passage of Exodus, where for ἐπιβαλεῖς ἐπ’ αὐτοὺς, as the Syriac copy has in both places, ἐπιβαλεῖς ἐπ’ αὐτὸ was incautiously edited by us.
