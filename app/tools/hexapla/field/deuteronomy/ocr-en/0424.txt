[13] Hieron. in Comment. in Epist. ad Gal. (Opp. T. VII, p. 430): “I found, then, this very passage in Deuteronomy set down thus in the LXX translators: _Cursed is every man who has not continued in all the words of this law, to do them. And all the people shall say, So be it._ But in Aquila thus: _Cursed is he who has not established the words of this law, to do them. And all the people shall say, Truly._ Symmachus: _Cursed is he who has not confirmed the words of this law, to do them. And all the people shall say, Amen._ Further, Theodotion translated thus: _Cursed is he who has not raised up the words of this law, to do them. And all the people shall say, Amen._” On Sym. cf. Hex. ad Ezech. xiii. 6.
