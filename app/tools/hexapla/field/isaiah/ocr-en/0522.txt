[35] Thus Cod. 88 as copied by Parsons, except that we have corrected ἑταῖροί μου for ετεροι μοι. Various attempts may be made with συνέπησαν and συνεπέσθη, which correspond to τῷ ἅπαξ λεγομένῳ קָפַד, _he rolled up_, or _was rolled up_. In writing indeed συνέπαισαν and συνεπαίσθη, from συμπαίω, come nearest; but to the notion of the Hebrew root קָפַד, _was contracted_, συνέσπασαν (or συνέσπασα) and συνεσπάσθη are more suitable, the latter of which is interchanged with the Syr. ܩܦܣ[?] Thren. v. 10. But even so this reading of Symmachus is not rightly reconciled with the following one. Theodotion's ἐστενώθη does not seem to need tampering with.
