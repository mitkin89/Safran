CAP. V.
[1] Hieron. See Hex. on Cant. Cant. i. 13.
[2] Curter. Syro-hex. in the margin ܡܠܬܐ ܥܒܪܝܬܐ ܘܐܩܘܠܐ. ܠܟܪܡܐ ܕܝܠܗ ܐܦܩܘ܀[?] Middeld. renders: “The Hebrew word . . . and Aquila have rendered τῷ ἀμπελῶνι αὐτοῦ;” as if the name of another translator had dropped out. But Cod. C. agrees with the Ambrosian, and the Syriac ܐܦܩܘ does not mean _they have rendered_, but _they have published_, ἐξέδωκαν. Cf. Bugati on Dan. p. 167. For my part I would render: Τὸ ῥητὸν τὸ Ἑβραϊκὸν καὶ Ἀκύλας κ.τ.ἑ.
