Jerome p. 323: “The word _Chanaan_ has been added from Theodotion's edition, for which Aquila translated _merchants_.” Montef. cites: Ἀ. Ο΄. ἔμποροι. Θ. Χαναάν. But the word ἔμποροι in the LXX corresponds to the Hebrew סֹחֲרֶיהָ; and that Aquila rendered not ἔμποροι but μετάβολοι may be conjectured from a comparison of our Hexapla on Zach. xiv. 21. Add that Codd. 22, 36, 48, and others supply in the text οἱ μετάβολοι αὐτῆς, transposing also ἔνδοξοι and ἄρχοντες according to the Hebrew truth.
