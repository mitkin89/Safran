=== Blatt 0511 · S. 495 · —Cap. XXXIII. 20.] JESAIAS.
--- Fußnoten
[16] (Forts.) ἐλυδεώθη I formerly conjectured ἐλιμνώθη, _it turned into a marsh_ (cf. on Cap. xxxv. 7); but now I incline to the view that the pair of readings belongs not to קָמַל but to אֻמְלְלָה, and that for Aquila ὠλιώθη should be restored, which he himself interchanged with the same Hebrew at Jerem. xiv. 2. It must not, however, be concealed that Procopius expressly referred the reading ἀχρεῖος to ἕλη ἐγένετο in a corrupt passage, which should be read thus: ὁ Σαρὼν εἰς ἕλη, φησὶν, ἐγένετο, ἡ ἀχρεῖος, τῆς ἐξ ἀκανθῶν ἐμπλησθεῖσα θρασύτητος.
