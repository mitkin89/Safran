=== Blatt 0504 · S. 488 · JESAIAS. [Cap. XXX. 15–
--- Fußnoten
[38] Procop., who omitted καὶ ἐν ἠρεμίᾳ by accident. We have supplied it from Cod. 88, where καὶ ἐν ἐρημίᾳ is written by a common error. Moreover this codex less well exhibits μεθήσεσθε for σωθήσεσθε, and at the end αὐτῶν for ὑμῶν. (In the text the obelus is in Syro-hex.)
[39] Curter.
[40] Cod. 88.
[41] Procop., who has ἱστία for ἱστίον. Cod. 88 cites: Σ. ἱστίον. Cf. our Hex. on Ezech. xxvii. 7.
[42] Cod. 88, where ἡμῖν is written.
[43] Euseb., Procop. Codd. 22, 36, and others: πάντες οἱ ὑπ. αὐτόν.
[44] Thus Syro-hex. Procop.: τὸ δὲ, ἅγιος, οὐ πρόσκειται παρ’ Ἑβραίοις, οὔτε κατὰ τὰς ἐκδόσεις τῶν ἄλλων.
[45] Curter. Thus without asterisk Codd. 23, 24, 88, and others, and Syro-hex., which notes in the margin: “Τὸ, οὐκ, is not in the other copies.” Procop.: τὸ Ἑβραϊκὸν, οὐκ ἔκλαυσεν, ἔχει.
[46] Curter.
[47] Euseb. Procop. cites: Σ. οὐ κλαύσεις. Θ. οὐ κλαυθήσεται. But Curter.: Θ. οὐ κλαύσετε. Montef., following Nobil., printed κλαυθμῷ twice for κλαυθμὸν, against Euseb.
[48] Thus Codd. 22, 36, and others, from another translator, as it seems.
[49] Procop. In Euseb. and the “MSS.” πρὸς is not read, which Codd. 22, 36, and others insert into the text τῶν Ο΄.
[50] Cod. 88.
[51] Thus Comp., Codd. III, XII (which has μιανεῖς in the margin), 26, 41, and others. Syro-hex. ܘܬܚܪܡܘܢ[?], which to Middeldorpf means καὶ ἐξαρεῖς. But more correctly, as it seems, Cod. C. ܘܬܛܢܦܘܢ[?], καὶ μιανεῖς.
[52] Cod. 88, which for Theod. supports the spelling δυσαλίαν. Cf. on Cap. xxviii. 13. For צֵא, _go out_, the LXX rendered צֹאָה, _excrement_, Aq. and Theod. צֹאָה, _filth_. Cf. on Cap. xxviii. 8. Symmachus' ἄφεδρος is _the monthly purification of women_, and in the LXX it is put for דְּוֺת at Levit. xii. 2.
[53] Cod. 88.
[54] Idem, where κτίσις is erroneously written.
[55] Idem. For ἀναπεποιημένα Syro-hex. has ܚܠܝܛ[?], _mixed_.
