=== Blatt 0563 · S. 547 · –CAP. LVIII. 14.] JESAIAS.

[33] Curter. on πηγὴ ἣν cites in the margin: ※ Οἱ Γ΄. ὕδατος ἧς. Syro-hex. in the margin: ※ ܣ. ܕܡܝ̈ܐ ↙.
[34] Cod. 88.
[35] Thus Ald., Codd. III, 26, 49, and others, with Cyril and Procopius. Jerome: "That which in the Alexandrian copies has been added at the beginning of this chapter: _And still in thee shall be my praise always;_ and at the end: _And thy bones shall spring up as grass, and shall grow fat, and shall possess by inheritance to generation and generations_, is not found in the Hebrew, nor even in the corrected and true copies of the LXX; therefore it should be marked beforehand with an obelus." The interpolation, _And still_ etc., so far as we know, is not found in the present copies; the first verse of the other, καὶ τὰ ὀστᾶ σου ὡς βοτάνη ἀνατελεῖ, is read both in the Hebrew and in the Greek Cap. lxvi. 14.
[36] Curter. Syro-hex. ※ ܐ. ܣ. ܬܩܝܡ ↙. So without asterisk Codd. 22, 23, 36, and others.
[37] Jerome: "In the Hebrew is read, _And there shall be called in thee_ GODER PHERES, which Aquila interpreted, περιφράκτης διακοπῆς, which we may express according to the sense, _he who sets himself against the raging anger of God._" The old reading of Jerome, propagated from Erasmus down to Martianay, is πεφράκτισμα χολῆς; in whose place Montef. produced from his MSS. ΠЄΦΡΑΡΙΗϹΑΙΑΚΟΗϹ, ΠΗΦΡΗΡΙЄϹΝΑΚΟΠΗϹ, and similar rubbish, noting: "That the last word is διακοπῆς, as Sym. also has, we do not doubt . . . but the first word, ΠЄΦΡΑΡΙΗΣ, we do not dare to restore even by conjecture, so corrupt is it." At length it fell to Vallarsi to dig out from the Ambrosian codex, in which there is written in uncial letters ΠЄΡΙΦΡΑΚΤΗϹΑΙΑΚΟΠΗϹ[?], the true reading, which now for the first time obtains its place in the Hexapla. And that no doubt may be left, Cod. 88 expressly cites: Ἀ. καὶ (sic) ἀναστήσεις ※ καὶ καλέσει σοι περιφράκτην.
