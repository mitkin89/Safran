[16] The reading διαλογιζόμενοι, which is found in Codd. Alex. and Jes., is, as it seems, nothing but a false spelling of the other, διαζόμενοι, which Codd. 26, 49, 106, 301 exhibit, and of which ἐργαζόμενοι seems to be a gloss. Rosenmueller on the passage, without even dreaming of the reading διαζόμενοι: “I, following Koppe, would rather read שְׁתֹתֶיהָ, _her weavers_, i.e. of Egypt, from שָׁתַת, which is to be compared with the Syriac ܐܬܡܚ[?], _he wove_ [ἐδιάσατο, Jud. xvi. 13], Chald. [and Hebr.] שְׁתִי, _warp_ . . . This sense the Alexandrians already perceived when they translated: καὶ ἔσονται οἱ ἐργαζόμενοι αὐτὰ (namely λίνον καὶ βύσσον) ἐν ὀδύνῃ.”
