Hieron.: “And what follows [in the Hebrew], _For double confusion, and for shame they shall praise their portion,_ which is not found in the LXX, seems to me to be explained thus. Because you had a double confusion, both over the people of the Jews, which had departed from God, and over the nations, which served idols, you shall see them converted to the fear of God praising _their portion;_ no doubt the Lord.”
