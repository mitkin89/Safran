[19] Codd. 86, 88, which exhibit ἡμῶν and ἡμᾶς (twice), except that Cod. 86 has ὑμᾶς at the end. In Cod. 88 ἀποβαλόμενοι is written.
[20] Cod. 88. It belongs to the Hebr. מְנַדֵּיכֶם, and is perhaps a scholion.
[21] Codd. 86, 88 cite: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. διὰ τὸ ὄ. μου· δοξασθήτω (διὰ τὸ ὄ. μου δοξασθήτω· Cod. 88) καὶ ὀψ. τὴν δόξαν καὶ εὐφροσύνην. We have corrected from Procopius, who cites: Σ. καὶ ὀψόμεθα ἐν τῇ εὐφ. ὑμῶν.
[22] Cod. 86 in the margin without a name. Perhaps it is Aquila's, as on Cap. lix. 18.
[23] Syro-hex. in the margin ܓ[?]. ܕܝܠܗ ◆ (Cod. C. ※). Thus in the text without the asterisk Cod. 23. Codd. 22, 36, and others have αὐτῷ for αὐτοῦ.
[24] Cod. 88.
[25] Curter. For ἔσωσεν we have taken διέσωσεν from Cod. 88, which cites: Σ. διέσωσεν ἄρσεν. In the text the obelus is in Syro-hex.
[26] Curter. at εἰ καὶ obscurely notes: ΟΜΩΣ ΟΙ Γ΄. ΟΙ Ο΄ Η. ΟΜΩΣ ΤΕ. Α. Θ.; from which Montef. extracted a hexaplaric note: Ἀ. Ο΄. Θ. ἢ καί . . . Σ. εἰ καί . . . But in Parsons' MSS. Curterius' reading is given thus: Οἱ Γ΄. ὁμοίως. Οἱ Ō. ἤ. Σ. ὁμοίως. Ἀ. Θ. εἰ. The note Οἱ Γ΄. ὁμοίως may indicate that the three translators expressed the copula; although I should believe that Aquila preferred εἰ ἐτέχθη, which is the reading of Cod. Alex. For εἰ καὶ ἐτέχθη stand Comp., Codd. XII, 86, 97, and others, and Syro-hex.

[29] Procop.
