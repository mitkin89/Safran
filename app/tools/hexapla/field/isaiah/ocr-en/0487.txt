More truly Jerome: “For _labana_, that is, _moon_, the LXX rendered _brick_, which in Hebrew is called LEBENA [לְבֵנָה], deceived by the ambiguity of the word. Again, for HAMMA, that is, _heat_, by which _the sun_ is understood, they put _wall_, which in Hebrew is called HOMA [חוֹמָה].” But these words, καὶ ἐντραπήσεται κ. τ. ἑ., Curter. cites in the margin without a name, as if to be inserted after τὸ τεῖχος, as they are read in Comp., Ald., Codd. 36, 48, 51, and others.
