[27] Cod. 88. Procop. says: Τινὲς δὲ τὸ τῆς χειροτονίας ῥητὸν οὕτως ἐκθέμενοι· ἐὰν ἀφέλῃς ἀπὸ σοῦ σύνδεσμον, ἐκτείνοντα δάκτυλον, καὶ λαλοῦντα ἀνωφελὲς, ἐπήγαγον; where the readings of the three translators lie confused. Montef. nevertheless printed: Ἀ. ἐὰν ἀφέλῃς ἀ. σ. σύνδεσμον, ἐκτείνοντα δ. καὶ λ. ἀνωφελές; merely noting that σύνδεσμον had crept in here from the version of Symmachus, and that from Jerome we should read πλάνην [rather σφαλμὸν], or something similar. The passage of Jerome is: "The Hebrew word MOTA, which in Jeremiah [xxvii. 2] is interpreted _iron collar_, is read twice in the present chapter. For in that place where above, following the LXX, we said: _Loose the obligations of violent exchanges_, let us know that for the one word MOTA they put _violent bonds_, for which Aq. translated _error_, Sym. _turning aside_, Theod. κλοιὸν, that is, _collar_. Again in the present place where we have rendered: _If thou take away the chain from the midst of thee_, and for _chain_ the LXX translated σύνδεσμον, that is, _binding together_, or _bond_; Aq., as above, interpreted _error_; Sym., going over to the opinion of Theodotion, put _collar_." The same a little after: "And χειροτονίαν, for which the three other translators with one voice translated _the outstretched finger_."
