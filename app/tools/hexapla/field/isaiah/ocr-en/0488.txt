--- Fußnoten
[3] We have not set down the beginning of this v. according to the LXX version, ὡς ἄνθρωποι ὀλιγόψυχοι, because it seems rather to belong to the end of v. 4, כְּזֶרֶם קִיר. Here Jerome must by all means be heard: “According to the LXX translators I could not find in this passage, I do not say the sense, but the order and sequence of the words.”
[4] After παρέδωκας Curter. cites in the margin: ※ ** γενῶν καταισχυνεῖς κ. τ. ἑ. That the first word was ἀλλογενῶν Drusius perceived, and he is confirmed by Syro-hex., which moreover prefixes a word corresponding to the Hebrew שְׁאוֹן, in Greek perhaps φθόγγον. The Syriac is: ܬ. ܩܠܐ[?] ܕܢܘܟܪ̈ܝܐ ܬܒܗܬ[?] ܐܝܟ[?] ܫܘܒܐ[?] ܒܛܠܠܐ[?] ܕܥܢܢܐ[?] ܫܒܘܚܬܐ[?] (Cod. C. ܫܒܘ̈ܚܬܐ[?]) ܕܥܫܝܢ̈ܐ ܬܡܟܟ ↙. Codd. 22, 23, 36, and others insert into the text after παρέδωκας: καύσωνα (or καύσων) — ταπεινώσεις (or ταπεινώσει).
[5] Curter. in the margin: ※ ταπεινώσεις. Syro-hex., as above, ܬܡܟܟ. The second person is in Codd. 22, 93; all the rest read ταπεινώσει. Montef. wrongly printed: תַּכְנִיעַ. ※ ταπεινώσεις.
[6] Euseb., Procop.
[7] These words, πότον — τρυγιῶν, are inserted into the text after ἐπὶ τὸ ὄρος τοῦτο by Codd. 22 (under ※), 36, 48 (under ÷), and others. Montef., from misunderstood notes of Curterius and Drusius, puts forward false readings: Ἀ. πότον λιπασμάτων, (πότον) διυλισμένον. Σ. πότον τρυγιῶν, (πότον) διυλισμένων.

[9] Curter. Syro-hex. in the margin in middle-sized characters: ܘܒܡܫܠܡܢܘܬܐ[?] ܢܫܬܠܡܘܢ, ܒܛܘܪܐ ܗܢܐ ↙.
[10] Procop. Jerome: “For which Symmachus translated, _the face of the ruler who rules over all peoples._
[11] Procop.: ἀντὶ δὲ τοῦ προσώπου τοῦ ἐξουσιαστοῦ, τὸ πρόσωπον τῆς σκοτίας Σύμμαχος (rather Ἀκύλας) τε καὶ Θεοδοτίων ἐξέδωκαν. Jerome: “For what we have translated, _the face of the bond bound over all peoples_, Aq. rendered _the face of darkness_, (_of darkness_) _over all peoples_.” The repetition of the word _of darkness_ is demanded both by the Hebrew truth and by Jerome's own words which follow: “And whereas Aq. said _darkness_ twice, Theod. named _darkness_ once, the rest alike.” As for the remainder, we have assigned καὶ καταποντίσει to Aquila on account of v. 8.
