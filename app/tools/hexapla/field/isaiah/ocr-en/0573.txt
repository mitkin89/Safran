=== Blatt 0573 · S. 557 · –Cap. LXIII. 4.] JESAIAS.
--- Fußnoten
CAP. LXIII.
[1] Curter. Hieron.: “_With dyed garments from Bosra._ Which many, falling into error, think is to be understood of the flesh of the Lord: that is, BASAR; but if this word meant _flesh_, it would be written with the middle letter SIN, whereas now it is written with SADE, and is interpreted _firm_ and _fortified._”
[2] Cod. 88, which has ἀπὸ βορρα (sic).
[3] Curter., Codd. 23, 88, and without the asterisk Comp., Codd. 22, 48, and others.
[4] Cod. 88.
[5] The same. Cf. Hex. on Jerem. xlviii. 12.
[6] The same. In the codex there is βιαίνων, which is no word. For צֹעֶה Sym. seems to have read צֹעֵד, comparing Hex. on Psal. lxvii. 8. In the LXX text Cod. 198 prefixes διαβαίνων.
[7] Procop., where ὑπερμαχῶ is printed. Euseb. explains: ὑπερμαχῶν γὰρ, κατὰ τὸν Σύμμαχον, τῶν ἀδίκως ὑπὸ τῶν ἀντικειμένων δυνάμεων καταπονουμένων, κ.τ.ἑ.
[8] Curter. from the Jesuit Codex exhibits πατήματος in the text, but πατητοῦ in the margin. Parsons., however, testifies of the same codex: “ὡς ἀπὸ πατήματος (in the margin add. τοῦ) XII.” Finally Montef. falsely cites from Curterius: Ο΄. ὡς ἀπὸ ※ πατητοῦ ↙ ληνοῦ.
[9] Cod. 88.
[10] The same in continuation. That is, Aq. rendered פָּרוּר, _pot._ Unless credence is to be given to Jerome, who says: “The word PHURA is ambiguous, and very often signifies both _winepress_ and _small flask._”
[11] Hieron.: “For _winepress_, which in Hebrew is called GETH, Theod. put the Hebrew word itself, PHURA. But Sym. is better in this passage, whom we too have followed.”
[12] Hieron.: “The LXX, for what we have said, _I have trodden the winepress alone,_ rendered _full, trodden down,_ which is rather to be read with the preceding section than to be the beginning of the following.” Therefore he had in his copy ληνοῦ πλήρους καταπεπατημένης, which is the reading of Comp., Ald., Codd. 22, 23, 36, 41, and others. Thus S. Cyprian. Epist. LXIII to Caecilius: “_As from the treading_ (πατήματος) _of a winepress full and trodden down._”
[13] Curter. without the name of the translator. Procop. cites: Σ. καὶ ἀπὸ τῶν λαῶν. These words, ληνὸν ἐπάτησα μονώτατος, Codd. 22, 36, and others insert after καταπεπατημένης.
[14] Cod. 88. Codd. 22, 36, and others in the text: ἐν τῇ ὀργῇ μου.
[15] Cod. 88.
[16] Procop. Cod. 86 cites: Σ. τὸ ἐπινίκιον αὐτῶν.
[17] Cod. 86. Moreover Cod. 93 cites: Θ. Σ. καὶ ἐρραντίσθη τῷ κατανικήματι αὐτῶν τὰ ἱμάτιά μου, καὶ πάντα τὰ ἐνδύματά μου ἐμόλυνα; which are read word for word in the text after εἰς γῆν in Codd. 22, 36, and others, some of which have ἐρράνθη for ἐρραντίσθη. Theodoret. also (Opp. T. I, p. 1068) cites: καὶ κατήγαγον εἰς γῆν τὸ αἷμα αὐτῶν, καὶ κατερραντίσθη τῷ καταν. αὐτῶν τὰ ἱμ. μου.
[18] Curter. Thus in the text Codd. 23 (under ※), 109, 302, 305. Syro-hex. in the margin ܬ. ܘܟܠܗܘܢ ܠܒܘ̈ܫܝ ܛܘܫܬ[?] ↙.
