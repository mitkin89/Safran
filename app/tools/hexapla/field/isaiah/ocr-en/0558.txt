[10] Cod. 88. Jerome: "_But draw near hither, ye sons of the sorceress_ . . . For _iniquity_, or _of the wicked_, which the LXX translated, Theod. put the Hebrew word itself, ONENA, which we, following Sym., have rendered _sorceress_." In the Hexapla edited by him P. B. Drach notes on the passage: "The editors of the works of St. Jerome, and after them Montef., wrongly wrote 'in _auguratricem_' for '_inauguratricem_.' Moreover the Greek word of Symmachus, ἐνοιωνιστρίας, which Drusius and Montef. did not know, and which we have inserted into our text, was supplied to us by L. Bos in his notes." He is mistaken. For Jerome undoubtedly wrote _auguratricem_, not _inauguratricem;_ and the word ἐνοιωνίστρια is not only not Symmachus', but not even Greek. See our Preface.
