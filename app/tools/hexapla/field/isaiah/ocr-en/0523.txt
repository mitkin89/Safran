=== Blatt 0523 · S. 507 · —Cap. XXXVIII. 20.] JESAIAS.
--- Fußnoten
[39] (Forts.) Aq. interpreted _horse Agor_, Theod. SIS AGOR . . . but Sym. translated thus, _like a shut-in swallow_ [ἐγκλεισθεῖσα, someone suggests] _so will I sing._" Cod. 88 cites the note: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. ἀγροῦ ἐγκεκλεισμένη; which with the help of Jerome may be read thus: Ἀ. Θ. ἀγούρ. Σ. ἐγκεκλεισμένη. (With Sym. may be compared the Targ.: _like a captured_ (דַּאֲחִיד) _and chirping swallow._) Cf. Hex. on Jerem. viii. 7.
[40] Codd. 86, 88. Cf. on Cap. x. 14.
[41] Codd. 86, 88: Ἀ. ωραιωθησαν (sic) οἱ ὀφθ. μου (μου om. Cod. 88) κ.τ.ἑ. We corrected it to ἠραιώθησαν in _Otio Norvic._ p. 49. Cf. Hex. on Jerem. xxxix. 10.
[42] Curter. Cod. 23 cites: καὶ εἶπα αὐτῷ, καὶ αὐτὸς — ψυχῆς μου. Syro-hex. in the margin: ※ οὕτως (ܗܟܢܐ) καὶ αὐτὸς ἐποίησε καθοδηγήσει (ܡܕܒܪܢܘܬܐ[?]) κ.τ.ἑ. Jerome cites only: _and he himself did it._
