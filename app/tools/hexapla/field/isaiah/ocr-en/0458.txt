[21] Curter. gives: ※ Θ. σπέρμα κ.τ.ἑ. Procop.: Θ. μὲν γὰρ ἔφη, τὸ (sic) σπέρμα ἅγιον τὸ στήλωμα αὐτῆς· ὁ δὲ Ἀ. στήλωσις ἔφησεν· ὁ δὲ Σ., ἡ στάσις αὐτῆς. Hieron.: “What we have said, _The holy seed shall be what stands in it_, or according to Aquila, _The holy seed shall be its shoot_, is not found in the LXX translators, but was added by Origen from the Hebrew and the edition of Theodotion.” The same is attested by S. Basil. and Procop. The reading of Theodotion is in the text of Comp., Ald., Codd. 22, 23 (under an asterisk), 36, 48, others. Erroneously Cod. 88: Θ. σπέρμα ἅγιον τὸ πλήρωμα αὐτῆς. Syro-hex. gives in the margin without a name: ܙܪܥܐ ܩܕܝܫܐ ܢܨܒܬܐ ܕܝܠܗ܀; but for ܢܨܒܬܐ, φυτὸν, or φύτευμα, Cod. C. has ܩܘܡܐ[?], which is interchanged with the Greek ὑπόστασις.
