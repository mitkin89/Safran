=== Blatt 0497 · S. 481 · –Cap. XXVIII. 27.] JESAIAS.
--- Fußnoten
[56] Cod. Barber. Montef., for ὥστε μὴ ἀναπεσεῖν, carelessly copied εἰς τὸ μὴ ἀναπεσεῖν, wrongly translating _that it may not fall down_ instead of _that it may not recline._ Then after σκηνὴ the word στενὴ seems to have fallen out by accident.
[57] Cod. Barber., which has συναφθῆναι [χ above φ] (sic). Moreover κηδίασις used to be printed, which is no word at all. The Lexicons of the O. T. do indeed cite a verb κηδιάω, _I take care_, from Sirac. xxii. 13, καὶ μὴ κηδιάσῃς ἐν τῇ ἀπονοίᾳ αὐτοῦ; but it is a false reading for ἀκηδιάσῃς. For κηδίασις, by a very slight change, we have printed ϏΗΔΙΑϹΙϹ, i.e. καὶ ἡ δίασις, _and the warping_, according to our conjecture proposed in _Otium Norvic._ p. 23. Cf. on Cap. xxx. 1.
[58] Curter., who for διακοπῶν in both readings erroneously has διακοπτων. But Montef. assigns διακοπῶν to Aquila, διακοπτόντων to Symmachus and Theod., although he admits that in Cod. Barber. there is plainly read: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. διακοπῶν. Syro-hex. also cites: ܣ. ܬ. ܐܝܟ ܕܒܛܘܪܐ ܕܬܘܪ̈ܥܬܐ[?] ܀ Moreover, in the LXX text Codd. 22, 36, and others insert after ἀσεβῶν: ὥσπερ ἐν τῷ ὄρει τῶν διακοπῶν; from which we have taken ὥσπερ, which was missing.
[59] Thus Comp., Codd. III, XII, 22, 23, 26, and others, and Syro-hex. Jerome: _bitterness._
