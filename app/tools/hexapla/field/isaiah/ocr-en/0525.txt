[14] Curter., who has ἐξηράνθη χόρτος without the article, against all the other witnesses. Codd. 86, 88 exhibit: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. ※ὅτι πν. (om. κυρίου) ἔπνευσεν εἰς (ἐπ’ Cod. 88) αὐτόν· ἀληθῶς κ.τ.έ. Nearly the same is in the text of Codd. 23, 109, 302, 305; and also, with ὅμοιος χόρτῳ for χόρτος in the first place, Codd. 22, 36, and others. Euseb. in his Commentary on the Psalms, p. 275, having cited the reading τῶν Ο΄, continues: οἷς ὁ προφήτης κατὰ τοὺς λοιποὺς ἑρμηνευτὰς προστίθησι λέγων· ἀληθῶς χ. ὁ λαός· ἐξηράνθη ὁ χ., ἐξέπεσε τὸ ἄνθος. Finally Jerome: "This, which is marked with asterisks: _Because the spirit of God blew upon it; truly the people is grass: the grass withered, the flower fell_, has been added from the Hebrew and the edition of Theodotion. From which it is manifest that it was either passed over by the LXX, or gradually lost through the fault of the scribes, since both the preceding and the following verse end in _flower._" Syro-hex., surprisingly, has silently imitated the defective reading.

Do not doubt my emendation. It is a fact, not a conjecture. Apollo never divined better." Less candidly Montef. notes: "Ἀ. πράσον ὁ λαός. Procopius."
