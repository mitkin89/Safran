Whether the word ἀποσιρωτὸν, or ἀποσηρωτὸν, is to be ascribed to Aquila, that wondrous artificer of such monstrosities, I do not know; in that case the preposition would represent the first letter of the Hebrew word (as מְזִמָּה, ἀπ-έννοια in Hex. on Psal. cxxxviii. 20), while σιρωτὸν, or rather σειρωτὸν (from σειρὰ, _chain_, _rope_, _line_), would be referred to the Chaldee שׁוּרָה, _row_, _series_, _line_. Let this suffice to illustrate an obscure word unknown to the lexicographers, until something more probable is brought forward.
