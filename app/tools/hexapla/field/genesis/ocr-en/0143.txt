=== Blatt 0143 · S. 31 · –Cap. XIV. 23.] GENESIS.
--- Fußnoten
[5] (Forts.) it seemed that πρινιδίων, or πρίνων, _of holm-oaks_, should be restored, the trees with which groves are mostly planted. But it is strange that the learned man did not perceive that under πρινηώνων there lies hidden, scarcely, nay not even scarcely, the good Greek word πρινεώνων, from πρινεὼν, _a place planted with holm-oaks_, of the same form as ῥοδεὼν, μυρσινεὼν, παπυρεὼν, etc. That Aquila, in so rendering, had in mind the Syriac word ܦܪܝܢܝܐ[?] or ܦܪܝܢܐ[?], _holm-oak_, which we touched upon at Jesai. xliv. 14, can hardly be doubted. Lagarde had hit upon the same emendation before us; for τῶν περιπεδίνων he adduced from his books ΤΩΝΑ. ΑΠΡΙΝΕΑΙΝΟΝ, ΤΩΝΑΛΠΡΙΝΕΑΙΝΟΝ, and ΤΩΝΠΡΙΝΕΩΗΩΝ. As regards Theodotion, although ἀλσῶν is not bad in itself, yet since in the same books, both of Vallarsi and, if we infer rightly, of Lagarde, ἀκτῶν is written, a word which we proved in Hex. on Cap. ii. 15 to contain the notion of _pleasantness_, we have, following the same Lagarde, claimed this for Jerome here too.

[7] At Ὀμμαίους F. C. Alter transcribed from Cod. 130: Σ. Ζοιζομμεὶν, noting: “And thus Sym. seems to favour the reading [of Ald. (with Σομαίους), Codd. But the reading Ζοιζομμεὶν without doubt belongs to זוּזִים, a people which Sym., together with modern interpreters, seems to have believed to be the same as the one which, called זַמְזֻמִּים by the Ammonites, is reckoned among the Rephaim in Deut. ii. 20. Hieron.: “ZUZIM and EMIM, moreover, are interpreted _terrible_ and _dreadful_; for which the LXX, translating the sense rather than word for word, put _very strong nations_. Further, BAEM, for which they said ἅμα αὐτοῖς, that is, _with them_, they thought was written with HE, misled by the similarity of the letter, whereas it is written [in only a few copies] with HETH . . . that is, _in Ham._”
