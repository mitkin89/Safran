Of the readings attributed to Aquila we have set down the one which is both Greek and best attested, although we do not altogether rest content with it. One misses καταφορὰ (cf. Hex. on Gen. ii. 21. Jesai. xxix. 10), which Montef. edited without a suitable witness. For Jerome, to whom he appeals, merely says: “For _ecstasy_ there is read in the Hebrew THARDEMA, that is, καταφορὰ, which above we translated as _deep sleep._”
