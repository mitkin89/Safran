=== Blatt 0170 · S. 58 · GENESIS. [CAP. XLI. 2–
--- Fußnoten
[2] Hieron.: "Twice in Genesis _achi_ is written, and it is neither a Greek nor a Latin word. But the Hebrew word itself has also been corrupted; for it is said _in_ AHU, that is, _in the marsh_. But because the letters VAU and IOD are similar among the Hebrews and differ only in size, the LXX translators rendered _achi_ for AHU, and, according to their custom of expressing a double aspiration, they joined the Greek letter CHI to the Hebrew letter HETH." The same, in the Comment. on Jes. xix. 7: "When I asked the learned what ἄχει meant, I heard from Egyptians that in their language everything green that grows in the marsh is called by this name."
