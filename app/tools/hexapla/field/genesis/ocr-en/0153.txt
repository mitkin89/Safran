[4] Hieron.: “Although Isaac sowed in a foreign land, yet I do not think that he had so great a yield of _barley_. Hence I think that is better which is found in the Hebrew, and which Aq. also rendered: _And he found in that year a hundredfold by estimate_, that is, ἑκατὸν εἰκασμούς [so Cod. Frising. in Lagarde, supported by two of Vallarsi's MSS., which write ΕΚΑΤΟΙΚΑϹΝωΥϹ and ΕΚΑΤΟΝΕΙΔΑϹΜΟΤΕ, instead of what used to be printed, ἑκ. εἰκασμένον]. For although _estimate_ and _barley_ are written with the same letters, yet _estimates_ are read SAARIM, but _barley_ SORIM.” The Graeco-Ven. translator: ἑκατὸν εἰκασμόν (perhaps εἰκασμῶν). Cf. Hex. ad Prov. xxiii. 7.

[8] Hieron.: “For _enmities_, which Aq. and Sym. rendered τὴν ἀντικειμένην and ἐναντίωσιν, that is, _adverse_ and _contrary_, the Hebrew has SATANA; from which we understand that Satan is interpreted _the adversary_.” On Aquila (who, I suspect, wrote ἀντικειμένη without the article) cf. Hex. ad Psal. cviii. 6. Zach. iii. 2. On the matter cf. Joseph. Ant. I, 18, 2: Τῶν δὲ προτέρων τὸ μὲν Ἔσκον καλεῖται· μάχης ἄν τις αὐτὸ φήσειε· τὸ δὲ ἕτερον Σιτεννά· ἔχθραν ἀποσημαίνει τὸ ὄνομα.
[9] Cod. X (with συνέτερος). Hieron.: “For _Ochozath the groomsman_ the Hebrew has _the company_ [rather _the possession_] _of his friends;_ so that it signifies not so much a man as the crowd of friends who had come with the king, among whom was also Phicol, the captain of his army.” Onkelos agrees, explaining: וְסִיעַת מְרַחֲמוֹהִי, _and the company of his friends._ Graeco-Ven. took both names as appellatives; its version runs: κατοχή τε τοῦ φίλου οἱ, καὶ στόμα παντὸς ἄρχοντος τῆς κείνου στρατιᾶς.
