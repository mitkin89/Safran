(On this part of the Colbertine Codex, about which Holmes's amanuensis is entirely silent, Grabe notes: "Some verses being again wanting [after Cap. xxxix. 21, καθὰ συνέταξε κύοιος[?] τῷ Μ.], a new leaf, badly torn or mutilated, presents on its front side the pericope from v. 37 of the same chapter, σκεύη αὐτῆς, up to Ἀαρὼν v. 41, with only a few words or syllables eaten away or cut off.") Of the remaining witnesses, Syro-hex. cites: τὸ χαλκοῦν αὐτῶν (ܕܝܠܗܘܢ), τοὺς ἀν. αὐτοῦ; Cod. 58: τὸ χαλκοῦν σὺν αὐτῷ (omitting what follows up to τὸν λουτῆρα κ. τ. ἑ.); Cod. 72: τὸ χαλκοῦν, τοὺς ἀν. αὐτοῦ (taking no account of the Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ). Finally, after τὸ χαλκοῦν Arab. 1, 2 add _to it_.
