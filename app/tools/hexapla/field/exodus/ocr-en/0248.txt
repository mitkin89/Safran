[23] Codd. 85 (on the testimony of Montef.), 130. Nobil. cites: Schol. τὸν ἀντιδάκτυλον. A little before, on λοβὸν Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand: τὸ ἄκρον, ἤγουν τὸ ἁπαλόν. Cf. Kimchi in Gesen. _Thes. Ling. Hebr._ p. 1511.
[24] So Syro-hex., and without hexaplaric marks, omitting also what lies under the obelus, Cod. 72, except that in the first place it wrongly gives τῶν υἱῶν αὐτῶν for τῶν υἱῶν αὐτοῦ.
[25] So Syro-hex., and without marks Codd. 58, 72 (with ἐπιχεεῖς), 85 (in the margin), 128, Arab. 1, 2 (both without κύκλῳ), Arm. 1.
[26] So Cod. 72, Syro-hex.
[27] The obelus is in Syro-hex. Cod. 58 rejects the clause.
[28] So Syro-hex. (which marks confusedly: ※ Ἑβρ. καὶ τὴν ↙ κέρκον αὐτοῦ ↙ (sic)), and without marks Codd. 72, 85 (with καὶ τὴν κ. in the margin), 128, 130 (as 85), 131 (with καὶ τὴν κ. written above), Arab. 1, 2.
[29] Nobil., Codd. 85 (on the testimony of Montef.), 130. One Regius in Montef.: οἷον (perhaps οἱ λ̥.) τὴν κνήμην.
[30] So Syro-hex., and without marks Codd. 58 (with κολλούραν in the text, and κολλουρίδα in the margin by the same hand), 72, 131 (with καὶ κολλύραν ἄρτου written above), Arab. 1, 2.
[31] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܐܢܘܢ. ܣ. ܬ. ܐܢܝܢ܀. In the text αὐτοὺς is had by Ald., Codd. III, VII (corrected from αὐτοῖς), X, 14, 15, 16, and others (among them 85 in the margin).
[32] The same: ܐ. ܕܡܘܠܝܐ. ܣ. ܬ. ܕܫܘ̈ܡܠܝܐ.
[33] So Syro-hex., and without marks Cod. 72, Arab. 1, 2. In the text τῶν υἱῶν Ἰσρ. is omitted by Comp., Ald., Codd. VII, X, 14, 15, 16, and others; in their place Codd. X (in the margin), 74, 75, and others have αὐτῶν.
[34] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܠܡܡܠܐ. ܣ. ܠܡܫܬܡܠܝܘ܀.
[35] So Syro-hex., and without asterisk Cod. 72. Arm. 1: καὶ τελειῶσαι ÷ ἐν αὐτοῖς.
[36] Nobil., Cod. X. Cod. 58 in the margin without name: ἐξιλασμοῦ. Less probably Cod. 130 in the margin without name: ἐξίλασμα. The full reading of the three translators was perhaps: τῇ ἡμέρᾳ ἐπὶ (or περὶ) τοῦ ἐξιλασμοῦ. Cf. on Cap. xxx. 10.
[37] Cod. X. Cf. Hex. ad Lev. ix. 15. Ezech. xliii. 20. Erroneously Codd. 57 (with οἱ ὅλοι), 85, 130: Οἱ λοιποί· περὶ ἁμαρτίας. Also one Regius in Montef.: Ἀ. Σ. Θ. ἐξιλασμοῦ περὶ ἁμαρτίας.
[38] Nobil., Codd. 85 (on the testimony of Montef.), 130.
