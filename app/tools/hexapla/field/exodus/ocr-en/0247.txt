=== Blatt 0247 · S. 135 · EXODUS. –Cap. XXIX. 18.]
--- Fußnoten
[2] (Forts.) hex., and without marks (with ἄρτον ἄζυμον) Cod. 72, Arab. 1, 2. Codd. 85, 130, both in the margin: Ο΄. κολύρας (sic) ἀζύμους. Nobil. on the following clause, καὶ λάγανα ἄζυμα, cites: Schol. κολλύρας ἀζύμους ἀναμεμιγμένας; from which Montef. unskilfully made a hexaplaric note: Ο΄. λάγανα ἄζυμα κεχρισμένα ἐν ἐλαίῳ. Ἀ. κολλύρας ἀζύμους ἀναμεμιγμένας ἐν ἐλαίῳ. Θ. ἀναπεποιημένους (sic).
[3] Codd. 85, 130: Θ. ἀναπεποιημένους. Ἀ. ἀναμεμιγμένους, the reading having been adapted, as it seems, to the masculine πεφυραμένους.
[4] So Syro-hex., and without marks Cod. III. Evidently it is credible that for אֶת־הַבְּגָדִים Symmachus put not τὰς στολὰς but τὰ ἱμάτια.
[5] So Syro-hex., and without asterisk Codd. 85, 130, both in the margin, prefixed by Ο΄. The supplement τοῦ ἐπενδύματος is in Codd. 14, 16, 25, and others.
[6] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܟܘܬܝܢܐ ܘܡܥܛܦܢܐ[?] ܕܗܘ[?] ܕܠܥܠ ܡܢ ܟܘܬܢܐ. ܣ. ܟܘܬܝܢܐ ܘܠܥܠ[?] ܡܢ ܟܘܬܢܐ ܕܠܥܠ ܡܢ ܟܘܬܢܐ܀ ܬ. ܟܘܬܝܢܐ ܘܠܥܠ[?] ܡܢ ܟܘܬܢܗ̇ ܕܟܬܦܬܐ[?]܀. The Greek of Symmachus and Theodotion is extant in Nobil., Codd. 85 (on the testimony of Montef.), 130 (which wrongly writes τὸν (sic) ἐπένδυμα for τὸν ἐπενδύτην). On Aq. cf. on Cap. xxviii. 31. On Theod. cf. Hex. ad Job. i. 20. Jesai. lix. 17. Ezech. xxvi. 16.
[7] Codd. 85, 130 (with Ο΄. for Ο΄. Ἀ.). So in the text Codd. 58, 72, 128.
[8] Nobil., Cod. 130 (with ἀφορισμός). The reading recalls Aquila rather than Symmachus, comparing Hex. ad Levit. xxi. 12. 2 Reg. i. 10. 4 Reg. xi. 12. Psal. cxxxi. 18.
[9] So Syro-hex., and without asterisk Cod. 72, Arab. 1, 2 (with τῶν υἱῶν), Arm. 1 (the same). Codd. 85 (without ταῖς), 130, both in the margin: Ο΄. ταῖς ζώναις Ἀαρών.
[10] Nobil. cites: Ἀ. διὰ ζώνην Ἀαρών; for which Montef. printed from one Regius and the Basil.: Ἀ. διαζωνὴ (sic) Ἀαρὼν, against the grammar of the language, unless I am mistaken. Perhaps more correctly Cod. 130: Ἀ. ζώνῃ Ἀαρών.
[11] Codd. 85, 130, and "two Regii."
[12] Nobil., Codd. 85, 130.
[13] Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand. Cf. Hex. ad Prov. x. 25.
[14] Cod. X, with which the Graeco-Ven. translator agrees. Cf. Hex. ad Levit. viii. 25. ix. 10. Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand: τὸ κατώτερον μέρος τοῦ ἥπατος.
[15] Cod. VII, written above by a second hand. Before καὶ ἐπιθήσεις Codd. 14, 16, 25, and others insert θυμιάσεις into the text.
[16] So Comp., Codd. 58, 71, 85, 128, 129. Codd. 85, 130, both in the margin: Ο΄. ἁμαρτίας (γάρ ἐστι).
[17] Codd. 85 (without ἐστὶν), 130 (with Ἀ. for Ἀ. Θ.).
[18] Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand.
[19] Cod. 32 in the text. I do not know whether this more recherché reading should be ascribed to Symmachus.
[20] Cod. VII in the margin by a second hand. Cf. Hex. ad Levit. iv. 8.
[21] So Comp., Ald., Codd. II (by a second hand), III, VII, 14, 15, 16, and others, Syro-hex.
[22] "From one Regius, and from the Roman Edition [where there is only: Schol. Οἱ ἄλλοι· εὐαρεστήσεως, from Theodoret, as it seems, in Quaest. LXII on Exod. xxix. 25]. Aquila and Theodotion are accustomed to render אִשֶּׁה by πύρρον [sic], as you may see throughout Leviticus."—_Montef._ On the spelling of the word πυρὸν see our note in Hex. ad Levit. ii. 9.
