In reconstructing the Greek, of which not even a trace survives, we have had Ceriani as our guide, except that for Sym. we have tried κελευόντων instead of ἐντελλομένων, a word which, though more rarely than its derivatives κέλευσμα and κελευστὴς, is used of _the shout by which men are commonly roused to carry out this or that task_. Cf. Hex. ad Jerem. xlviii. 33.
