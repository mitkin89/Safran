"It is strange, then, that not everything [from καὶ ἀνήνεγκεν (v. 29) to τῷ Μωυσῇ (v. 32)] is inserted under the asterisk, indeed that some things are even under the obelus, which therefore must have been present in Origen's LXX. But since the very ancient Cod. V too . . . agrees with ours in the hexaplaric marks, except that it omits the last obelus, and Arm. 1 supports both with its imperfect system of marks, it seems to me that Origen had fuller LXX codices than the others which are now available to us."—_Ceriani._
