[19] Thus Codd. 22, 34, 36, and others, Theodoret. (who has τελεσθῆναι). The version ἕως τοῦ παλαιωθῆναι (לְבַלֵּא) τὸ π. seems to be of another translator, whom Tertull. adv. Judaeos, p. 294 imitated: “_until the transgression grow old_ (_and sins be sealed up_).”
[20] Cod. 87, Syro-hex.
[21] Euseb. ibid. pp. 384, 385.
[22] Cod. 87. Syro-hex. ܘܠܡܒܨܪܘ ܥܘܠܐ., _and that iniquity may be diminished._ (As for the critics (e. g. Schleusner. in _Nov. Thes._ s. v. σπανίζω, Gesen. in _Thes. Ling. Hebr._ p. 538) who judged that σφραγίσαι should be restored for σπανίσαι, appealing to Athanasius and Theodoret (authors who, however, had in their hands the version of Theodotion, not that of the LXX), I wonder that they did not call Eusebius as a witness, who in Dem. Evang. p. 385 expressly names the Elders: ἐτέλεσαν τὴν ἀθεσίαν, καὶ ἐτελείωσαν τὴν ἑαυτῶν ἁμαρτίαν, κατὰ τὴν Ἀκύλου ἑρμηνείαν· κατὰ δὲ τοὺς Ο΄, ἐδέθη αὐτῶν καὶ ἘΣΦΡΑΓΊΣΘΗ ἡ ἁμαρτία. But it is more than certain that this writer too had in mind the version of Theodotion, which alone was sanctioned by the Church in the book of Daniel.)
[23] Euseb. ibid. p. 384.
[24] Thus Comp., Ald., Codd. III, XII, 22, 33, and others, Euseb., Theodoret. The double version of Theodotion is attested with remarkable agreement by the codices as well as by the authors, except Theodoret according to Holmes' Cod. 229, where by accident, as it seems, the clause καὶ ἀπαλεῖψαι τὰς ἀδικίας is wanting. Tertull. l. c. has only: “_and righteousnesses be entreated_” (corr. “_unrighteousnesses_”).
[25] Euseb. ibid. p. 385.
[26] Syro-hex., and without the obelus Cod. 87.
[27] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܘܠܡܝܬܝܘ ܙܕܝܩܘܬܐ ܕܠܥܠܡ܀[?]
[28] Cod. 87, the metobelus being absent. Syro-hex. reads and marks: καὶ σ. τὸ ὅραμα ※ καὶ προφήτας, καὶ εὐφρᾶναι ↙ ἅ. ἁ. On προφήτας the Syriac cites in the margin: προφητείαν (ܢܒܝܘܬܐ); and on καὶ εὐφρᾶναι: καὶ τοῦ χρῖσαι (ܘܠܡܡܫܚ). Euseb. ibid. p. 387: δύναται δὲ καὶ κατὰ τὴν τῶν Ο΄ ἑρμηνείαν τοιαύτην ἔχειν διάνοιαν τὸ, τοῦ σφραγίσαι ὅρασιν καὶ προφήτην; whence Montef. wrongly gave: Ο΄. Θ. καὶ τοῦ σφραγίσαι—ἅγιον ἁγίων.
[29] Euseb. ibid. pp. 386, 387, passed over by Montfaucon by some accident or other.
[30] Cod. 87, Syro-hex. On ἀποκριθῆναι the Syriac cites in the margin: καὶ τοῦ διανοηθῆναι (ܘܠܡܣܬܟܠܘ).
[31] Cod. 87, Syro-hex., which notes on δύο in the margin: ἑβδομάδας.
[32] Euseb. ibid. p. 397. For Symmachus Gaisford edited τὰς ἑπτὰ with the article from Cod. Paris.
