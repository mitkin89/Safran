=== Blatt 0949 · S. 933 · –Cap. XII. 10.] DANIEL.
--- Fußnoten
[52] Cod. 87, Syro-hex. Hieron.: “The LXX alone, freeing themselves from every question about the name [Apedno], rendered: _And he shall set up his tabernacle then between the seas and the holy mount of the will, and the hour of his consummation shall come_.”
[53] The same: “Aquila: _And he shall plant the tabernacle of his praetorium in_ Ἀφαδανῷ [MS. Sangerm.: _of his praetorium_ APEΔNO] _between the seas, on the glorious and holy mount, and he shall come even to his end_.” Where two versions of Aquila, τοῦ πραιτωρίου αὐτοῦ, and Ἀπεδνὼ, seem to have coalesced.
[54] Hieron.: “Sym. rendered this place thus: καὶ ἐκτενεῖ—ἕως ἄκρου αὐτοῦ; which in Latin means: _And he shall stretch out the tents of his cavalry between the seas, upon the holy mount of strength; and he shall come even to the top of the mount_.” Schol. in Nobil.: τὴν σκηνὴν αὐτοῦ ἐφαδανῶ· τουτέστι, σκηνὴν τοῦ ἱπποστασίου, ὅπερ ἐστὶ τὸ ἱππικὸν στρατόπεδον.
CAP. XII.
[1] Thus Comp., Ald., Codd. III, XII, 26, 33, and others, Chrysost., Theodoret.
[2] Cod. 87, Syro-hex. Montef. edited: Ο΄. Θ. καὶ οἱ συνιέντες λάμψουσιν κ. τ. ἑ., from Jerome, who says ambiguously: “Which place Theod. and the common edition expressed thus: _And they that understand shall shine as the brightness of the firmament, and of the righteous very many_ (or _to very many_) _as the stars for ever and beyond_.”
[3] In the version τῶν Ο΄ De Magistris copied from Cod. 87 οἱ πολλοί; but Cod. 88, Syro-hex. reject the article.
[4] The same edited: τῷ ἐπάνω τοῦ ὕδατος τοῦ ποταμοῦ, and Bugatus attests that it stands thus in the Chisian Cod.; which, however, against the consensus of Cod. 88 and Syro-hex., which have only τῷ ἐπάνω, I could hardly believe.
[5] Thus Comp., Ald., Codd. III, XII, 23, 33, 34, and others.
[6] Montef. cites from Chrysost.: Ἄλλ. καὶ ἐν τῷ συντ. διασκορπισμὸν χειρὸς λαοῦ ἡγιασμένου, γνώσονται ἅγιον, καὶ συντ. ταῦτα πάντα (sic). Thus Codd. 22 (with ἅγιοι), 36, 48, 51, 231, and (with συντελεσθήσονται) Codd. 34, 87, Theodoret. (who does not know χειρὸς). The shorter reading, χειρὸς λαοῦ ἡγιασμένου, is in Comp., Ald., Codd. XII, 23, 33, 35, and others; and perhaps it is Aquila's. (For יַד־עַם Theodotion seems to have read יָדְעוּ, neglecting the words קֹדֶשׁ תִּכְלֶינָה, for which an interpolator supplied ἅγιον καὶ συντελεσθήσεται.)
[7] Thus Cod. 88, Syro-hex. καὶ τίνος κ. τ. ἑ. used to be printed.
