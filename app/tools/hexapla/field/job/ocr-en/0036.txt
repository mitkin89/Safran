[3] At the words καὶ οὐκ Syro-hex. gives in the margin: νεώτερος ὑμῶν ὤν (ܟܕ […] ܡܢܟܘܢ ܐܝܬܝ ܀). It belongs to the reading of Codd. Alex., 249: καί γε νεώτερος ὑμῶν ὢν (ὢν om. Cod. Alex.) οὐκ εἰμὶ ἀσυνετώτερος ὑμῶν. Moreover, for εἰμὶ ὑμῶν Syro-hex. has: ὑμῶν εἰμι ※ ἐγώ ↙.
[4] One Reg.
[5] “The version which is current as that of the LXX interpreters, καὶ οὐκ ἀσ. εἰμι ὑμῶν, is marked with an asterisk in the Latin Ed. of Jerome, because it is perhaps Theodotion's.”—_Montef._ Jerome's version is: “_And_ ※ _indeed_ : _I am not more foolish than you;_” as if he had read καί ※ γε ↙ οὐκ εἰμὶ κ.τ.έ. Moreover Syro-hex. continues to Theodotion: ܘܥܡܗ[?] ܒܙܒܢܐ[?]. ܐܢܐ ܐܝܬܝ ܡܢܟܘܢ ܀.
[6] “Colb. without the name of the interpreter.”—_Montef._ It seems to be a scholium.
[7] Syro-hex. in the margin ܐ. ܬ. ※ ܐܢܬܘܢ ܀.
[8] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܐܣܘ̈ܬܐ ܕܚܒܠܐ[?] ܐܢܬܘܢ ܟܠܟܘܢ ܀. The Greek is Middeldorpf's, who wrongly referred it to the former clause, וְאוּלָם אַתֶּם טֹפְלֵי־שָׁקֶר. Cf. Hex. on Psal. xcv. 5.
[9] “Orat., one Reg., Nicetas [who compiled Polychronius] and Drusius. Colb. ascribes only these words to Symmachus, καὶ ἰατροὶ etc., to which it prefixes an asterisk. Drusius suspects that κατασκευάζοντες ἄλογα is Symmachus' [rather Aquila's] or Theodotion's: but all the MSS. and printed editions protest.”—_Montef._ That these words, προστίθεσθε ψεύσματι, belong to Sym. I have no doubt, although that interpreter rendered the Hebrew otherwise at Psal. cxviii. 69: the rest is not free from suspicion. The words κατασκευάζοντες ἄλογα I would rather believe to be an explanation than another version of the Hebrew טֹפְלֵי־שָׁקֶר.
[10] Colb., one Reg.
[11] Thus Syro-hex. In the LXX today ἀκούσατε δέ is read, but δὲ is omitted by Cod. Alex. and others.

[13] Thus Codd. 137, 255. Cod. 256 in the text reads: ἢ ὑποτελεῖσθαι (sic). ἢ πρόσωπον αὐτοῦ λαμβάνετε; ἆρα πρόσωπον αὐτοῦ ὑποτελεῖσθε (sic). ὑμεῖς δὲ κ.τ.έ.
[14] Nobil. from Olymp., Colb., Orat., one Reg., and Codd. 137, 255. In Cod. 137 ὑπολαμβάνετε is wrongly read.
[15] Nobil. from Olymp., Orat., one Reg.
[16] [Σ.] ἆρα δύνασθε κ.τ.έ. “Colb. without the mark of Symmachus; for it is ascribed to Symmachus only by conjecture.”—_Montef._ Cod. 255 ascribes it to Aquila, which everyone sees to be utterly false. It is a scholium on the word ὑποστελεῖσθε, and in fact one of Olympiodorus in the Cat. of Nicet. p. 262.
