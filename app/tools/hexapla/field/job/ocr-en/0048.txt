=== Blatt 0048 · S. 36 · JOBUS. [Cap. XIX. 19.
--- Fußnoten
[20] Colb., one Reg.
[21] "Thus all the MSS. and printed editions."—_Montef._ [Montef. printed κρέει, but κρέᾳ is in Polychron.] Thus Syro-hex., except that it has ἐκολλήθη τὰ ὀστᾶ μου (ܕܒܩܘ ܓܪ̈ܡܝ).
[22] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܬ. ܠܡܫܟܐ ܕܝܠܝ ܘܠܒܣܪܝ ܕܒܩܘ ܓܪ̈ܡܝ.
[23] Syro-hex. continues to Aquila: ܘܐܬܦܨܝܬ ܒܡܫܟܐ ܕܫܢ̈ܝ܀
[24] Polychron. and "all." Syro-hex. continues to Symmachus: καὶ ἐξέτιλα (ܘܬܠܫܬ) τὸ δέρμα ὀδοῦσιν ἐμοῖς. ("The verb ܬܠܫ, _he plucked out, uprooted, tore apart_, common among the Chaldeans, is undeservedly omitted in the Syriac Lexicons. See these passages, in which it answers to the Gr. ἐκτίλλειν: Psal. li. 5. cxl. 6 in the margin, Eccles. iii. 2. Jerem. xxiv. 6. etc."—_Middeld._) Schleusner is therefore mistaken in the _Nov. Thesaur._ s. v. ἐκτίλλειν: "This version seems rather to be Aquila's."
[25] Syro-hex. continues to Theodotion: ܐܫܬܒܩܬ ܡܢ ܡܫܟܐ ܕܫܢ̈ܝ܀
[26] Colb., one Reg., according to Montef. Perhaps one should read ἤγγισέ μοι, so that it is referred to the Hebr. נָגְעָה בִּי.
[27] Jerome: “※ _with an iron pen_ : _and lead._” It is not Theodotion's, if credence is to be given to Syro-hex., which gives in the text: ※ ܬ. ܒܠܘܚܐ[?] ܕܦܪܙܠܐ ܘܐܒܪܐ ↙.
[28] Montef. printed from Colb. and Jerome: ※ εἰς μαρτύριον. Syro-hex. in the text: ἢ (in the margin Θ. εἰς μαρτύριον) ※ ἐν πέτρᾳ ↙ ἐγγλυφῆναι.
[29] Chrysost. in Nicet., Colb., one Reg.
[30] The reading σῶμα is in Codd. Alex., 254; ἀνατλῶν or ἀνατλοῦν in Comp. and six MSS. Syro-hex. in the text: τὸ δ. μου τὸ ἀνατλῶν (ܕܡܣܝܒܪ) ταῦτα; in the margin, however: τὸ σῶμά μου τὸ ἀντλῆσαν (ܕܕܠܐ[?]) ταῦτα.
[31] Polychron. and "all."
[32] Nicet., Orat., one Reg.
[33] Thus Syro-hex. in the text. These words, which today are found in the LXX, Orat. and one Reg. also ascribe to Symmachus.
[34] Nicet. and "all."
[35] Nicet., Colb., one Reg. Jerome: “_Fear ye also_ ※ _the sword._”
[36] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܐܝܟܢܐ ܕܬܕܥܘܢ ܕܕܝܢܐ܀ ܣ. ܬ. ܐܝܟܢܐ ܕܬܕܥܘܢ ܕܐܝܬܘܗܝ ܕܝܢܐ܀ Montef. printed from Colb.: Ἀ. Θ. ὅπως γνῶτε ὅτι ἔστι κρίσις; which Olymp. ascribes τοῖς λοιποῖς.
