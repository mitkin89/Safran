On the authority of the Syriac and Cod. Alex. we have written μετ᾿ αὐτόν for μετ᾿ αὐτοῦ, which is read in the present-day copies τῶν Ο΄. Jerome: “※ _For he has no pleasure in his house after him._”

[31] Ἀ. Θ. ἡμισεύθησαν. "Thus all except Drusius, who reads ἡμισευθήσεται, less correctly."—_Montef._ The reading ἡμισευθήσεται flowed from Olymp., who says in his commentary: διαιρεθήσεται γὰρ ὁ χρόνος αὐτοῦ τῆς ζωῆς, ἢ κατὰ Θ. καὶ Ἀ. ἡμισευθήσεται. Syro-hex. ascribes the rendering ἡμισεύθησαν to Aquila alone (ܐ. ܐܬܦܠܓܘ܀), more truly, as it seems, if the name of Theodotion is rightly prefixed to the two versicles. Jerome: “※ _although the number of his months be cut in half._”

[35] Colb.: Ἐκ Θ. οἱ β΄. Jerome has it under the asterisk; not so Syro-hex. For ἁπλοσύνης Schleusner cites from a MS. Catena: Σ. ἀφροσύνης, which is the reading of Comp., Cod. Alex., others, and Syro-hex. in the margin.
[36] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܒܓܪܡܢܘܬܐ ܕܦܫܝܛܘܬܐ ܕܝܠܗ܀, with the scholium: ܓܪܡܢܘܬܐ ܚܣܝܢܘܬܐ ܗܝ܀ Middeld. renders: Ἀ. ἐν ὀστῷ. But by the form ܓܪܡܢܘܬܐ, which the Lexicons do not recognise, the Syriac expressed a word coined for the occasion, ὀστέωσις, which Schleusner hesitantly claimed for Aquila in the _Nov. Thes._ s. v., and also in _Opusc. Crit._ p. 350. Cf. our Hexapla on Jesai. xl. 29. xli. 21.
