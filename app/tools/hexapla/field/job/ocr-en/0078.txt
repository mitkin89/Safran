[54] Syro-hex. continues (Aquila's): ܩܢܝܢܐ ܟܕ ܐܦ ܥܠ ܒܥܒܘܪܐ ܕܢܡܘܣܐ܀[?] Middeld. renders: κτίσεις σφόδρα ἐπὶ παρανόμοις. The reading κτίσεις for κτήσεις is an error of the transcript which our Syrian used. Next, Syr. ܟܕ ܐܦ is not σφόδρα, but καίπερ (e.g. Hebr. v. 8. 2 Pet. i. 12 in Pesch.). Cf. Hex. on Job. xxxvii. 1, 11. Eccles. iv. 14. Lastly, as to Middeld.'s assertion that the word παρανόμοις suits neither the character of Aquila nor the Hebrew עוֹלֶה, the Syr. ܒܥܒܘܪܐ ܕܢܡܘܣܐ[?] does not mean παράνομος, but παρανομία (Prov. v. 22. x. 27), which that translator exchanged with the Hebrew עַוְלָה Job. vi. 29. xi. 14.
