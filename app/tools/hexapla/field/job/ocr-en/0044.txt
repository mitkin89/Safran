CAP. XVII.

[3] "Colb. has: Ἐκ Θ. οἱ δ΄. [namely from τίς ἐστιν as far as κακίας, v. 5.] ... After that Ἐκ Θ. οἱ δ΄. there is added μ° π̄[?] ᾱ., by which is signified, I think, that Theodotion alone is found in the first versicle, μόνος πρὸς ᾱ. Then it indicates Symmachus for the second, third and fourth, in this way, ※ C. ※ ※ ※."—_Montef._ The same, in the Praelimin. p. 69, has only two asterisks after C. "It is more than certain that the second siglum, π̄[?], signifies not πρὸς, but Θεοδοτίων: and thus all the difficulties are resolved from which the illustrious restorer of the Hexapla could not extricate himself, as he candidly admits in the Preliminaries, and also in the note on Cap. xxi. 21 of this book. Cf. below Cap. xx. 23 and xxi. 21, 28."—_Drach._ In the Latin Ed. of Jerome asterisks are prefixed to four versicles, but the fourth versicle extends as far as the word ἐτάκησαν. Syro-hex. has: ※ Θ. τίς ἐστιν—συνδεθήτω↙. ὅτι καρδίαν—φρονήσεως, ※ διὰ τοῦτο—κακίας↙.
