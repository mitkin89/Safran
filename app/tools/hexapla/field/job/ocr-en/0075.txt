CAP. XXXV.
[1] The words ἢ ἐρεῖς, τί ποιήσω ἁμαρτών; which are absent from Ed. Rom., are supplied by Ald., Codd. III, 23, and others, with Olymp. and Syro-hex. Thus, except ὅτι for ἢ, Comp. and three MSS. Even so the Hebrew words מַה־יִּסְכָּן־לָךְ are left unrendered, except in Cod. 249 alone, which gives: ἢ ἐρεῖς, τί κατευθύνει σοι; ἢ τί π. ἁ. Syro-hex. supplies in the margin: ܡܢܐ ܡܥܕܪ ܠܟ܀.
