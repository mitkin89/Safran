The verb ܥܕܬܬܐ[?], which we do not know whether it is known from elsewhere, we have rendered into Greek by ἐκκλησιάζειν, according to the philological scholium of the codex: ܡܥܕܬܬܐ[?] ܐܢܬ ܡܢ ܥܕܬܐ ܀.
