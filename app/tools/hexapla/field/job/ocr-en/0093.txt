In translating the Syriac after Middeld., besides other necessary corrections, I have twice given ἐν τῷ προσεύξασθαι αὐτὸν for εὐξαμένου αὐτοῦ, on account of Montfaucon's Hexapla, who cites from Colb. and the Regius: Σ. (Σ. Θ. Colb.) ἐν τῷ προσεύξασθαι αὐτόν; although this perhaps would rather be in Syriac: ܟܕ ܨܠܝ.
