[5] Hieron.: “Whereas the LXX rendered: _Sacrifice men, for the calves have failed_, and we have translated: _sacrifice men, worshipping calves_, Sym. rendered: _sacrifice; let men worship calves_; so that the sense is: _sacrifice_, that is, offer sacrifice to idols, and the punctuation should follow up to this point; [then] the rational animal, _men, are to worship calves_, dumb creatures.” Syro-hex. on ܕܒܚܘ, θύσατε, cites: ܣ. ܕܒܚܘ ܀; and then: ܣ. ܠܥܓ̈ܠܐ ܢܣܓܕܘܢ ܀; which agree exactly with the Latin, provided ܕܒܚܘ is taken as an imperative, not as an indicative, which is Middeldorpf's error.

[9] The first reading is in Codd. III, XII, 22, 23 (with ἀπὸ), 26, 36, 40 (as 23), and others, and S. Cyril.; the second in Comp., Codd. 62, 86, and others, and Theodoret. Hieron.: “LXX: _and as vapour from locusts_, or _from tears_, because in most codices we find ἀκρίδων, in others δακρύων.”
[10] Cod. 86. In Sym. we are at a loss over the word κανθαρὶς, which according to Hesych. is χρυσοειδὴς ἰχθὺς, ἢ ζωΰφιον λυμαντικὸν σίτου καὶ ἀμπέλου καὶ κήπων. The reading of Theodotion too (which is written in the codex: καὶ ὡς σκιά· τῆς ἀκρίδος) seems hardly admissible against the agreement of our Syrian and Hieron.
[11] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܡܢ ܡܚܪܬܐ[?] ܀ ܣ. ܡܢ ܚܘܪܐ ܀ ܬ. ܡܢ ܬܛܠܝܠܐ ܀. In Aquila Middeld. would have us write ܡܚܪ̈ܘܬܐ[?], as in Psal. xli. 8. Jerem. xx. 2, 3. Mal. iii. 10 in Syro-hex., against the codex. In Theod. the Syriac ܬܛܠܝܠܐ alternates with the Greek στέγη, στέγος, φάτνωμα (Amos viii. 3), δόκωσις (Eccles. x. 18); καπνοδόχη, however, we have borrowed from Hieron., who says: “But we ask why the LXX rendered _locusts_ instead of _chimney_, which Theod. translated καπνοδόχην. Among the Hebrews _locust_ and _chimney_ are written with the same letters . . . if it is read ARBE, it means _locust_; if OROBBA, _chimney_; for which Aq. rendered καταράκτην, Sym. _hole._ But he properly calls a _cataract_ a hole made in a wall, through which the smoke goes out.”
[12] Syro-hex., with the scholion: “The things to which obeli are attached were not placed in the Hexapla.” Hieron.: “_Establishing the heaven—after these things_: since these are not found in the Hebrew and are rendered by none of the translators, and are not read in the ancient edition of the LXX either, they must be marked with an obelus.”
