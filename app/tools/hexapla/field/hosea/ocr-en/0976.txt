With our Syrian agrees Hieron.: “Where we put _heaps_, which in Hebrew are called GALLIM, and properly signify θῖνας, that is, mounds gathered from sand, which especially in the desert and on the shores are either increased or diminished as the wind blows, the LXX translated _tortoises_, for which Sym. rendered _heaps of stones_, Theod. _hills._ And indeed, if you consider θῖνας, they have a likeness to great tortoises, in a desert field, or on banks and shores, rising a little from the ground.” (On גַּלִּים, χελῶναι, Bochart in _Hieroz._ P.
