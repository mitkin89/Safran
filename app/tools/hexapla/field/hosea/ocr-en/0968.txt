[27] Cod. 86. Syro-hex. cites: Ἀ. τῆς συναγωγῆς (ܕܟܢܘܫܬܐ). Θ. (sic) τῆς μαρτυρίας (ܕܣܗܕܘܬܐ).
[28] Cod. 86.
[29] Hieron.: "Whereas we have said, _they shall be laid waste_, and in the Hebrew is written SOD LAEM, that is, _devastation to them_, Sym. rendered _destruction_, Theod. _misery_ [cf. Hex. on Psal. xxxi. 4]. Moreover, in the common edition we read it in two ways: for some codices have δῆλοί εἰσιν, that is, _they are manifest;_ others δείλαιοί εἰσιν, that is, _they are fearful_, or _wretched_." The second reading is not extant today; only the Alexandrine Codex reads δηλαι· οιεισιν (sic).
[30] Victor Antioch. in Nobil. on Jerem. ii. 23: Ὠσηὲ δέ φησιν ὁ προφήτης· ὠλόλυζον ἐπὶ τῆς κοίτης αὐτῶν· ὅπερ Ἀκ. καὶ Σύμ., ἀσελγῶς ἐλάλησαν, ἐξέδωκαν. Cod. 86 cites: Σ. ἀλλὰ ἀσελγῶς ἐλάλησαν. With Victor agrees our Syrian: ܐ. ܣ. ܐܠܐ ܒܛܠܡܐܝܬ[?] (not, as Norberg., ܕܒܛܠܡܐܝܬ[?]) ܡܠܠܘ܀ ܬ. ܐܝܟ ܫܒܥܝܢ܀
[31] Cod. 86. Parsons copied from the margin of Cod. 42: Σ. ἐχρεμέτισαν ἐν τοῖς κλίτεσιν αὐτῶν. For C΄ perhaps Є΄ should be written.
[32] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܡܬܢܟܦܝܢ[?] ܗܘܘ܀ ܣ. ܡܬܚܪܓܪܝܢ[?]܀ ܬ. ܐܝܟ ܫܒܥܝܢ܀ The former translator, if our version is correct, seems to have had in view the meaning of the Hebrew גּוּר, _to fear_; the other (whom Hieron. imitated: _over wheat and wine they ruminated_) drew the word from גָּרַר, _he ruminated_.
