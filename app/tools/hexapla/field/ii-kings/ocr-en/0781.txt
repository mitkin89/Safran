[17] Syro-hex. in the text: ܫܢܝܐ, a word which is interchanged with the Greek μωρὸς, ἄφρων, and the like, and only in this place with ἐπίληπτος; but in the margin: ܐ. ܡܫܢܝܐ[?], i.e. ἐπίληπτος, comparing Hex. on Jerem. xxix. 26. xlix. 3. Hos. ix. 7. Since, however, in the text τῶν Ο΄ all the books firmly hold ὁ ἐπίληπτος, it has seemed better, with Middeldorpf, to ascribe to Aquila ὁ παράπληκτος (מְשֻׁגָּע Deut. xxviii. 34 in the LXX), or ὁ παραπληκτευόμενος (מִשְׁתַּגֵּעַ 1 Reg. xxi. 14 in Aquila's reading). Cf. on v. 20.
[18] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܣ. ܘܠܡܡܠܠܐ[?] ܕܝܠܗ. Cf. Hex. on Psal. liv. 3. ciii. 34. Hieron.: "_and what he said._" (The word ἀδολεσχία is rendered variously by our Syrian: ܪܢܝܐ[?], ܡܡܠܠܐ[?], once (Psal. liv. 2) ܗܘܓܝܐ; here, however, ܦܘܚܠܐ[?] (ܦܘܚܠܐ[?]), i.e., as Bar Bahlul explains it, ܦܚܠܘܬܐ[?], "_babbling, idle talkativeness._" Cf. Hex. on Jerem. xx. 7. The errors of Masius and Middeldorpf concerning this word were corrected by Bernstein in _Zeitschrift der Deutschen-morgenl. Gesellschaft_, T. III, p. 415.)
