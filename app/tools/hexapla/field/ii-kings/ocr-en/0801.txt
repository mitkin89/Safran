Some books [Comp., the rest] have εἰς μέρος; one [Cod. 245, with τοῦ δρ.] εἰς τέλος δρυμοῦ.” The reading μέλον (not μέλος, as Montef. printed), rashly admitted into the text by Grabe, is exhibited by not even one of our books, and I should believe it to be plainly fictitious.
