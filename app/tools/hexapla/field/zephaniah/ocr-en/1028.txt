=== Blatt 1028 · S. [1012] · [ZEPHANIAS]
--- Fußnoten
CAP. I.
[1] Hieron.: “What we have put in the LXX, _and the ungodly shall be weakened,_ was added from Theodotion's translation; for which Sym. rendered, _and the stumbling-blocks with the ungodly,_ so that _shall be gathered,_ or _shall fail,_ is to be understood; but the Fifth ed., _and weakness shall fail with the ungodly._” Cod. 86 cites: Σ. καὶ τὰ σκ. σὺν ἀσεβέσι, and thus in the text (with σὺν τοῖς ἀσ.) for καὶ ἀσθ. οἱ ἀσεβεῖς Codd. 36, 238 (in the margin), 240. But these words, καὶ ἀσθ. οἱ ἀσεβεῖς, are read without asterisk in Syro-hex. and all the others, except Codd. 130, 311.
[2] Syro-hex. in the margin ※ܥܡ ܟܘ̈ܡܪܐ܀. Thus in the text without asterisk Comp., Ald. (with ἱερῶν), Codd. 22, 36, 42 (as Ald.), and others, Hieron.
[3] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܗܝܟܠܢܐ܀ ܣ. ܡܛ̈ܢܦܐ܀ ܬ. ܟܘ̈ܡܪܐ܀. On Theod. cf. Hex. on 4 Reg. xxiii. 5. “I can scarcely believe that Aquila put ܗܝܟܠܐ, _temple._”—_Middeld._ In the codex there is the adjective ܗܝܟܠܢܐ, which is equally obscure, and would have reduced us to a standstill, had we not lighted upon a passage of Jerome from which light has shone upon us in the darkness. For he, having rendered the Hebrew, _and the names of the temple-keepers with the priests,_ explains thus: “Whence he expressly called the priests of the idols not _priests,_ but τεμενίτας, that is, _temple-keepers,_ or _fanatics,_ which in Hebrew is called ACCHUMARIM.” Here you have a word of all the rarest, but good Greek, τεμενίτης, from τέμενος (as from _fanum, fanaticus,_ properly _one who used to rave and prophesy about the shrines_), and which corresponds excellently with the Hebrew כֹּמֶר; which, however, would never, we think, have occurred to Jerome, had he not had it before his eyes, found in one or other of the three translators. That this was Aquila is shown both by the novelty of the word itself and by the express testimony of our Syriac; which, since it usually renders τέμενος by ܗܝܟܠܐ (see Hos. viii. 14. Ezech. vi. 4), could not, we know not how, have expressed τεμενίτης more significantly than by the form peculiar to derivatives, ܗܝܟܠܢܐ. For the rest, anyone can see that ܗܝ̈ܟܠܢܐ should be written with _Ribui_ in accordance with the Hebrew verity.
