[39] Syro-hex. Thus in the text Codd. 22, 36, and others. Hieron. (who writes ÷ _their priests_:, against our Syrian): "The idol of the Ammonites, which is called _Melchom_, that is, _their king_, shall be carried off to the Assyrians; and _his princes_, that is, _his priests_, shall likewise be led away. _Priests_ is not found in the Hebrew, but _princes_. The LXX therefore added _priests_, so that if you wish to know who those princes are, you may hear: the priests." But that phrase, οἱ ἱερεῖς αὐτῶν, which the common Syriac also recognizes, seems to have been taken from the parallel passages Jerem. xlviii. 7. xlix. 3.
