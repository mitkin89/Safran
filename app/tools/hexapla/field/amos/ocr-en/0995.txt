CAP. IX.
[1] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܒܢܝܢܐ ◆. Cod. 86, which in the text, with Codd. III, XII, 49, and others, has θυσιαστήριον, cites in the margin: Σ. (sic) οἰκοδόμημα ἱλαστήριον.
[2] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܬ. ܥܠ ܩܒܘܪܝܢ ◆. "I have tried in vain to explain the Syriac ܩܒܘܪܝܢ."—_Middeld._ It is a Greek word expressed in Syriac letters, κιβώριον, a name given to 1. _the seed-cup of the Egyptian bean._ 2. _a drinking cup made in its likeness_ (Hor. Od. II, vii, 21). 3. in the ecclesiastical architecture of the Greeks, _a canopy_, or _tabernacle_, _extended over the principal altar_ (in modern Italian, _il baldacchino_), so called from its likeness to an inverted cup. (Cf. Bernstein in _Zeitschrift_ etc. T. IV, p. 210, who pointed out the passage of Bar-Hebraeus in the _Chronicon_ p. 142, l. 8.)
[3] Syro-hex., which writes: ※ Ἀ. καὶ ἐάν ↙. Thus without the asterisk Codd. 22, 36, and others.
[4] Thus Comp., and Cod. 86 in the margin without a name.

Thes._ s. v. cites only Jos. xv. 3] expressed קַרְקַע by the word ἔδαφος. But see Prov. xiv. 12. xvi. 25 in Syro-hex. Immediately, on the words of the LXX, ἐντελοῦμαι τῷ δράκοντι (אֶת־הַנָּחָשׁ), S.
