=== Blatt 0715 · S. 603 · -Cap. VI. 11–14.] III REGUM.
--- Fußnoten
[10] (Forts.) machus's ἐκθέτας, in English _balconies_, cf. Hex. on Ezech. xlii. 3.
[11] So Comp., Codd. 19, 82, 93, 108.
[12] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܘܥܒܕ ܡܪܙܒܐ[?]. The Syriac word is interchanged with ἐπίθεμα at Exod. xxv. 16; with ἐπίβλημα in Hex. on Jos. ix. 5. In architecture, however, ἐπίβλημα might perhaps denote a structure which rests against the principal building and is, as it were, _laid beneath_ its walls, which may be the notion of the Hebrew word יָצוּעַ.

[19] So Comp., Codd. 121 (with λ. ὀλ. ἀκρ. ἀργοῖς (in the margin ἀπηρτισμένοις)), 123 (with λ. ὀλ. ἀκρ. ἀργοῖς), and perhaps Syro-hex. (with ܒܟܐܦܐ ܕܓܙܝܪܐ ܡܫܡܠܝ̈ܬܐ, i.e. λίθοις ἀκροτόμου ὁλοκλήροις). In Codd. 44, 74, and others (among them 243) there is a reading mixed from several, λίθοις ὀλ. ἀκρ. ἀπηρτισμένοις ἀργοῖς; where the word ἀπηρτισμένοις savours of Aquila as its author, comparing Hex. on Deut. xxvii. 6.
