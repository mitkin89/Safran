[27] (Forts.) Montef., noting: "Ἀδικίας, and another reading λῃστῶν, are given by MS. Jes. They are perhaps scholia." Cod. Alex. alone has λῃστῶν for ὑαίνης; but in the clause ἢ σπήλαιον κύκλῳ αὐτῆς, Codd. XII (in the margin), 33, 36, and others insert λῃστῶν after σπήλαιον. Further, after κύκλῳ αὐτῆς, for the Hebrew עָלֶיהָ, which is wanting in the LXX, Comp., Codd. XII, 22, 23, 26, others, and Syro-hex. have ἐπ᾽ αὐτὴν; while Cod. XII gives ἀδικίας in the margin (as if for עַוְלָה?).
