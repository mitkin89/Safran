[5] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܗܘܝܬܝ ܕܙܢܝܬܐ[?] ܀ ܣ. ܐܙܕܢܝܬܝ[?] ܀ (Thus Norberg. In the codex the reading of Symmachus is less probably referred to ἐκάθισας.) The Syriac of Aquila literally means ἐγενήθης παλλάκη. But it is far more probable that our Syrian paraphrased by this expression the single word συνεκοιτάσθης, with which Aq. exchanged the Hebr. יִשְׁגָּלֶ[?] Deut. xxviii. 30, as also σύγκοιτος with שֵׁגַל Psal. xliv. 10.
