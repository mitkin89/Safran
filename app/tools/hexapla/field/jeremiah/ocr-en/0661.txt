The same notes in the margin in a middle hand: “These which have asterisks have been added from the edition (ܡܦܩܢܘܬܐ) of Theodotion in place of the preceding ones which have lemnisci (ܠܡܢܝ̈ܣܩܘܣ); the rest made them equivalent in force (ܕܫܘܝܢ ܒܚܝܠܐ, ἰσοδυναμοῦντα).” Jerome on vv. 18–22: “These are not found in the LXX, but have been translated from the Hebrew truth; in place of which they put something else, which was not written, saying: _To me, for thus saith the Lord: And the rest of the vessels which the king of Babylon did not take, when he carried away Jechoniah from Jerusalem, shall come into Babylon, saith the Lord;_ setting down the sense rather than the words: perhaps thinking it unreasonable that God should speak _to the pillars, and to the sea, and to the bases, and to the rest of the vessels which had remained in Jerusalem;_ as if we did not read that the Lord both rebuked the worm at dawn and spoke to the sea: _Be still, and be silent._”
