=== Blatt 0670 · S. 654 · JEREMIAS. [Cap. XXX (xxxvii). 7–
--- Fußnoten
[18] Syro-hex. Cod. 88 wrongly marks: ※ ἐγενήθη ↙. οὐαὶ ὅτι. (In the printed text, for ὅτι ἐγενήθη read ἐγενήθη. ὅτι, from Ald., Codd. II, III, XII, 22, 26, 33, others.)
[19] Codd. 86, 88, of which the former has Θ. instead of Σ. After στενός ἐστι Codd. 22, 36, others add καὶ θλίψεως.
[20] Cod. 86. Thus in the text Cod. 88, and under the asterisk Syro-hex.
[21] Cod. Jes. Thus in the text Comp., Codd. 22, 36, others, and under the asterisk Cod. 88, Syro-hex.
[22] Cod. 86. Thus in the text Codd. 22, 36, others.
[23] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܠܐ ܢܫܬܥܒܕܘܢܗ ܬܘܒ ܢܘܟܪ̈ܝܐ. ܐܠܐ ܢܦܠܚܘܢ ܠܡܪܝܐ ܠܐܠܗܐ[?] ܕܝܠܗܘܢ ܘܠܕܘܝܕ ܡܠܟܗܘܢ ◆. Cf. on Cap. xxvii. 7.
[24] Thus Syro-hex., and without the asterisk Codd. 62, 233.
[25] Cod. Jes., and under the asterisk Codd. 86, 88, Syro-hex. Codd. 22, 36, others have φησὶ instead of λέγει. But all without exception write καὶ εὐπαθήσει, not, as Montef. carelessly copied, ἐν εὐπαθήσει. For τῆς αἰχμ. Grabe printed γῆς αἰχμ., against all the authorities. Hieron.: “This περικοπὴ [vv. 10, 11] is not found in the LXX, and in most copies of the common edition it has been added from Theodotion under asterisks.”
[26] Cod. Jes., where these words, πλὴν σὲ—ἔκλειψιν, seem to have dropped out by accident. The same, for ἔκλειψιν in the former place, writes εἴσλειψιν, or εἰς λεῖψιν. For the rest, instead of these words, πλὴν σὲ—ἔκλειψιν, which we have taken from Codd. 22, 36, others, other words, σὲ δὲ οὐ ποιήσω συντέλειαν, are found in Codd. 86, 88, and Syro-hex.
[27] Hieron.: “But what he added, _that you may not seem to yourself innocent;_ or according to Sym., _and cleansing I will not cleanse you;_ or according to Aq., _when I have instructed you by judgment, I will by no means make you innocent_, signifies this: that the whole world needs the mercy of God, and no one, however holy he may be, goes securely to the judge.” The Greek of Aquila seems to differ hardly a whit from Theodotion (cf. on Cap. xlvi. 28); Sym. perhaps rendered: καὶ καθαρίζων οὐ καθαριῶ σε; which is the reading of the Complutensians. Cf. Hex. on Job. x. 14.
