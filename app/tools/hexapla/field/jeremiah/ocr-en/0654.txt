=== Blatt 0654 · S. 638 · JEREMIAS. [Cap. XXV. 17 (xxxii. 3)–
--- Fußnoten
[37] (Forts.) ܘܢܬܬܙܝܥܘܢ[?] ܐܟܣܢܐ[?] ܘܢܚܡܚܡܘܢ[?] ◆ ܣ. ܘܢܬܚܡܚܡܘܢ[?] ܘܢܫܬܓܫܘܢ[?] ◆. The Syriac ܢܬܚܡܚܡܘܢ[?] Norberg renders "_they shall live in delights_", following Castell, for whom ܚܡܚܡ[?] is "_he tore in pieces_", and ܐܬܚܡܚܡ[?], "_was torn, was delighted._" But at Jerem. iv. 19, where Syro-hex. has ܡܬܚܡܚܡ[?], the Greek has σπαράσσεται, which the LXX interchanged with the Hebrew הִתְגָּעַשׁ at 2 Reg. xxii. 8, and Aq. at Jerem. xlvi. 7. Moreover, in the present passage on the word ἐκμανήσονται Parsons cites from Codd. 86, 88: Ἀ. Σ. σπαραχθήσονται; a reading which anyone can see belongs to הִתְגֹּעֲשׁוּ, not to הִתְהֹלְלוּ.

[44] Thus from Cod. Jes. Montef., who however, by conjecture, substituted γῆς Οὖς for the codex's reading νήσου. After Ἀραβίαν a double version, καὶ π. τοὺς β. τῆς γῆς αὐτοῦ Ουζ (sic), has crept into Codd. 36, 48, and others, against Syro-hex. “Montef. gives this reading of Theodotion [τῆς νήσου] from MS. Jes.; but I believe it is a corrupted writing. Who does not see that νήσου could have arisen from γης ους?”—_Bahrdt._ The scribes' error, if so, is older than Jerome, who notes: “It should be known that this little verse is not found in the LXX, and that Theodotion rendered _island_ for _Us_.” The codices fluctuate between τῆς νήσου, τῆς μήσου, νήσων (thus Codd. 36, 48, others, and Syro-hex.) and μήσων. Cf. on v. 22.
