[23] Hieron., translating the Hebrew: “_And you shall call upon me, and you shall go_ (or according to Sym. _you shall find_).” Drusius, Montef., and others held that the reading of Symmachus belongs to the following verse, where the Hebrew has וּמְצָאתֶם, not noticing that in the hexaplaric books, except Cod. 88, the passage runs thus: καὶ ἐλπίδα, καὶ ἐπικαλέσασθέ με, καὶ εὑρήσετε, καὶ πορεύεσθε.
