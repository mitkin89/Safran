[61] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܕܡܦܠܓ[?] ܕܚܕܝܪ ܡܢ ܩܪܒܐ ܀ A difficult reading indeed, which in Greek literally means: Ἀ. (ἐν λαῷ μου) διῃρημένῳ, κυκλουμένῳ ὑπὸ πολέμου (or, in one word, πολιορκουμένῳ, which for our translator is ܕܚܕܝܪ ܩܪܒܐ, Jesai. xxvii. 3). Whether the latter clause is an explanation of the former, or belongs to another translator whose name has perished, we leave undecided; as for the former, ܕܡܦܠܓ[?], accidentally omitted by Norberg, if we have rendered it correctly into Greek, it must be considered whether it is a miswriting of the word διῃρμένῳ, comparing Hex. on Deut. iii. 5, where עָרִים בְּצֻרֹת for the same translator are πόλεις διῃρμέναι, _cities raised on high._ Jerome, however, who assigned _fortified_ to Aquila, we should believe either to have expressed the _sense_ of the Greek word, or to have had regard to the other edition of this translator.
