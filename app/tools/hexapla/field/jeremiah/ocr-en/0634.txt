[26] Thus Cod. 88, Syro-hex.
[27] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܪܕܝܐ[?] ܕܡܝܐ ܚܝ̈ܐ ܀. (The Syriac ܪܕܝܐ[?], which for Castell is "_a rivulet, a little spring_", is put in the Hex. on Ezech. vi. 3 for the Hebrew אָפִיק, "_torrent_". In other places (Jerem. li. 37. Baruch v. 7) it is interchanged with the Greek θῖνες.)
[28] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܬܫܒܘܚܬܐ ܕܝܠܝ ܀.
[29] Cod. Jes. Thus under the asterisk Cod. 88, Syro-hex., and without the asterisk Codd. 22, 36, others.
[30] Codd. 86, 88. For ἠπείχθην Cod. 86 has εἰπείχθην written, Cod. 88 ἠπείλθην. Hesych.: Ἐπειχθῶμεν· σπουδάσωμεν; a sense which agrees well with the Hebrew אוּץ, "_he hastened_". Our Syrian, however, seems to have read something else, giving: ܣ. ܠܐ ܡܬܟܠܝܬ ܗܘܝܬ ܡܢ (_I was held back_, ἐπεσχόμην?) ܒܝܫܘܬܐ ܕܒܬܪܟ ܀.

[41] Syro-hex. ܐ. ܫܦܘܠܐ ܀ ܣ. ܕܥܘܡܩܐ ܀. The Syriac ܫܦܘܠܐ, according to Castell, is "_the foot of a mountain_", Gr. ὑπώρεια (in Suidas ἡ ὑπὸ τὸ ὄρος πεδιάς); a word which is to be claimed for Aquila, besides this passage, at Jos. xv. 33. See Scharfenberg's _Animadversiones_ on the passage. On Sym. cf. Hex. on Jerem. xxxii. 44. Obad. 19.
[42] Thus Codd. II, III, XII, 23, 86 (with ※ before καὶ), others. In Codd. 48, 88, others, and Syro-hex. καὶ θυσίας is wanting.
