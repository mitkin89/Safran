[15] “Euseb. De locis Hebraicis: Γημαρώθ. Ἀ. ἐν φραγμοῖς. Σ. γαρημώθ. Where γημαρὼθ seems to have been put for γηδαρώθ. The reading of Symmachus is corrupted; for Hieron. [Opp. T. II, p. 227] has thus: ‘Gebarth, _for which Aq. translates_, in the hedges; _but Sym._, in the colonies.’”—_Montef._ I formerly suspected that for _in coloniis_ (in the colonies) we should write _in coronis_ (on the copings), namely of the walls, which reading can without any difficulty be reconciled with the anonymous one, διὰ τῶν τριγχῶν. But now I hold it for certain that the words of Jerome belong not to our passage, but to Cap. xli. 17.
