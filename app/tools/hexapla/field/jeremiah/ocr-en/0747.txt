=== Blatt 0747 · S. 731 · –CAP. LI (xxviii). 34.] JEREMIAS.
--- Fußnoten
[61] Syro-hex. ܣ. ܓܒܝܐ ◆. The same in the margin without a name ܛܦܣܪ ◆. Vulg. “_Taphsar._”
[62] Syro-hex. cites: Ἀ. Σ. βροῦχον (ܙܚܠܐ). The same on ܠܡܕܒܪ̈ܢܐ, τοὺς ἡγουμένους (v. 28) adds: ܐ. ܠܐܛܘܣ. ܣ. ܙܝܥܐ ◆. "Ἀ. ܠܐܛܘܣ. A word unknown to me, which, if you please, you may compare with לָטִים, λῶτος, in Castell. S. _commotion._"—_Norberg._ "The Syriac word seems to me to have been badly copied. For I have tried everything by which some light might be shed on that little word, but without success. Perhaps it is to be derived from ܛܘܣ, _to fly_."—_Spohn._ As regards Symmachus, Spohn rightly saw that the Syr. ܙܝܥܐ is not to be translated _commotion_, but _terrifying_, in Greek, as it seemed to him, ὀρθοτριχοῦντα; and certainly Sym. employs ὀρθοτριχεῖν for סָמַר Psal. cxviii. 120. But in the passages Jerem. v. 30. xviii. 13 our Syrian rendered φρικτὰ by ܙܝ̈ܥܐ, nor is it to be translated otherwise in this place, as Codd. 86, 88 testify, where we have: Ἀ. Σ. ὡς βροῦχον φρικτόν. For Aquila, the reading I formerly attempted in the _Otium Norvic._ p. 55 for ܠܐܛܘܣ, namely ܕܠܐ ܛܐܣ, _not flying_, ἄπτερον, ἄπτιλον, as an epithet of the cankerworm, now does not please me. "Another correction is proposed by Drach in his recent edition of the Hexapla, namely that the Syrian badly copied the Greek word ἀριστεῖς (I should prefer, from the Syriac form, ἀρίστους), so that for ܠܐܛܘܣ we should write ܠܐܪ̈ܣܛܘܣ; and indeed in the MS. the reading of Aquila is referred to ܠܡܕܒܪ̈ܢܐ, אֶת־פַּחוֹתֶיהָ. But that the ܙܝܥܐ of Symmachus is referred to the same place in the MS. has evidently happened by mistake, since it should be placed immediately after ܐ. ܣ. ܙܚܠܐ ◆, but separately, as belonging to Symmachus alone."—_Ceriani._
