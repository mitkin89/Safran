Other difficulties, from which Middeldorpf confesses he cannot extricate himself, are in reality none. For the siglum ܓ (in the cod. ܓ̈), both in the text and in the margin, does not signify _the Three translators_, but, as our friend Ceriani has informed us, is, according to the usage of the codex, a mere index mark, like ܗܘ̈[?] (sic) at the beginning of the next verse, where the marginal note is exactly the same.
