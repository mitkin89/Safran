Alex. inserts the same after ῥῆσιν ἐμοῦ στόματος, and Codd. 161, 248 under the asterisk. Jerome, in Epist. LII to Nepotian: “_Get wisdom, get understanding; forget not, and turn not aside from the words of my mouth._”
