[19] Nobil. gives: Ἀ. μὴ ἀποδοκίμασον. Σ. μὴ ἀπωθῇς; both ungrammatical. Moreover, for the Hebr. מָאַס Aquila's usual word is ἀπορρίπτειν, Symmachus's ἀποδοκιμάζειν, Theodotion's ἀπωθεῖσθαι. Since therefore the Syrian offers: Σ. μὴ ἀποδοκιμάσῃς (ܠܐ ܬܣܠܐ), we have without hesitation assigned this to Symmachus. As to Theodotion the matter is not so certain; for Parsons. too gives: Σ. μὴ ἀποθήσῃς Cod. 161. Σ. μὴ αποθης (sic) Cod. 248; neither of which is Greek. Finally we have restored the form ἀπωθήσῃς, rarer indeed, but not altogether to be rejected, and have assigned it to the translator Theodotion.
