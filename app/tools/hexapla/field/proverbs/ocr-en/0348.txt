[29] Jerome, in his Commentary on Ephesians (Opp. T. VII, p. 641): “Hence I think that in Proverbs too it is written thus: γυνὴ εὐχάριστος ἐγείρει ἀνδρὶ δόξαν, _a thankful woman raiseth up glory for her husband_, in place of _gracious_. We should seem to do violence to Scripture, and boldly to take a _thanksgiving_ woman for a _gracious_ one, did not the other editions also agree with our opinion. For Aquila and Theod. and Sym. put it thus, γυνὴ χάριτος, that is, _a gracious woman_, and not εὐχάριστος, which pertains to the giving of thanks.”
