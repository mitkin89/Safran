# Berakhot 7–9 review

The private screenshot comparison now covers all 57 Berakhot units. Published
aggregate counts are in `comparison-summary.json`; they describe feature
compatibility, not an accuracy score or fully verified token analyses.

Independent corrections preserve לפי as a preposition, temporal אחר כך as an
adverbial expression, construct בית in school names, demonstrative אותו before
הכוס, and היה בא as a past periphrasis with a participle. The cardinal ששה must
not inherit a spurious prefix/יהוה analysis. משקלקלו retains the reduplicated
Pilpel stem (Segal §195). All occurrence corrections remain bound to exact
pointed source words and token positions.

Open differences include רבוא gender, the stem classification of להחלק,
pointed נזכר, and זקנה with its feminine subject אמך. External tags are evidence
to assess against the pointed text and grammar, rather than automatic overrides.
Unreadable suffix features remain unconfirmed. Duplicate screenshots were not
counted as additional evidence.

The initial independent Ma’agarim check is documented separately in
`maagarim-review.md`.
