# Ma’agarim: initial Berakhot cross-check

Checked 2026-11-26 against the Academy of the Hebrew Language's Historical
Dictionary Project, Ma’agarim. This is a separate, limited check, not a completed
second comparison of all Berakhot tokens. No Ma’agarim corpus was imported.

The live results explicitly identify the Mishnah witness as Budapest, Magyar
Tudomanyos Akademia, MS. Kaufmann, A 50. Its transcription preserves editorial
deletion/insertion signs; these must be aligned separately from Be’eri's pointed
edition before comparing analyses.
