# Greek pseudepigrapha: texts and automatic analyses

These data are not covered by Safran's MIT code license. Source components retain their separate licenses; the text and automatic analysis layer are supplied as a collection. No NLP model weights are distributed.

## Reading texts

**Online Critical Pseudepigrapha (OCP)**, Ian W. Scott and Ken M. Penner, general editors: https://pseudepigrapha.org/ . Repository: https://github.com/OnlineCriticalPseudepigrapha/Online-Critical-Pseudepigrapha . OCP's text repository is licensed **CC BY 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ ; see its `LICENSE.CC-BY-4.0`. This import uses only the Greek language layer of T12P, PsPhoc, 3Bar and 1En, not OCP's modern translations. Each file retains its original edition bibliography and witness names; exact URLs and checksums are in `tools/pseudepigrapha/sources.json`.

- **Twelve Patriarchs:** Greek Wikisource contributors, *Διαθήκαι των ΙΒ' Πατριαρχών*, https://el.wikisource.org/wiki/Διαθήκαι_των_ΙΒ%27_Πατριαρχών , **CC BY-SA 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . OCP describes this as apparently based on R. H. Charles, *The Greek Versions of the Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs* (Oxford 1908, public domain), and explicitly provisional, not yet collated against Charles. Safran keeps this provenance and qualification; it does not claim a newly checked Charles edition.
- **Pseudo-Phocylides:** Theodor Bergk, *Poetae Lyrici Graeci*, 4th ed., vol. 2 (Leipzig 1882), pp. 361–373, as transcribed in OCP. The ancient poem and historical edition are public domain.
- **3 Baruch:** OCP's Greek edition, citing J.-C. Picard, *Apocalypsis Baruchi Graece* (1967), pp. 81–96. Permission here is OCP's open electronic text license; Safran does not reproduce scans or commentary from Picard's book.
- **Greek 1 Enoch:** OCP's distinct 7QEnoch, POxy2069, CB185, V1809, Gizeh, Gizeh2, Syncellus and Jude witnesses. Bibliographies are retained separately. These are source witness labels, not new assertions of authorship or fragment identification. Safran does not combine them into a reconstructed complete Greek book.

Safran changes: XML extraction, NFC normalization, HTML escaping, explicit preservation of native numbering, compact storage and alignment to the matching reader positions. In 1 Enoch, 106:17b remains separately identified within reader row 106:17; later verses are not shifted. Alternative source readings remain separately accessible. Source headings are preserved in metadata. No spelling corrections, translations or reconstructions were made.

## Stanza analysis layer

`greek-pseudepigrapha-auto.js` and the retained raw `tools/pseudepigrapha/stanza-annotations.jsonl.gz` contain automatically generated analyses. **Stanza / Stanford NLP Group**, version 1.11.0, Greek Perseus `perseus_nocharlm` POS model and PROIEL `proiel_nocharlm` lemma model, resource release 1.11.0. Software: **Apache 2.0**, https://github.com/stanfordnlp/stanza . Model information: https://huggingface.co/stanfordnlp/stanza-grc and https://stanfordnlp.github.io/stanza/performance.html . Stanford's model notice provides **Open Data Commons Attribution License 1.0**, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/ ; the Hugging Face model card also records Apache 2.0. These notices describe upstream models, not a claim that the models were trained on unrestricted data.

Training-data credit and notice: **Universal Dependencies Ancient Greek–Perseus / Ancient Greek Dependency Treebank / Perseus Digital Library**, https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Ancient_Greek-Perseus , **CC BY-NC-SA 2.5**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.5/ . Additional lemma-training credit: **PROIEL / University of Oslo / Dag Haug and the original annotators**, via Universal Dependencies Ancient Greek–PROIEL, https://universaldependencies.org/treebanks/grc_proiel/index.html , **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/ . Safran distributes its generated Stanza analysis layer separately under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ . This does not relicense the reading texts. Model checksums, Stanza/Torch versions, seed and CPU settings are retained in `annotation-source.json`; original UPOS, XPOS and UFeats remain in the raw analysis archive. The app needs neither Stanza nor an online inference service.

These analyses have not been editorially reviewed word by word. Fragmentary, restored or illegible reader words are excluded conservatively; unsupported analyses stay empty. Annotation coverage is not linguistic accuracy.

## OGA analysis entries

Entries marked `oga` in the automatic asset retain **CC BY-SA 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Credit: **Giuseppe G. A. Celano, Opera Graeca Adnotata 0.2.0**, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14206061 , via the Open Greek `oga-v1` export, https://huggingface.co/datasets/ciscoriordan/open-greek-corpus-annotation-exports . Only complete identical Gizeh verse streams are eligible, preserving the native source lemma/tag. No OGA analysis is transferred between different witnesses.
