# Changelog

### New in 2.4.0
- **Zunz Bible: about 170 transcription errors corrected** (found while aligning the Pentateuch, Psalms and Prophets word by word, checked against the Hebrew): misread letters such as „Rachzug“ → „Nachzug“, „Terasim“ → „Terafim“, „Ramen“ → „Namen“, „Bölkerreiche“ → „Völkerreiche“, „ganjen“ → „ganzen“; glued or split words („mitmeinen“, „fortzie hen“); duplicated or garbled endings removed (Ez 24:27, 35:15, Hos 11:11, Hab 2:20, 1 Sam 23:28, 2 Sam 11:27); garbled passages restored (Mi 5:13 „heraus deiner Mitte“, Ez 39:11); missing words supplied after the Hebrew (Am 7:17 „wird aus seinem Lande hinweggeführt werden“, Ex 24:1 „Komme herauf zum Ewigen, du und Aharon“); wrong words corrected after the Hebrew (Lev 20:14 „Mutter“, Dtn 5:20 „Finsterniß“, 2 Kgs 18:32 „Honigs“).
- **Interlinear: Zunz, Prophets word by word (Joshua to Malachi):** all 9,296 verses of the Former and Latter Prophets (21 books) are now aligned word by word with the Westminster text (previously phrase by phrase), including Ketiv/Qere pairs; the translation statistics in the word card now cover the Prophets too.
